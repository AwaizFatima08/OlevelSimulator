Q1Displacement, velocity, acceleration · Core · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 5 marks

A particle moves in a straight line so that, t seconds after passing through a fixed point O, its displacement from O is s m, where s = t³ − 9t² + 24t.

  1. (a) Find the values of t when the particle is instantaneously at rest. [3]
  2. (b) Find the acceleration of the particle when t = 5. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) t = 2 and t = 4
    v = 3t² − 18t + 24 B1; = 3(t − 2)(t − 4) = 0 M1; t = 2, 4 A1
  2. (b) 12 m/s²
    a = 6t − 18 M1; = 12 A1
Q2Displacement, velocity, acceleration · Core · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 5 marks

A particle starts from a fixed point O and moves in a straight line. Its velocity t seconds later is v m/s, where v = 5t − t².

  1. (a) Find the acceleration of the particle when t = 1. [2]
  2. (b) Find the displacement of the particle from O when t = 3. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) 3 m/s²
    a = 5 − 2t M1 = 3 A1
  2. (b) 13.5 m
    s = 5t²/2 − t³/3 (+ c, c = 0) M1 A1; s(3) = 22.5 − 9 = 13.5 A1

  1. (a) Find the acceleration of the particle when t = 2. [2]
  2. (b) Find the displacement of the particle from O when t = 2. [3]

    a = 6t − 2 M1 = 10 A1
  2. (b) 6 m

Q4Displacement, velocity, acceleration · Core · Paper 2 style · calculator · 5 marks

A cyclist sets off from rest along a straight road. Her velocity t seconds later is v m/s, where v = 0.3t(10 − t) for 0 ≤ t ≤ 10.

  1. (a) Find the maximum speed of the cyclist. [2]
  2. (b) Find the distance travelled by the cyclist in the 10 seconds. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) 7.5 m/s
    dv/dt = 3 − 0.6t = 0, t = 5 M1; v = 7.5 A1
  2. (b) 50 m
    ∫ (3t − 0.3t²) dt = 1.5t² − 0.1t³ M1 A1; 150 − 100 = 50 A1

  3. (c) Find the distance travelled in the first 4 seconds, correct to 3 significant figures. [3]
Show answer

  1. (a) Find the initial velocity and acceleration of the particle. [2]
  2. (b) Find the time at which the velocity is 8 m/s, correct to 3 significant figures, and the acceleration at that time. [4]
  3. (c) State the value that the velocity approaches as t becomes very large. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) v = 2 m/s, a = 2 m/s²
    B1 B1 (a = 2e^(−t/4))
  2. (b) t = 5.55 s; a = 0.5 m/s²
    e^(−t/4) = 0.25 M1; t = 4 ln 4 = 5.55 A1; a = 2 × 0.25 M1 = 0.5 A1
  3. (c) 10 m/s
    B1
Q10Displacement, velocity, acceleration · Standard · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 6 marks

A particle moves in a straight line so that, t seconds after passing through a fixed point O, its displacement from O is s m, where s = ln(t + 1) − t/3.

  1. (a) Find the time at which the particle is at rest. [2]
  2. (b) Find the exact displacement of the particle from O at this time. [2]
  3. (c) Find the acceleration of the particle at this time. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) t = 2
    v = 1/(t + 1) − ⅓ M1; t = 2 A1
  2. (b) ln 3 − ⅔ m
    s(2) = ln 3 − 2/3 B1 B1
  3. (c) −1/9 m/s²
    a = −1/(t + 1)² M1 = −1/9 A1
Q11Displacement, velocity, acceleration · Standard · Paper 2 style · calculator · 6 marks

A lift in an office tower in Dubai rises from rest. Its velocity t seconds later is v m/s, where v = 0.6t² − 0.1t³, for 0 ≤ t ≤ 6.

  1. (a) Show that the lift is at rest when t = 6. [1]
  2. (b) Find the maximum speed of the lift. [3]
  3. (c) Find the height through which the lift rises in the 6 seconds. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) v(6) = 21.6 − 21.6 = 0
    B1
  2. (b) 3.2 m/s
    dv/dt = 1.2t − 0.3t² = 0 M1; t = 4 A1; v = 3.2 A1
  3. (c) 10.8 m
    ∫₀⁶ v dt = [0.2t³ − 0.025t⁴] M1 = 10.8 A1
Q12Displacement, velocity, acceleration · Standard · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 6 marks

A particle moves in a straight line so that, t seconds after observations begin, its displacement from a fixed point O is s m, where s = 2t³ − 15t² + 24t + 5.

  1. (a) Find the values of t when the particle is at rest and its displacements from O at these times. [4]
  2. (b) Find the total distance travelled in the first 4 seconds. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) t = 1, s = 16; t = 4, s = −11
    v = 6t² − 30t + 24 = 6(t − 1)(t − 4) M1 A1; s(1) = 16, s(4) = −11 A1 A1
  2. (b) 38 m
    s(0) = 5: 11 + 27 M1 = 38 A1
Q13Displacement, velocity, acceleration · Standard · Paper 2 style · calculator · 5 marks

A particle starts from rest at O and moves in a straight line with velocity v = kt(8 − t) m/s, where k is a constant. It travels 36 m in the first 6 seconds.

  1. (a) Find the value of k. [3]
  2. (b) Find the acceleration of the particle when t = 6. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) k = 0.5
    ∫₀⁶ k(8t − t²) dt = k[4t² − t³/3] M1 A1; 72k = 36, k = 0.5 A1
  2. (b) −2 m/s²
    a = k(8 − 2t) M1 = −2 A1

Q15Displacement, velocity, acceleration · Challenge · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 6 marks

Two particles A and B leave a fixed point O at the same time and move in the same straight line. After t seconds, A has velocity (4t + 1) m/s and B has velocity 3t² m/s.

  1. (a) Find the exact value of t when A and B next meet. [4]
  2. (b) Find the velocities of A and B at this time, in the form a + b√2. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) t = 1 + √2
    s_A = 2t² + t, s_B = t³ B1; t³ − 2t² − t = 0 M1; t² − 2t − 1 = 0 M1; t = 1 + √2 A1
  2. (b) v_A = 5 + 4√2, v_B = 9 + 6√2
    B1 B1

Q17Displacement, velocity, acceleration · Challenge · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 7 marks

Show answer
  1. (a) t = ln 3; a = 6 m/s²
    (eᵗ − 1)(eᵗ − 3) = 0 M1; t = ln 3 A1; a = 2e^(2t) − 4eᵗ M1 = 18 − 12 = 6 A1

    s = ½e^(2t) − 4eᵗ + 3t M1; [ ] from 0 to ln 3 = 3 ln 3 − 4 A1; distance 4 − 3 ln 3 A1
Q18Displacement, velocity, acceleration · Challenge · Paper 2 style · calculator · 6 marks

A particle passes through O with velocity 6 m/s. Its acceleration t seconds later is a m/s², where a = −4/(t + 1)².

  1. (a) Find an expression for v in terms of t, and explain why the particle never comes to rest. [3]
  2. (b) Find the displacement of the particle from O when t = 3, correct to 3 significant figures. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) v = 4/(t + 1) + 2; v > 2 for all t ≥ 0
    integrate M1; c = 2 A1; 4/(t + 1) > 0 so v > 2 B1
  2. (b) 11.5 m

Q24Motion graphs · Standard · Paper 2 style · calculator · 5 marks

A train leaves a station from rest and accelerates uniformly to a speed of V m/s in 40 s. It travels at V m/s for 3 minutes and then decelerates uniformly to rest in 60 s. The distance between the stations is 4.2 km.

  1. (a) Sketch the velocity-time graph for the journey. [1]
  2. (b) Find the value of V, correct to 3 significant figures. [3]
  3. (c) Find the deceleration of the train. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) trapezium: rising line for 40 s, horizontal for 180 s, falling line for 60 s
    B1
  2. (b) 18.3 m/s
    area = ½(40)V + 180V + ½(60)V = 230V M1 A1; V = 4200/230 = 18.3 A1
  3. (c) 0.304 m/s²
    18.26 ÷ 60 B1

Q29Motion graphs · Challenge · Paper 2 style · calculator · 6 marks

A car in Peshawar starts from rest and accelerates uniformly to a maximum speed, then immediately decelerates uniformly to rest. The whole journey takes 60 s and covers 900 m. The magnitude of the deceleration is half the magnitude of the acceleration.

  1. (a) Find the maximum speed of the car. [2]
  2. (b) Find the acceleration and the deceleration of the car. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) 30 m/s
    area = ½ × 60 × V = 900 M1; V = 30 A1
  2. (b) 1.5 m/s² and 0.75 m/s²
    deceleration time = 2 × acceleration time M1; t₁ = 20, t₂ = 40 A1; 30/20 = 1.5 A1; 30/40 = 0.75 A1
