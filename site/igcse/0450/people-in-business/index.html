Q1Recruitment, selection and training · Core · Paper 1 style · 4 marks

Marvi Bank is opening a new branch in Hyderabad and has just hired 14 new customer service staff.

  1. (a) Define 'induction training'. [2]
  2. (b) State two benefits to Marvi Bank of giving induction training to the new staff. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Training given to new employees when they start a job to introduce them to the business, its people, rules and procedures.
    1 mark: training for new employees. 1 mark: introduces the business, e.g. layout, colleagues, health and safety, policies.
  2. (b) Any two, e.g. new staff become productive more quickly; they make fewer mistakes with customers; they feel welcome so are less likely to leave; they learn safety and security rules.
    1 mark each for any two valid benefits.
Q2Recruitment, selection and training · Core · Paper 1 style · 4 marks

Shalimar Grand Hotel in Karachi needs to recruit a new receptionist. The HR manager is preparing a job description and a person specification.

  1. (a) Identify two items that would be included in the job description for the receptionist. [2]
  2. (b) Identify two items that would be included in the person specification for the receptionist. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Any two, e.g. job title; main duties and tasks (checking guests in and out, answering phone calls); hours of work; who the receptionist reports to; place of work.
    1 mark each for any two items. Do not accept qualities of the person (they belong in the person specification).
  2. (b) Any two, e.g. qualifications required; experience needed; skills such as good spoken English and Urdu; personal qualities such as being polite and well presented.
    1 mark each for any two items describing the ideal candidate.
Q3Recruitment, selection and training · Core · Paper 1 style · 6 marks

Crust & Crumb is a bakery in Rawalpindi. It employs 4 part-time sales assistants who each work 25 hours a week. Each is paid PKR 450 per hour.

  1. (a) Calculate the bakery's total weekly wage cost for the part-time sales assistants. Show your working. [2]
  2. (b) Identify two methods the bakery could use to recruit new part-time sales assistants, and explain why each is suitable for a small bakery. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) PKR 45,000
    M1: 4 × 25 × 450. A1: PKR 45,000.
  2. (b) e.g. 1) A notice in the shop window: costs almost nothing and is seen by local people, such as students and parents, who may want part-time hours nearby. 2) Word of mouth / recommendation by current staff: free and quick, and current staff can suggest reliable people. Other: local social media group or community page (cheap, reaches local people); local newspaper (wider reach but costs more).
    For each of two methods: 1 mark for a suitable method, 1 mark for explaining why it suits a small local bakery (e.g. low cost, reaches local applicants). Do not credit expensive methods such as recruitment agencies or national newspapers unless justified.
Q4Recruitment, selection and training · Core · Paper 1 style · 4 marks

Al-Noor Textiles has closed one of its weaving sheds because demand for its cloth has fallen. It has also sacked one worker who was repeatedly late.

  1. (a) Define 'dismissal'. [2]
  2. (b) Identify two reasons why a business might make workers redundant. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) When an employee is made to leave their job because of their own fault, such as poor behaviour, breaking rules or poor performance.
    1 mark: employment ended by the employer. 1 mark: because of the employee's conduct or performance (not because the job is no longer needed).
  2. (b) Any two, e.g. fall in demand for the product; a factory or branch closes; machines/automation replace workers; the business relocates; a merger creates duplicate jobs.
    1 mark each for any two reasons where the job itself is no longer needed.
Q5Recruitment, selection and training · Standard · Paper 1 style · 8 marks

FreshMart is a supermarket chain with 18 stores in Islamabad and Rawalpindi. It needs a new store manager for its largest branch. The HR director wants to promote one of the current assistant managers.

  1. (a) Identify two methods FreshMart could use to recruit externally. [2]
  2. (b) Explain two advantages to FreshMart of recruiting the store manager internally. [6]
Show answer
  1. (a) Any two, e.g. job advertisements in newspapers; online job websites; recruitment agencies; university job fairs; social media.
    1 mark each.
  2. (b) e.g. 1) The assistant manager already knows FreshMart's systems, suppliers and customers, so needs little induction and can run the busy branch quickly, keeping sales high. 2) Promotion opportunities motivate other assistant managers across the 18 stores, which may reduce labour turnover and recruitment costs. Other: cheaper and quicker than advertising; the business already knows the candidate's ability and reliability.
    For each of the two points: 1 mark for identifying the point, 1 mark for applying it to the business, 1 mark for developing the consequence (max 3 per point). Application must refer to FreshMart, e.g. 18 stores, largest branch, supermarket.
Q6Recruitment, selection and training · Standard · Paper 1 style · 8 marks

Spice Route is a busy restaurant in Dubai. It is open from 11:00 to 23:00, and most customers come at lunchtime and in the evening. The owner is deciding whether to replace three full-time waiters with six part-time waiters.

  1. (a) Define 'part-time employee'. [2]
  2. (b) Explain one advantage and one disadvantage to Spice Route of employing part-time waiters. [6]
Show answer
  1. (a) An employee who works fewer hours per week than a full-time employee (for example fewer than about 30–35 hours).
    1 mark: works for the business. 1 mark: fewer hours than full-time / a normal working week.
  2. (b) Advantage: part-time waiters can be scheduled only at the lunch and evening peaks, so the restaurant does not pay staff during the quiet afternoon, lowering wage costs per meal. Disadvantage: six part-time waiters may be less committed and need more training and supervision, so service quality may fall and customers may not return.
    Advantage: 1 identify, 1 apply, 1 develop. Disadvantage: 1 identify, 1 apply, 1 develop. Other disadvantages: harder to communicate with more staff; more recruitment cost; staff may leave for full-time work.
Q7Recruitment, selection and training · Standard · Paper 1 style · 8 marks

Pak Packaging Ltd employs 25 workers who are all paid the minimum wage. The government has announced that the monthly minimum wage will rise from PKR 37,000 to PKR 40,000.

  1. (a) Calculate the increase in Pak Packaging's monthly wage bill for these 25 workers. Show your working. [2]
  2. (b) Identify two other legal controls over employment that affect businesses. [2]
  3. (c) Explain two ways the rise in the minimum wage could affect Pak Packaging. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) PKR 75,000 per month
    M1: 40,000 − 37,000 = 3,000 per worker. A1: 3,000 × 25 = PKR 75,000.
  2. (b) Any two, e.g. health and safety at work laws; laws against discrimination (for example by gender or religion); laws on maximum working hours; rules on unfair dismissal; employment contract requirements.
    1 mark each.
  3. (c) e.g. 1) Higher costs: the wage bill rises by PKR 75,000 a month, which reduces profit unless the firm raises the prices of its boxes. 2) Motivation: workers may feel better rewarded, so they may work harder and fewer may leave, which reduces recruitment costs.
    For each of two points: 1 mark for the effect, 1 mark for development in context. Other: may replace workers with machines; may recruit fewer staff.
Q8Recruitment, selection and training · Standard · Paper 2 style · 12 marks

Sindh Snacks makes crisps in a factory in Hyderabad. It needs a production supervisor for a shift of 30 workers. Appendix 1 shows the two shortlisted applicants.Appendix 1Applicant A – Amina: internal applicant; machine operator at Sindh Snacks for 8 years; no supervisory experience; has led the factory's safety committee; Intermediate certificate; expected salary PKR 95,000 per month.Applicant B – Bilal: external applicant; BSc Food Science; 3 years as a shift supervisor at a soft-drinks company; would need to move from Multan; expected salary PKR 120,000 per month.

  1. (a) Which applicant should Sindh Snacks choose as production supervisor? Justify your answer using suitable calculations and Appendix 1. [12]
Show answer
  1. (a) Calculations: PKR 300,000 per year (Bilal costs more). Amina: knows the machines, workers and procedures; cheaper by PKR 300,000 a year; safety committee shows leadership; promotion motivates other operators. But no supervisory experience and fewer qualifications; may find it hard to manage former colleagues. Bilal: has 3 years of supervisory experience and food science knowledge which may improve quality; brings new ideas. But costs more, needs induction, may not stay if relocation fails. Judgement e.g. Amina, because she knows the factory and the shift workers, and the saving can pay for supervisory training, which reduces the main risk.
    Calculations: (120,000 − 95,000) = 25,000 per month. 25,000 × 12 = PKR 300,000. Content: Bilal costs PKR 300,000 a year more; Amina — internal, knows machines and workers, safety committee leadership, motivates others, but no supervisory experience and may struggle to manage former colleagues; Bilal — 3 years' supervisory experience and food-science degree, new ideas, but dearer, needs induction and relocation. Level 2 (5–8): advantages and disadvantages analysed and applied to Sindh Snacks, with a decision that is only partly justified or one-sided. Level 1 (1–4): relevant knowledge with little application or analysis; a decision with no reasoning earns no evaluation marks. Q9Recruitment, selection and training · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 12 marks

CodeNest is a software company in Bengaluru, India. It has hired 12 new junior developers. It is considering two ways to train them in its programming systems.Option A – off-the-job course at a training institute: fee $850 per person, and each developer misses 5 working days. The company values a developer's lost working day at $120.Option B – on-the-job training by a senior developer: the senior developer is paid an extra $1,500 in total, and the company estimates that the senior developer's own lost output is worth $2,400.

  1. (a) Option B costs $3,900. Which option should CodeNest choose to train its new developers? Justify your answer using suitable calculations. [12]
Show answer
  1. (a) Calculations: $17,400. Option B is $13,500 cheaper and the training is directly related to CodeNest's own systems and projects; developers keep producing while learning. However, the senior developer's bad habits may be passed on, and his own work suffers. Option A gives expert, structured training and possibly recognised qualifications, but costs more and developers are away for 5 days. Judgement, e.g. Option B because CodeNest's systems are specific to the company, so an outside course may not cover them, and the saving of $13,500 is large.
    Calculations: cost per person = 850 + 5 × 120 = $1,450. 12 × 1,450 = $17,400. Content: Option A costs 12 × (850 + 5 × 120) = $17,400, so B is $13,500 cheaper; B uses CodeNest's own systems and developers keep working, but the senior developer's output falls and bad habits may pass on; A gives expert, structured training and possibly qualifications, but costs more and developers miss 5 days. Mark bands (12; knowledge 2, application 2, analysis 4, evaluation 4): Level 3 (9–12): developed analysis of both sides / all options applied to CodeNest, using the data, and a justified decision that explains why the rejected option(s) are weaker. Level 2 (5–8): advantages and disadvantages analysed and applied to CodeNest, with a decision that is only partly justified or one-sided. Level 1 (1–4): relevant knowledge with little application or analysis; a decision with no reasoning earns no evaluation marks. Q10Recruitment, selection and training · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 8 marks

Medicare Pharmacies has 40 branches in Pakistan. When a branch manager leaves, head office always advertises the job externally.

  1. (a) Identify two stages of the recruitment and selection process after a vacancy has been advertised. [2]
  2. (b) Do you think Medicare Pharmacies should always recruit branch managers externally? Justify your answer. [6]
Show answer
  1. (a) Any two, e.g. receiving application forms/CVs; shortlisting applicants; interviewing; testing (skills or aptitude tests); checking references; offering the job/contract of employment.
    1 mark each.
  2. (b) For: brings new ideas and experience from other retailers; wider choice of applicants, e.g. qualified pharmacists; avoids jealousy between internal candidates. Against: more expensive and slower (advertising, interviews); new manager needs induction and does not know the 40-branch systems; existing staff are demotivated if they never get promotion, so good pharmacists may leave. Judgement, e.g. no – recruit internally where a suitable assistant manager exists, and only go external when no one has the skills, because promotion chances keep experienced staff.
    Up to 2 marks for knowledge/analysis of points for, up to 2 for points against, 2 marks for a justified judgement that explains why one side outweighs the other in this context.

Q14Organisation and management · Core · Paper 1 style · 4 marks

Q15Organisation and management · Standard · Paper 1 style · 6 marks

Q18Organisation and management · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 8 marks

Q23Motivating employees · Standard · Paper 1 style · 6 marks

Sialkot Sportswear pays its machinists a piece rate of PKR 35 for each shirt they sew. Last week Saima sewed 340 shirts. The business is considering changing to a time rate of PKR 230 per hour for a 48-hour week.

  1. (a) Calculate how much more Saima earned last week under the piece rate than she would have under the time rate. Show your working. [2]
  2. (b) Outline one advantage and one disadvantage to Sialkot Sportswear of paying a piece rate. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) PKR 860
    M1: piece rate 340 × 35 = 11,900; time rate 48 × 230 = 11,040. A1: 11,900 − 11,040 = PKR 860.
  2. (b) Advantage: machinists are encouraged to sew more shirts, which raises output and the business only pays for what is produced. Disadvantage: workers may rush, so the quality of the shirts may fall and more need to be checked or rejected.
    2 marks each: 1 for the point, 1 for linking it to Sialkot Sportswear. Other disadvantage: workers' pay is unstable, which may cause stress or staff leaving.
Q24Motivating employees · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 8 marks

StarLink Contact Centre in Manila, Philippines, has many staff leaving within a year. Staff say the work is repetitive and boring, although the pay is above average.

  1. (a) Using Herzberg's theory, explain why the above-average pay is not stopping staff from leaving. [2]
  2. (b) Explain two non-financial methods StarLink could use to motivate staff. Which method is most likely to reduce the number of staff leaving? Justify your answer. [6]
Show answer
  1. (a) Pay is a hygiene factor: good pay prevents dissatisfaction but does not motivate; staff need motivators such as interesting work, recognition and responsibility.
    1 mark: pay is a hygiene factor. 1 mark: hygiene factors only remove dissatisfaction / motivators are missing.
  2. (b) Job rotation: staff move between tasks (inbound calls, emails, complaints), reducing boredom, but each task may still be repetitive. Job enrichment: staff are given more responsibility, e.g. solving complaints themselves, which gives a sense of achievement (a Herzberg motivator), but needs training. Other: teamworking, training and promotion opportunities, recognition schemes. Judgement, e.g. job enrichment, because the problem is boring work and enrichment adds the motivators Herzberg says are missing, whereas rotation only varies equally simple tasks.
    Up to 2 marks for each method (knowledge + analysis). 2 marks for a justified choice that explains why the other method is less effective.
Q25Motivating employees · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 12 marks

Indus Insurance's monthly salary bill for its sales staff is PKR 3.6 million. Sales have fallen. The managing director is choosing between:Option 1 – a bonus of 5% of the salary bill, paid every month if the sales target is met;Option 2 – a training and promotion programme costing PKR 900,000 in its first year, with clear career steps for staff.

  1. (a) Which option should Indus Insurance choose to motivate its sales staff? Justify your answer using suitable calculations. [12]
Show answer
  1. (a) Calculations: PKR 2,160,000. Option 1: directly linked to sales, so it may raise sales quickly; only paid if targets are met; but costs up to PKR 2.16 m a year, may encourage mis-selling, and the effect fades (Herzberg: money is a hygiene factor). Option 2: cheaper (PKR 0.9 m); satisfies esteem and self-actualisation needs; builds skills that help selling; but the effect on sales is slower and some trained staff may leave for rivals. Judgement with reasoning, e.g. Option 2, because it costs less than half of Option 1 and gives long-term motivation, unless sales must rise immediately.
    Calculations: 5% × 3.6 m = PKR 180,000 per month. × 12 = PKR 2,160,000. Content: Option 1 costs up to 5% × 3.6 m × 12 = PKR 2.16 m a year, paid only if targets are met, quick link to sales, risk of mis-selling, money as a hygiene factor; Option 2 costs PKR 0.9 m, meets esteem and self-actualisation needs, builds selling skills, slower effect, trained staff may leave. Mark bands (12; knowledge 2, application 2, analysis 4, evaluation 4): Level 3 (9–12): developed analysis of both sides / all options applied to Indus Insurance, using the data, and a justified decision that explains why the rejected option(s) are weaker. Level 2 (5–8): advantages and disadvantages analysed and applied to Indus Insurance, with a decision that is only partly justified or one-sided. Level 1 (1–4): relevant knowledge with little application or analysis; a decision with no reasoning earns no evaluation marks. Correct calculations count towards application and analysis; follow through an earlier calculation error.
Q26Internal and external communication · Core · Paper 1 style · 4 marks

Royal Furniture makes sofas in Gujranwala and sells them to shops across Pakistan.

  1. (a) Define 'internal communication'. [2]
  2. (b) Identify two barriers to effective communication. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) The exchange of information between people within the same organisation.
    1 mark: passing information/messages. 1 mark: between people inside the business (e.g. managers and workers).
  2. (b) Any two, e.g. use of jargon/technical language; poor listening; noise or distractions; wrong medium chosen; message too long or unclear; language differences; long chain of command; poor attitude towards the sender.

Q27Internal and external communication · Core · Paper 1 style · 4 marks

The manager of a clothing shop in Quetta uses notices on the staff board and also holds a weekly staff meeting.

  1. (a) Explain the difference between one-way and two-way communication. [2]
  2. (b) State which of the two methods used by the manager is two-way communication, and give one benefit of using it. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) One-way communication goes from the sender to the receiver with no reply or feedback; two-way communication allows the receiver to reply and give feedback.
    1 mark for each correct description.
  2. (b) The weekly staff meeting; benefit, e.g. staff can ask questions so the manager can check they have understood / staff can suggest ideas.
    1 mark: staff meeting. 1 mark: valid benefit.
Q28Internal and external communication · Standard · Paper 1 style · 8 marks

SpeedPost, a courier firm, has 400 delivery drivers across Karachi. It must tell all drivers about a change to delivery routes that starts in two days.

  1. (a) Identify two elements of effective communication, other than the message itself. [2]
  2. (b) Explain two methods SpeedPost could use. Which method is best? Justify your answer. [6]
Show answer
  1. (a) Any two: sender (transmitter); receiver; medium (method used); feedback.
    1 mark each.
  2. (b) Text message to phones: reaches all 400 drivers immediately wherever they are, cheap, and gives a written record, but drivers may not read it or have questions. Depot briefing meeting: two-way so drivers can ask about new routes and managers can check understanding, but hard to gather 400 drivers who work different shifts within two days. Judgement, e.g. text messages followed by a short briefing at each depot for questions, because speed matters with only two days and the drivers are spread across the city.
    Up to 2 marks for each method (application + analysis). 2 marks for a justified judgement.
Q29Internal and external communication · Standard · Paper 1 style · 8 marks

Habib Agro Exports has offices in Multan and Hamburg, Germany. Managers in the two offices often misunderstand each other's emails and orders are sometimes sent with the wrong specifications.

  1. (a) Explain two barriers to communication that Habib Agro Exports faces, and suggest how each could be reduced. [6]
  2. (b) Outline one example of external communication for Habib Agro Exports. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 1) Language: emails written in a second language may be unclear; reduce by using simple English, translators, or standard order forms. 2) Distance / time-zone difference: messages wait hours for a reply, so problems are not cleared up; reduce with scheduled video calls during overlapping office hours. Other: technical jargon about produce specifications – use a glossary; no feedback – ask the receiver to confirm the order.
    For each of two barriers: 1 mark for the barrier, 1 mark for its effect in context, 1 mark for a suitable way to reduce it.
  2. (b) e.g. sending an invoice or a price list to a German supermarket customer; emailing a shipping company about delivery dates.
    1 mark for a message to someone outside the business; 1 mark for a relevant method/context.

CareWell Hospitals has 1,500 staff in three hospitals in Lahore. Staff complain that they do not hear about changes to policies. Managers are choosing one method:Option 1 – a printed monthly newsletter costing PKR 60 per copy, one copy per staff member each month;Option 2 – a staff intranet and phone app: set-up cost PKR 800,000 plus PKR 150,000 per year maintenance;Option 3 – a 30-minute weekly team meeting on each ward, led by ward managers.

  1. (a) Which communication method should CareWell Hospitals choose? Justify your answer using suitable calculations. [12]
