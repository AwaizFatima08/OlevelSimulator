Q1Tests for ions, gases & water · Core · Paper 2 style · 1 marks
  1. (a) A solution of an unknown salt is tested. Aqueous sodium hydroxide gives a white precipitate that dissolves in excess to give a colourless solution. Aqueous ammonia gives a white precipitate that does not dissolve in excess. Which cation is present?A Al³⁺B Ca²⁺C Zn²⁺D Cr³⁺ [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) A Al³⁺
    Al³⁺: white ppt soluble in excess NaOH(aq), insoluble in excess NH₃(aq). Zn²⁺ is wrong because its white ppt also dissolves in excess ammonia; Ca²⁺ ppt does not dissolve in excess NaOH; Cr³⁺ gives a green ppt.
Q2Tests for ions, gases & water · Core · Paper 6 style · 6 marks

Four gases, A to D, are collected in test-tubes during practical work.

  1. (a) Gas A relights a glowing splint. Identify gas A. [1]
  2. (b) Gas B turns damp red litmus paper blue. Identify gas B. [1]
  3. (c) Gas C bleaches damp litmus paper. Identify gas C. [1]
  4. (d) Gas D turns acidified aqueous potassium manganate(VII) from purple to colourless. Identify gas D. [1]
  5. (e) Describe the test for carbon dioxide and its positive result. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Oxygen
    B1
  2. (b) Ammonia
    B1
  3. (c) Chlorine
    B1
  4. (d) Sulfur dioxide
    B1
  5. (e) Bubble the gas through limewater; limewater turns milky.
