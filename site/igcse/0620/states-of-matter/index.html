Q3Kinetic particle theory & changes of state (incl. gas P/V/T) · Core · Paper 4 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) Describe the arrangement and the movement of the particles in a solid. [2]
  2. (b) Describe how the arrangement and movement of the particles change when the solid melts. [2]
  3. (c) State two differences between the particles in a liquid and the particles in a gas. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Particles are packed closely in a regular arrangement (lattice); they vibrate about fixed positions.
    B1 close together / regular pattern; B1 vibrate only, in fixed positions.
