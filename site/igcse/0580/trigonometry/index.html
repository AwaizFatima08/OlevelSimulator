Q8Sine & cosine rules, ½ab sin C · Challenge · Paper 4 style · calculator · 9 marks

  1. (a) Calculate AC. [3]
  2. (b) Calculate CD. [3]
  3. (c) Calculate the area of the field ABCD, correct to 3 significant figures. [3]
Show answer

  3. (c) Calculate the bearing of B from H, correct to the nearest degree. [3]
Show answer
