Q1Averages & range · Core · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 5 marks

The ages, in years, of 9 children at a cricket academy in Sialkot are:12, 15, 11, 14, 12, 13, 16, 12, 12

  1. (a) Write down the mode. [1]
  2. (b) Find the median. [1]
  3. (c) Find the range. [1]
  4. (d) Calculate the mean. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 12
    B1
  2. (b) 12
    Ordered: 11, 12, 12, 12, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16; middle (5th) value. B1
  3. (c) 5
    16 − 11 = 5. B1
  4. (d) 13
    Total = 117 M1; 117 ÷ 9 = 13 A1
Q2Averages & range · Core · Paper 3 style · calculator · 5 marks

The rainfall, in mm, recorded in Lahore on 10 days in July was:0, 12.4, 3.6, 0, 25.0, 8.2, 0, 15.8, 4.4, 6.6

  1. (a) Calculate the mean daily rainfall. [2]
  2. (b) Find the median daily rainfall. [2]
  3. (c) Find the range. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) 7.6 mm
    Total = 76.0 M1; 76.0 ÷ 10 = 7.6 A1
  2. (b) 5.5 mm
    Ordered: 0, 0, 0, 3.6, 4.4, 6.6, 8.2, 12.4, 15.8, 25.0 M1; (4.4 + 6.6) ÷ 2 = 5.5 A1
  3. (c) 25 mm
    25.0 − 0 = 25. B1

Q4Averages & range · Standard · Paper 2 style · no calculator · 3 marks

A teacher asked her class how many books they read last month.Number of books: 0, 1, 2, 3Frequency: 3, x, 6, 4The mean number of books is 1.55.

  1. (a) Show that 0.55x = 3.85. [2]
  2. (b) Find x. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) (x + 24) ÷ (x + 13) = 1.55 gives x + 24 = 1.55x + 20.15, so 0.55x = 3.85
    Σfx = x + 12 + 12 = x + 24 and Σf = x + 13 M1; equation and rearrangement A1
  2. (b) x = 7
    3.85 ÷ 0.55 = 7 B1
Q5Averages & range · Standard · Paper 2 style · no calculator · 5 marks

The mean of five numbers is 8. Four of the numbers are 3, 7, 9 and 12.

Q11Averages & range · Challenge · Paper 2 style · no calculator · 5 marks
