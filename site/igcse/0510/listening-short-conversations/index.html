Q1Multiple choice (A–C), two questions per recording · Core · Paper 2 style · 2 marks

You will hear two friends, Ahmed and Usman, talking about a school trip. For each question, choose the correct answer, A, B or C.Transcript (read it aloud, or read it silently)Ahmed: Are you excited about the trip to Murree on Friday?Usman: Yes, but I'm worried about the cold. My mum says it might snow.Ahmed: The teacher said to bring a warm jacket, gloves and a hat. I'm going to buy new gloves tonight.Usman: I've got gloves, but I need a hat. Do you know what time the bus leaves?Ahmed: Seven o'clock, from the school car park. We'll be back at about eight in the evening.Usman: Seven? That's so early! I'll have to set two alarms.

  1. (a) What does Usman need to get for the trip? [1]
  2. (b) What time does the bus leave? [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) A a hat
    'I've got gloves, but I need a hat.' Ahmed is buying gloves.
  2. (b) A 7 a.m.
    Seven o'clock; 8 p.m. is the return time.

  2. (b) C 1,100 rupees
    She is not a student, so she pays 1,100.
