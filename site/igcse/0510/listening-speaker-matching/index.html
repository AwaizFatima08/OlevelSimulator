Q1Six speakers matched to opinions · Core · Paper 2 style · 6 marks

You will hear six people talking about learning to cook. For Speakers 1 to 6, choose from the list A–H which opinion each speaker expresses. Use each letter only once. There are two extra letters which you do not need to use.A Cooking is a good way to save money.B Cooking with family members is enjoyable.C Online videos are the best way to learn.D Cooking helps you eat more healthily.E Making mistakes is part of learning.F Cooking is too time-consuming for students.G Everyone should learn some basic dishes before leaving home.H Cooking is a creative activity.Transcript (read it aloud, or read it silently)Speaker 1: My mother always cooked for us, and when I moved to university I could only boil an egg! I really think every young person should learn to make at least a few simple meals before they live on their own. It would have saved me a lot of stress.Speaker 2: I burned the rice the first three times I tried to make biryani. My brother laughed at me, but I don't mind. You only find out how long things take by getting them wrong, and now my biryani is the best in the family.Speaker 3: People say cooking saves money, but for me the real reason is what goes into my body. When I make my own food, I use less oil and salt, and I add more vegetables. I feel much fitter than when I bought takeaway meals.Speaker 4: I've tried recipe videos, but honestly they go too fast. What I love is Saturday afternoons in the kitchen with my grandmother. She tells stories while we chop onions, and we laugh a lot. The food is almost less important than the time together.Speaker 5: For me, a recipe is just a starting point. I like to change things – add a different spice, or mix ideas from Thai and Pakistani cooking. It's like painting, but you can eat the result.Speaker 6: I live with three friends, and if we bought ready-made meals every day we'd have no money left by the middle of the month. We take turns to cook big pots of daal and vegetables, and it costs very little. I think it's the cheapest way to live.

  1. (a) Speaker 1 [1]
  2. (b) Speaker 2 [1]
  3. (c) Speaker 3 [1]
  4. (d) Speaker 4 [1]
  5. (e) Speaker 5 [1]
  6. (f) Speaker 6 [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) G – Everyone should learn some basic dishes before leaving home.
    'every young person should learn… a few simple meals before they live on their own'.
  2. (b) E – Making mistakes is part of learning.
    'You only find out… by getting them wrong' = mistakes are part of learning.
  3. (c) D – Cooking helps you eat more healthily.
    Mentions saving money but says 'the real reason is what goes into my body' – health, not A.
  4. (d) B – Cooking with family members is enjoyable.
    Rejects videos (not C); enjoys cooking with her grandmother.
  5. (e) H – Cooking is a creative activity.
    'like painting' – creative.
  6. (f) A – Cooking is a good way to save money.
    'the cheapest way to live' – saving money. (Unused options: C and F.)
