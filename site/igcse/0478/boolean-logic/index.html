X = (A AND B) OR (NOT C)

  1. (a) Complete the truth table for X for all combinations of inputs A, B, C. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) A B C | X0 0 0 | 10 0 1 | 00 1 0 | 10 1 1 | 01 0 0 | 11 0 1 | 01 1 0 | 11 1 1 | 1
    1 mark for each two correct rows (8 rows = 4 marks).
Q2Logic circuits, expressions and problem statements · Core · Paper 2 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) A logic circuit is described as follows. Inputs A and B go into an OR gate. Input C goes into a NOT gate. The outputs of the OR gate and the NOT gate are the inputs to an AND gate, whose output is X. Write the logic expression for this circuit. [2]
  2. (b) Complete the truth table for X for all combinations of inputs A, B, C. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) X = (A OR B) AND (NOT C)
    1 mark for (A OR B), 1 mark for AND NOT C correctly combined.
  2. (b) A B C | X0 0 0 | 00 0 1 | 00 1 0 | 10 1 1 | 01 0 0 | 11 0 1 | 01 1 0 | 11 1 1 | 0
    1 mark for each two correct rows (8 rows = 4 marks).
Q3Logic circuits, expressions and problem statements · Core · Paper 2 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) A garden sprinkler S is switched on (S = 1) when the soil is dry (D = 1) and it is not raining (R = 0), or when the manual switch is on (M = 1). Write a logic expression for S. [3]
  2. (b) Complete the truth table for S for all combinations of inputs D, R, M. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) S = (D AND NOT R) OR M
    1 mark for D AND …, 1 mark for NOT R, 1 mark for OR M correctly placed.
  2. (b) D R M | S0 0 0 | 00 0 1 | 10 1 0 | 00 1 1 | 11 0 0 | 11 0 1 | 11 1 0 | 01 1 1 | 1
    1 mark for each two correct rows (8 rows = 4 marks).

Q5Logic circuits, expressions and problem statements · Core · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Complete the truth table for both expressions.X = (A OR B) AND (NOT (A AND B))Y = A XOR B [3]
  2. (b) State what the truth table shows about the two expressions. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) A B | X Y0 0 | 0 00 1 | 1 11 0 | 1 11 1 | 0 0
    X column correct: 1 mark. Y column correct: 1 mark. Both columns fully correct: 1 further mark.
  2. (b) They are equivalent: they give the same output for every combination of inputs.
    1 mark.
Q6Logic circuits, expressions and problem statements · Core · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) A circuit has two inputs, P and Q. P goes into a NOT gate. The output of the NOT gate and Q are the inputs to an AND gate, whose output is X. Write the logic expression for X. [1]
  2. (b) Complete the truth table for X for all combinations of inputs P, Q. [2]
  3. (c) State the values of P and Q that make X = 1. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) X = (NOT P) AND Q
    1 mark.
  2. (b) P Q | X0 0 | 00 1 | 11 0 | 01 1 | 0
    1 mark for each two correct rows.
  3. (c) P = 0 and Q = 1
    1 mark.

Q11Logic circuits, expressions and problem statements · Standard · Paper 2 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) A logic circuit is described as follows. Inputs A and B go into a NOR gate. Inputs B and C go into a NAND gate. The outputs of the NOR gate and the NAND gate are the inputs to an OR gate, whose output is X. Write the logic expression for this circuit. [2]
  2. (b) Complete the truth table for X for all combinations of inputs A, B, C. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) X = (A NOR B) OR (B NAND C)
    1 mark for (A NOR B) and (B NAND C), 1 mark for joining them with OR.

    1 mark for each two correct rows (8 rows = 4 marks).
Q12Logic circuits, expressions and problem statements · Standard · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) The truth table shows the output X of a logic circuit.A B C | X0 0 0 | 00 0 1 | 10 1 0 | 00 1 1 | 11 0 0 | 11 0 1 | 11 1 0 | 01 1 1 | 1Write a logic expression that gives this output. [3]

Q16Logic circuits, expressions and problem statements · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 8 marks
  1. (a) A crane on a building site in Dubai is allowed to lift (L = 1) only when the load sensor shows the load is within the safe limit (W = 1) and either the wind speed is not high (H = 0) or the site engineer has turned an override key (E = 1). Write a logic expression for L. [3]
  2. (b) Complete the truth table for L for all combinations of inputs W, H, E. [4]
  3. (c) Explain why the override key cannot make the crane lift an unsafe load. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) L = W AND (NOT H OR E)
    1 mark for W AND …, 1 mark for NOT H, 1 mark for OR E inside the bracket.
  2. (b) W H E | L0 0 0 | 00 0 1 | 00 1 0 | 00 1 1 | 01 0 0 | 11 0 1 | 11 1 0 | 01 1 1 | 1
    1 mark for each two correct rows (8 rows = 4 marks).
  3. (c) W is ANDed with the rest of the expression, so if W = 0 then L = 0 whatever E is.
    1 mark.
Q17Logic circuits, expressions and problem statements · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 8 marks
  1. (a) A bank vault unlocks (V = 1) when at least two of its three managers, A, B and C, have inserted their keys (1 = key inserted). Write a logic expression for V using only gates with at most two inputs. [4]
  2. (b) Complete the truth table for V for all combinations of inputs A, B, C. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) V = (A AND B) OR (C AND (A XOR B)) — or equivalent, e.g. (A AND B) OR (A AND C) OR (B AND C)
    1 mark for A AND B; 1 mark for a term covering C with A; 1 mark for a term covering C with B; 1 mark for correct OR combination with no extra combinations. Accept any equivalent expression.
  2. (b) A B C | V0 0 0 | 00 0 1 | 00 1 0 | 00 1 1 | 11 0 0 | 01 0 1 | 11 1 0 | 11 1 1 | 1
    1 mark for each two correct rows (8 rows = 4 marks).
Q18Logic circuits, expressions and problem statements · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 8 marks
  1. (a) A greenhouse heater H is switched on (H = 1) when the temperature is low (T = 0) and it is night-time (N = 1), unless a window is open (W = 1), in which case the heater stays off. Write a logic expression for H. [3]
  2. (b) Complete the truth table for H for all combinations of inputs T, N, W. [4]
  3. (c) State how many NOT gates are needed to draw the circuit directly from your expression in (a), using only AND, OR and NOT gates. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) H = (NOT T AND N) AND NOT W
    1 mark for NOT T, 1 mark for AND N, 1 mark for AND NOT W.
  2. (b) T N W | H0 0 0 | 00 0 1 | 00 1 0 | 10 1 1 | 01 0 0 | 01 0 1 | 01 1 0 | 01 1 1 | 0
    1 mark for each two correct rows (8 rows = 4 marks).
  3. (c) 2
    1 mark. (One NOT gate for T and one for W.)

Q20Logic circuits, expressions and problem statements · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 6 marks

A student claims that the circuit X = (A XOR B) XOR C outputs 1 whenever an odd number of its inputs are 1.

  1. (a) Complete the truth table for X for all combinations of inputs A, B, C. [4]
  2. (b) Use your truth table to state whether the student is correct. [1]
  3. (c) Suggest a use for this circuit in data transmission. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) A B C | X0 0 0 | 00 0 1 | 10 1 0 | 10 1 1 | 01 0 0 | 11 0 1 | 01 1 0 | 01 1 1 | 1
    1 mark for each two correct rows (8 rows = 4 marks).
  2. (b) Correct: X = 1 for exactly the rows with one or three inputs equal to 1.
    1 mark.
  3. (c) Generating/checking a parity bit (it gives the parity of the bits).
    1 mark.
Q21Logic circuits, expressions and problem statements · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) The truth table shows the output X of a logic circuit.A B C | X0 0 0 | 00 0 1 | 00 1 0 | 10 1 1 | 01 0 0 | 01 0 1 | 01 1 0 | 11 1 1 | 1Write a logic expression for X using no more than three gates. [4]
  2. (b) Explain why X is always 0 when B = 0. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) X = B AND (A OR NOT C) (or an equivalent expression)
    1 mark for B AND …; 1 mark for A OR …; 1 mark for NOT C; 1 mark for correct structure using no more than three gates. Accept equivalents, e.g. (B AND NOT C) OR (A AND B).
  2. (b) B is ANDed with the rest of the expression, so B = 0 forces the AND gate output to 0.
    1 mark.
Q22Logic circuits, expressions and problem statements · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 8 marks
  1. (a) A conveyor belt in a rice mill runs (R = 1) only when its motor is not overheating (M = 0) and exactly one of these is true: the hopper is full (F = 1), or the operator presses the manual start button (S = 1). Write a logic expression for R. [3]
  2. (b) Complete the truth table for R for all combinations of inputs M, F, S. [4]
  3. (c) Explain why an XOR gate is used rather than an OR gate. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) R = NOT M AND (F XOR S)
    1 mark for NOT M, 1 mark for F XOR S, 1 mark for AND joining them.
  2. (b) M F S | R0 0 0 | 00 0 1 | 10 1 0 | 10 1 1 | 01 0 0 | 01 0 1 | 01 1 0 | 01 1 1 | 0
    1 mark for each two correct rows (8 rows = 4 marks).
  3. (c) XOR gives 1 only when exactly one input is 1; OR would also give 1 when both F and S are 1.
    1 mark.
