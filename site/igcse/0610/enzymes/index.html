  2. (b) Enzymes are not used up / are unchanged at the end of the reaction, so each enzyme molecule can break down many lactose molecules.
    B1 not used up / unchanged; B1 reused many times.
  3. (c) The active site may change shape so lactose no longer fits; fewer or no enzyme–substrate complexes; lactose not broken down.
    1 mark: active site shape changed; 1 mark: lactose no longer binds / enzyme does not work (or works more slowly).

  3. (c) The experiment is repeated with twice the concentration of enzyme and the same amount of substrate. Predict the effect on the initial rate and on the final mass of product. Explain your answer. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) 8 mg per minute
    16 ÷ 2 = 8 mg/min (B1).
  2. (b) All the substrate has been used up / converted to product, so there is nothing left for the enzyme to act on; the enzyme is not used up.
