Q2Standard electrode potentials & cells · Core · Paper 4 style · 6 marks

A cell is made from a Zn²⁺(aq)/Zn half-cell and an Ag⁺(aq)/Ag half-cell under standard conditions.E°: Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻ ⇌ Zn −0.76 V; Ag⁺ + e⁻ ⇌ Ag +0.80 V

  1. (a) Calculate the standard cell potential, E°cell. [1]
  2. (b) State which electrode is the negative electrode and give the direction of electron flow in the external circuit. [2]
  3. (c) Write the equation for the overall cell reaction. [1]
  4. (d) The salt bridge is made from filter paper soaked in saturated potassium nitrate, not potassium chloride. Explain the purpose of the salt bridge and why KCl is not suitable here. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) +1.56 V
    E°cell = E°(right/positive) − E°(left/negative) = +0.80 − (−0.76) = +1.56 V
  2. (b) The zinc electrode is negative; electrons flow from zinc to silver through the wire.
    B1 zinc is negative (more negative E°). B1 electrons from Zn to Ag in the external circuit.
  3. (c) Zn + 2Ag⁺ → Zn²⁺ + 2Ag
    B1 balanced, correct species (2 Ag⁺ per Zn).
  4. (d) It completes the circuit by allowing ions to move between the half-cells (keeps each solution neutral); Cl⁻ would precipitate Ag⁺ as AgCl, lowering [Ag⁺] and changing the potential.
    B1 allows movement of ions / completes circuit without mixing solutions. B1 KCl gives AgCl precipitate with Ag⁺(aq).

  1. (a) Use the data to calculate E°cell for the reaction between acidified MnO₄⁻ and Cl⁻ ions. [2]
  2. (b) Explain why dilute hydrochloric acid is not used to acidify potassium manganate(VII) in a titration with iron(II) ions. [2]
  3. (c) Suggest why dilute sulfuric acid is suitable. [1]

Q7Standard electrode potentials & cells · Standard · Paper 4 style · 5 marks

E°: H₂O₂ + 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ ⇌ 2H₂O +1.77 V; O₂ + 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ ⇌ H₂O₂ +0.68 V

  1. (a) Use the data to show that hydrogen peroxide can disproportionate under standard conditions. Calculate E°cell. [2]
  2. (b) Write the equation for the disproportionation. [1]
  3. (c) A bottle of 6% hydrogen peroxide from a pharmacy can be kept for months. Explain why this does not contradict your answer to (a). [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) H₂O₂ acts as both oxidising agent (+1.77 V couple) and reducing agent (+0.68 V couple): E°cell = 1.77 − 0.68 = +1.09 V, positive so feasible.

  1. (a) Calculate E° for the M²⁺/M half-cell. [1]
  2. (b) Calculate the mass of copper deposited on the copper electrode. Give your answer to 3 significant figures. [2]
  3. (c) Use the loss in mass of electrode M to calculate the relative atomic mass of M and suggest its identity. [2]

    M1 mol M = mol Cu = 0.00560; A1 0.629 ÷ 0.00560 = 112.4, Cd (112.4)
