Q1Kc & Kp calculations · Core · Paper 2 style · 4 marks

Hydrogen and iodine react in a sealed flask: H₂(g) + I₂(g) ⇌ 2HI(g). At 700 K the equilibrium concentrations are [H₂] = 0.0040 mol/dm³, [I₂] = 0.0025 mol/dm³ and [HI] = 0.0230 mol/dm³.

  1. (a) Write an expression for Kc for this reaction. [1]
  2. (b) Calculate the value of Kc at this temperature. Give your answer to 3 significant figures. [2]
  3. (c) Explain why Kc for this reaction has no units. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) Kc = [HI]² / ([H₂][I₂])
    B1
  2. (b) 52.9
    M1 0.0230² / (0.0040 × 0.0025)A1 52.9
  3. (c) There are equal numbers of moles of gas (concentration terms) on each side, so the units cancel.
    B1

Q3Kc & Kp calculations · Core · Paper 4 style · 3 marks

Carbon monoxide and steam are sealed in a 2.00 dm³ vessel at 900 K: CO(g) + H₂O(g) ⇌ CO₂(g) + H₂(g). At equilibrium the vessel contains 0.40 mol CO, 0.20 mol H₂O, 0.60 mol CO₂ and 0.60 mol H₂.

  1. (a) Write the expression for Kc for this reaction. [1]
  2. (b) Calculate the value of Kc at 900 K and state its units. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Kc = [CO₂][H₂] / ([CO][H₂O])
    B1 correct expression with products on top
  2. (b) Kc = 4.50 (no units)
    M1 concentrations: [CO] = 0.20, [H₂O] = 0.10, [CO₂] = 0.30, [H₂] = 0.30 mol dm⁻³A1 Kc = 0.30 × 0.30 / (0.20 × 0.10) = 4.50; no units because the number of gas molecules is the same on both sides
Q4Kc & Kp calculations · Core · Paper 4 style · 3 marks

Sulfur dioxide and oxygen are sealed in a container at 700 K: 2SO₂(g) + O₂(g) ⇌ 2SO₃(g). At equilibrium [SO₂] = 0.40 mol dm⁻³, [O₂] = 0.20 mol dm⁻³ and [SO₃] = 0.80 mol dm⁻³.

  1. (a) Calculate Kc for this equilibrium, giving its units. [2]
  2. (b) The forward reaction is exothermic. State and explain how Kc changes if the temperature is raised. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) Kc = 20.0 mol⁻¹ dm³
    M1 Kc = [SO₃]² / ([SO₂]²[O₂]) = 0.80² / (0.40² × 0.20)A1 = 20.0; units (mol dm⁻³)^(2−3) = mol⁻¹ dm³
  2. (b) Kc decreases, because a higher temperature favours the endothermic backward reaction and lowers the amount of SO₃ relative to SO₂ and O₂
    B1 decreases with a reason linked to the equilibrium shifting left

Q7Kc & Kp calculations · Standard · Paper 4 style · 5 marks

Nitrosyl chloride decomposes reversibly: 2NOCl(g) ⇌ 2NO(g) + Cl₂(g). A sealed container at 400 K is filled with 2.00 mol NOCl. At equilibrium it contains 1.00 mol NOCl, 1.00 mol NO and 0.50 mol Cl₂. The total pressure is 200 kPa.

  1. (a) Calculate the partial pressure of NOCl at equilibrium, in kPa. [2]
  2. (b) Write the expression for Kp for this equilibrium. [1]
  3. (c) Calculate Kp, giving its units. [2]

    M1 mole fraction of NOCl = 1.00 / 2.50 = 0.400A1 p(NOCl) = 0.400 × 200 = 80 kPa

    M1 p(NO) = 1.00/2.50 × 200 = 80 kPa and p(Cl₂) = 0.50/2.50 × 200 = 40 kPaA1 Kp = 80² × 40 / 80² = 40 kPa
Q8Kc & Kp calculations · Standard · Paper 4 style · 4 marks

Compounds A₂ and B₂ react reversibly: A₂(g) + B₂(g) ⇌ 2AB(g). A 1.00 dm³ sealed flask initially contains 1.00 mol A₂ and 1.00 mol B₂ and no AB. At equilibrium Kc = 9.00 at this temperature.

  1. (a) Write the expression for Kc. [1]
  2. (b) Show that the amount of AB at equilibrium is 1.20 mol. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) Kc = [AB]² / ([A₂][B₂])
    B1 correct expression
  2. (b) Let x mol of A₂ react: [AB] = 2x and [A₂] = [B₂] = (1 − x). Then (2x)²/(1 − x)² = 9.00, so 2x/(1 − x) = 3, x = 0.600 and n(AB) = 1.20 mol.
    M1 define x and write equilibrium amounts (AB = 2x, A₂ = B₂ = 1 − x)M1 (2x)²/(1 − x)² = 9.00 and take the square rootA1 x = 0.600, so n(AB) = 1.20 mol
Q9Kc & Kp calculations · Standard · Paper 4 style · 4 marks

Substance A dimerises reversibly: 2A(g) ⇌ B(g). At 350 K the equilibrium concentrations are [A] = 0.0200 mol dm⁻³ and [B] = 0.300 mol dm⁻³.

  1. (a) Calculate Kc, giving its units. [2]
  2. (b) The temperature is raised and Kc falls to 300 mol⁻¹ dm³. Deduce the sign of ΔH for the forward reaction and give a reason. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Kc = 750 mol⁻¹ dm³
    M1 Kc = [B]/[A]² = 0.300 / 0.0200²A1 = 750; units (mol dm⁻³)^(1−2) = mol⁻¹ dm³
  2. (b) ΔH is negative (the forward reaction is exothermic). A higher temperature lowers Kc, so the equilibrium shifts left towards the endothermic backward reaction.
    B1 ΔH negative (exothermic)B1 reason: higher temperature shifts the equilibrium to the endothermic (backward) side, lowering Kc

  1. (a) Calculate the amounts of nitrogen and hydrogen that were present at the start. [2]
  2. (b) Calculate the partial pressure of each gas at equilibrium. [2]
  3. (c) Calculate Kp and give its units. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) N₂ 3.20 mol; H₂ 9.60 mol
    M1 1.60 mol NH₃ formed needs 0.80 mol N₂ and 2.40 mol H₂A1 both
  2. (b) p(N₂) = 4.29 × 10³ kPa; p(H₂) = 1.29 × 10⁴ kPa; p(NH₃) = 2.86 × 10³ kPa
    M1 mole fractions (total 11.2 mol)A1 all three
