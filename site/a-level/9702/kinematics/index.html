  2. (b) Calculate the minimum horizontal speed needed for the ball to clear the net. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) Show that the ball hits the net: time to net = 9.00/12.0 = 0.750 s; height at net = 3.00 − ½ × 9.81 × 0.750² = 0.24 m, which is below the top of the net (2.43 m).

Show answer
  1. (a) 3.77 s
    uᵧ = 28 sin 40° = 18.0 m s⁻¹ (B1); −1.8 = 18.0t − 4.905t² (M1); positive root t = 3.77 s (A1).

  1. (a) Show that the equation of the path is y = x tan θ − gx²/(2u² cos² θ), where y is the height above the release point after a horizontal distance x. [2]
  2. (b) Calculate the launch speed needed for the ball to pass through the centre of the hoop. [3]
  3. (c) Calculate the time the ball takes to reach the hoop. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = (u cos θ)t, y = (u sin θ)t − ½gt²; eliminate t.
    M1: t = x/(u cos θ); A1: substitute into y = u sin θ t − ½gt² to give the result.
  2. (b) 8.37 m s⁻¹
    y = 1.05 m, x = 6.0 m, θ = 50° (M1); 1.05 = 7.151 − 9.81×36/(2u² × 0.4132) (M1); u = 8.37 m s⁻¹ (A1).

  2. (b) Calculate the speed of the package just before it lands and the angle its velocity makes with the horizontal. [3]

  1. (a) 424 m
