Q1Horizontal circles · Core · Paper 3 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) A particle of mass 0.3 kg is attached to one end of a light inextensible string of length 0.8 m. The other end is fixed to a point O on a smooth horizontal table. The particle moves on the table in a horizontal circle with centre O at a constant speed of 4 m s⁻¹. Find the tension in the string. [2]
  2. (b) Find the angular speed of the particle and the time it takes to complete one revolution, giving the time correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 6 N
    M1: T = mv²/r = 0.3 × 16/0.8.A1: T = 6 N.
  2. (b) ω = 5 rad s⁻¹; period 1.26 s
    B1: ω = v/r = 4/0.8 = 5 rad s⁻¹.B1: period = 2π/5 = 1.2566… ≈ 1.26 s.
Q2Horizontal circles · Core · Paper 3 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Take g = 10 m s⁻². A conical pendulum consists of a small bob of mass 0.2 kg on a light inextensible string of length 1.25 m, whose upper end is fixed at a point A. The bob moves in a horizontal circle below A with the string inclined at angle θ to the vertical, where cos θ = 0.8. Find the tension in the string. [2]
  2. (b) Find the angular speed of the bob. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) 2.5 N
    M1: resolve vertically: T cos θ = mg.A1: T = 2/0.8 = 2.5 N.
  2. (b) √10 ≈ 3.16 rad s⁻¹
    B1: radius r = 1.25 × 0.6 = 0.75 m.M1: T sin θ = mω²r: 2.5 × 0.6 = 0.2 × ω² × 0.75.A1: ω² = 10, ω = 3.16 rad s⁻¹.
Q3Horizontal circles · Core · Paper 3 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Take g = 10 m s⁻². A car is driven round a horizontal roundabout in Lahore on a circular path of radius 40 m. The coefficient of friction between the tyres and the road is 0.6. Find the greatest speed at which the car can travel without slipping, correct to 3 significant figures. [3]
  2. (b) State, with a reason, whether the answer to part (a) depends on the mass of the car. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) 15.5 m s⁻¹
    M1: friction provides the centripetal force: F = mv²/r.M1: F ≤ μR = 0.6mg.A1: v² ≤ 0.6 × 10 × 40 = 240, v ≤ 15.5 m s⁻¹.
  2. (b) No: m cancels from mv²/r ≤ μmg.
    B1: mass cancels.

Q16Horizontal circles · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 7 marks

Show answer
  1. (a) T(AP) = 6.1 N, T(BP) = 1.1 N
    B1: P is 0.4 m below A; radius = 0.3 m; each string makes angle θ with the vertical where cos θ = 0.8, sin θ = 0.6.M1: vertically: 0.8T_A − 0.8T_B = 0.4g ⇒ T_A − T_B = 5.M1: horizontally: 0.6(T_A + T_B) = 0.4 × 36 × 0.3 = 4.32 ⇒ T_A + T_B = 7.2.A1: T_A = 6.1 N.A1: T_B = 1.1 N.

    M1: T_B = 0 ⇒ T_A = 5, 0.6 × 5 = 0.4ω² × 0.3.A1: ω² = 25, ω = 5 rad s⁻¹.

    M1: v² = 2g × 1.2.A1: v = √24 = 4.90 m s⁻¹.

  1. (a) Take g = 10 m s⁻². A particle P of mass m is attached to a light inextensible string of length a, whose other end is fixed at O. P moves in a complete vertical circle. Let φ be the angle OP makes with the upward vertical, and let the speed of P be u at the lowest point. Show that the tension in the string is T = mu²/a − 2mg − 3mg cos φ. [6]
Show answer
  1. (a) T = mu²/a − 2mg − 3mg cos φ (shown)
    M1 energy: v² = u² − 2ga(1 + cos φ); M1 radial towards O: T + mg cos φ = mv²/a; M1 T = m[u² − 2ga(1 + cos φ)]/a − mg cos φ; M1 simplify to T = mu²/a − 2mg − 3mg cos φ; A1 check: at the top (φ = 0) T = mu²/a − 5mg, at the bottom (φ = 180°) T = mu²/a + mg.
Q59Vertical circles (energy & reaction) · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) Take g = 10 m s⁻². A particle of mass 0.3 kg is attached to a light inextensible string of length 0.5 m whose other end is fixed at O. The particle moves in a vertical circle, and the tension in the string at the lowest point is six times the weight of the particle. Show that the speed at the lowest point is √(5ga), and show that the tension at the highest point is zero. [6]
Show answer
  1. (a) u² = 5ga (shown); T = 0 at the top (the string is just taut there)
    M1 radial at lowest point: T − mg = mu²/a; M1 T = 6mg gives u² = 5ga (shown); M1 energy: v_top² = u² − 4ga = ga; M1 radial at top: T + mg = mv²/a = mg, so T = 0 (shown); A1 the particle just completes the circle with the string just taut.
Q60Vertical circles (energy & reaction) · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) Take g = 10 m s⁻². A particle of mass m is attached to a light inextensible string of length a, fixed at O, and moves in a vertical circle. The particle is projected horizontally from the lowest point with speed u, where u² = 2ga. Show that the string becomes slack only when P is at the same level as O, and state the speed of P at that instant. [6]
Show answer
  1. (a) Slack exactly at the level of O (where T = 0); the speed there is 0
    M1 use T = mu²/a − 2mg − 3mg cos φ with u² = 2ga; M1 T = 2mg − 2mg − 3mg cos φ = −3mg cos φ; M1 for P above O (cos φ > 0) the tension would be negative, so the string is slack before the top; M1 for P below O (cos φ < 0) the tension is positive; M1 so the string is taut until P reaches O's level, where T = 0; A1 energy gives v² = u² − 2ga(1 + 0) = 0 at O's level, so the speed is 0.
