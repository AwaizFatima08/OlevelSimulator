The draft profits, before any corrections, were: 2025 $38,400; 2026 $41,250.

  1. (a) Calculate the corrected profit for the year ended 31 December 2025. [1]
  2. (b) Calculate the corrected profit for the year ended 31 December 2026. [3]
  3. (c) Calculate the overall effect of all the corrections on Dua's capital at 31 December 2026. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) $35,700
    38,400 − 2,700 B1
  2. (b) $42,800
    41,250 + 2,700 (opening inventory overstated, so cost of sales too high) M1 − 1,150 (write-down of shoes) M1 = $42,800 A1
  3. (c) Decrease of $1,150
    −2,700 + 2,700 − 1,150 M1; capital falls by $1,150 A1
Q75Inventory valuation (NRV) · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 7 marks

Rafiq runs a furniture showroom in Hyderabad and sells at cost plus 25%. His inventory count at 30 June 2026 gave a figure of $73,500 at selling price, because the assistant recorded the price labels. Further information:• Included in the count is a dining set with a price label of $4,000. It has a scratched table top and will be sold for $2,600 after a repair costing $300.• A sofa with a price label of $1,500 was sold and paid for on 29 June 2026 but was still in the showroom waiting for delivery; it was included in the count. The sale was recorded.• A wardrobe costing $1,200 was out on loan to a customer for approval (sale or return) and was not included in the count.

  1. (a) Calculate the value of inventory at cost before the dining set is considered, after removing the sofa and adding the wardrobe. [3]
  2. (b) Calculate the final value of inventory at 30 June 2026. [3]
  3. (c) Explain why the sofa is excluded although it is still in the showroom. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) $58,800
    (73,500 − 1,500) ÷ 1.25 = 57,600 M1 M1; + 1,200 = 58,800 A1
  2. (b) $57,900
    Dining set cost 4,000 ÷ 1.25 = 3,200; NRV 2,300 M1; write-down 900 M1; $57,900 A1
  3. (c) Ownership has passed to the customer and the sale has been recorded, so it is no longer Rafiq's inventory.
    B1
