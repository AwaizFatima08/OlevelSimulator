Q1Admission, retirement and goodwill · Core · Paper 1 style · 1 mark
  1. (a) The partners of Hilltop Traders, Quetta, agree to value goodwill at two years' purchase of the average profit of the last three years. Profits were: year 1 $84,000; year 2 $96,000; year 3 $105,000.What is the value of goodwill? [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) B $190,000
    Average profit = (84,000 + 96,000 + 105,000) ÷ 3 = $95,000; × 2 years = $190,000. A is the average only; C uses the latest year; D is the total of three years.
Q2Admission, retirement and goodwill · Core · Paper 2 style · 5 marks

Jabbar and Sons, a partnership of opticians in Peshawar, made these profits:Year Profit $ Weight2023 72,000 12024 81,000 22025 90,000 3The partners agree to value goodwill at three years' purchase of the weighted average profit.

Q4Admission, retirement and goodwill · Core · Paper 2 style · 7 marks

Kamran and Laila run a bakery in Islamabad, sharing profits equally. On 1 April 2026 Mehwish is admitted for a one-quarter share of profits; Kamran and Laila will share the remaining three-quarters equally. Goodwill is valued at $72,000 and is not to be recorded in the books.

  1. (a) Explain why an adjustment for goodwill is needed when a new partner is admitted. [2]
  2. (b) Calculate the net amount credited or debited to each partner's capital account for goodwill. [3]

Show answer
  1. (a) The old partners built up the goodwill; without an adjustment the new partner would share in the value they created, so the old partners must be compensated for the share they give up.

    New ratio K:L:M = 3/8 : 3/8 : 2/8. Kamran: +36,000 created − 27,000 written off = +9,000 (B1). Laila: same, +9,000 (B1). Mehwish: −72,000 × 1/4 = −18,000 (B1).
  3. (c) Dr $ Cr $Capital – Mehwish 18,000Capital – Kamran 9,000Capital – Laila 9,000
    Dr Mehwish 18,000 (1); Cr Kamran 9,000 and Laila 9,000 (1).

Q30Partnership Act and agreements · Standard · Paper 2 style · 6 marks

Shoaib and Tahira formed a partnership without any agreement. Shoaib contributed capital of $60,000 and also lent the business $20,000 at the start of the year. Tahira contributed capital of $40,000 and works full time in the business; Shoaib works part time. The profit for the year before any interest on Shoaib's loan was $50,000. Shoaib claims profits should be shared in the ratio of capitals.

  1. (a) Calculate each partner's share of the profit as required by the Partnership Act 1890. [3]
  2. (b) Calculate Shoaib's total income from the business for the year. [1]
  3. (c) Explain whether Shoaib's claim is valid. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Shoaib $24,500; Tahira $24,500.
    Loan interest 5% × 20,000 = 1,000 is an expense (M1); profit 49,000 (A1) shared equally 24,500 each (A1).
  2. (b) $25,500
    24,500 + 1,000 = 25,500 (B1).
  3. (c) No. Without an agreement the Act requires equal sharing of profits, whatever the capital contributed.
    1 mark: claim not valid. 1 mark: the Act applies when there is no agreement – equal shares, no interest on capital.
