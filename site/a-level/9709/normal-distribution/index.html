Q1Normal probabilities · Core · Paper 5 style · 3 marks
  1. (a) The random variable X has the distribution N(50, 4²). Find P(X < 56). [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) 0.933
    M1 standardise: (56 − 50)/4 = 1.5M1 Φ(1.5)A1 0.933
Q2Normal probabilities · Core · Paper 5 style · 3 marks
  1. (a) The masses of Sindhri mangoes from an orchard are normally distributed with mean 320 g and standard deviation 25 g. Find the probability that a randomly chosen mango has a mass greater than 350 g. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) 0.115
    M1 z = (350 − 320)/25 = 1.2M1 1 − Φ(1.2)A1 0.115
Q3Normal probabilities · Core · Paper 5 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) The time taken by Usman to drive to work is normally distributed with mean 42 minutes and standard deviation 6 minutes. Find the probability that on a randomly chosen day his journey takes between 35 and 50 minutes. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) 0.787
    M1 standardise both: 1.333 and −1.167M1 Φ(1.333) − Φ(−1.167) = Φ(1.333) + Φ(1.167) − 1A1 0.9088 − 0.1216A1 0.787
Q4Normal probabilities · Core · Paper 5 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) A machine fills bags of rice. The mass of rice in a bag is normally distributed with mean 1005 g and standard deviation 3.5 g. Find the probability that a randomly chosen bag contains less than 1000 g. [3]
  2. (b) In a batch of 500 bags, find the expected number that contain less than 1000 g. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) 0.0766
    M1 z = (1000 − 1005)/3.5 = −1.429M1 1 − Φ(1.429)A1 0.0766
  2. (b) 38.3 (about 38)
    B1 500 × 0.0766 = 38.3
Q5Normal probabilities · Standard · Paper 5 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) The heights of 300 students at a college in Peshawar are modelled by a normal distribution with mean 165 cm and standard deviation 8 cm. Estimate the number of students who are taller than 175 cm. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) 32 students
    M1 z = 1.25M1 1 − Φ(1.25) = 0.106M1 300 × 0.106 = 31.7A1 32
Q6Normal probabilities · Standard · Paper 5 style · 3 marks
  1. (a) The lifetimes of a type of battery are normally distributed with mean 480 hours and standard deviation 35 hours. Find the lifetime that is exceeded by 90% of batteries. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) 435.1 hours
    M1 Φ⁻¹(0.9) = 1.282M1 (t − 480)/35 = −1.282A1 t = 435.1 hours
Q7Normal probabilities · Standard · Paper 5 style · 3 marks

Show answer
  1. (a) 0.866
    M1 standardise: P(−1.5 < Z < 1.5)M1 2Φ(1.5) − 1A1 0.866
Q8Normal probabilities · Standard · Paper 5 style · 3 marks
  1. (a) The speeds of deliveries bowled by a fast bowler are normally distributed with mean 132 km/h and standard deviation 5.5 km/h. Find the value of k such that 20% of his deliveries are faster than k km/h. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) k = 136.6
    M1 z = 0.842M1 (k − 132)/5.5 = 0.842A1 k = 136.6
Q9Normal probabilities · Standard · Paper 5 style · 5 marks

  2. (b) Three customers are chosen at random. Find the probability that at least one of them waits more than 9 minutes. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 0.106
    M1 z = 1.25M1 1 − Φ(1.25)A1 0.106
  2. (b) 0.285
    M1 1 − (1 − 0.106)³A1 0.285
Q10Normal probabilities · Challenge · Paper 5 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) The lengths of bolts made by a machine are normally distributed with mean 40 mm and standard deviation 0.6 mm. A bolt is acceptable if its length is between 39 mm and 41.2 mm. Find the probability that a randomly chosen bolt is acceptable. Give your answer correct to 4 significant figures. [4]
  2. (b) Bolts are packed in boxes of 10. Find the probability that a box contains at most one bolt that is not acceptable. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) 0.9295
    M1 z values −1.667 and 2M1 Φ(2) − Φ(−1.667) = Φ(2) + Φ(1.667) − 1A1 0.9772 + 0.9522 − 1A1 0.9295
  2. (b) 0.846 (accept 0.847 from p = 0.9295)
    M1 binomial with p = 0.9295M1 p¹⁰ + 10p⁹(1 − p)A1 0.846 (0.847 if the rounded p = 0.9295 is used)
Q11Normal probabilities · Challenge · Paper 5 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) The scores in an aptitude test are normally distributed with mean 70 and standard deviation 10. A candidate is known to have scored more than 75. Find the probability that the candidate scored more than 85. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) 0.217
    M1 P(X > 85 | X > 75) = P(X > 85)/P(X > 75)M1 1 − Φ(1.5) = 0.0668M1 1 − Φ(0.5) = 0.3085A1 0.217
Q12Normal probabilities · Challenge · Paper 5 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) A fruit seller has a large crate of apples, 60% of variety A and 40% of variety B. The masses of variety A apples are distributed N(150, 12²) grams and those of variety B are distributed N(165, 15²) grams. An apple is chosen at random. Find the probability that its mass is more than 160 g. [4]
  2. (b) Given that the apple has a mass of more than 160 g, find the probability that it is variety A. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 0.374
    M1 P(A > 160) = 1 − Φ(0.833) = 0.2023M1 P(B > 160) = Φ(0.333) = 0.6306M1 0.6 × 0.2023 + 0.4 × 0.6306A1 0.374
  2. (b) 0.325
    M1 0.6 × 0.2023 ÷ 0.3736A1 0.325

Q16Normal approximation to binomial · Standard · Paper 5 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) A survey suggests that 60% of commuters in Karachi travel to work by bus. A random sample of 50 commuters is taken. Show that a normal approximation is appropriate. [1]
  2. (b) Use the normal approximation to find the probability that between 25 and 35 (inclusive) of the commuters travel by bus. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) np = 30 > 5 and nq = 20 > 5
    B1 both np and nq calculated and compared with 5
  2. (b) 0.888
    B1 mean 30, variance 12M1 continuity corrections 24.5 and 35.5M1 z = ±1.588M1 2Φ(1.588) − 1A1 0.888

Q18Normal approximation to binomial · Standard · Paper 5 style · 5 marks

  1. (a) 0.0890

    B1 sensible comparison, e.g. close because np and nq are large

Q20Normal approximation to binomial · Standard · Paper 5 style · 6 marks

Q22Normal approximation to binomial · Challenge · Paper 5 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) The daily demand for naan at a bakery in Rawalpindi is normally distributed with mean 380 and standard deviation 40. The bakery makes 420 naan each day. Find the probability that on a randomly chosen day the demand is at least 420, so that the bakery sells out. Give your answer to 4 decimal places. [2]
  2. (b) Use a suitable approximation to find the probability that the bakery sells out on at least 40 days out of 200. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) 0.1587
    M1 z = (420 − 380)/40 = 1A1 1 − Φ(1) = 0.1587
  2. (b) 0.0666 (accept 0.0663 from unrounded p)
    B1 X ~ B(200, 0.1587): mean 31.74, variance 26.70M1 continuity correction 39.5M1 z = (39.5 − 31.74)/5.167 = 1.502M1 1 − Φ(1.502)A1 0.0666 (0.0663 with unrounded p = 0.15866)

Q26Finding mean/sd from probabilities · Standard · Paper 5 style · 5 marks

Q27Finding mean/sd from probabilities · Standard · Paper 5 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) The masses of watermelons sold at a market are normally distributed. The mean mass is four times the standard deviation, and 6.68% of the watermelons have a mass less than 2.5 kg. Find the mean and standard deviation. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) μ = 4 kg, σ = 1 kg
    B1 z = −1.5M1 (2.5 − 4σ)/σ = −1.5A1 σ = 1A1 μ = 4
Q28Finding mean/sd from probabilities · Standard · Paper 5 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) The times taken by candidates to complete an online test are normally distributed with standard deviation 8 minutes. 20% of candidates take more than 75 minutes. Find the mean time. [3]
  2. (b) Find the percentage of candidates who take less than 60 minutes. [2]
