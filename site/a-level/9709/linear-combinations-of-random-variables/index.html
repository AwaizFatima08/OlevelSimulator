Q1Linear combinations of normals · Core · Paper 6 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) The independent random variables X and Y have distributions N(20, 9) and N(15, 16) respectively. State the distribution of X + Y. [2]
  2. (b) Find P(X + Y > 40). [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) N(35, 25)
    B1 mean 35B1 variance 9 + 16 = 25
  2. (b) 0.159
    M1 z = (40 − 35)/5 = 1A1 1 − Φ(1) = 0.159
Q2Linear combinations of normals · Core · Paper 6 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) The independent random variables X and Y have distributions N(30, 5²) and N(24, 3²). Find P(X < Y). [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) 0.152
    M1 X − Y has mean 6M1 variance 25 + 9 = 34M1 P(X − Y < 0) = Φ(−6/√34) = 1 − Φ(1.029)A1 0.152

Show answer

    B1 E = 30 − 12 = 18M1 Var = 9 × 4 + 4 × 2.5A1 46

Q6Linear combinations of normals · Core · Paper 6 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) The random variable X has the distribution N(12, 2²). X₁, X₂, …, X₅ are five independent observations of X. Find P(X₁ + X₂ + X₃ + X₄ + X₅ > 65). [3]
  2. (b) Find P(5X > 65) and explain why this differs from your answer to (a). [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) 0.132
    B1 sum ~ N(60, 20)M1 z = 5/√20 = 1.118A1 0.132
  2. (b) 0.309; 5X has variance 100, not 20
    B1 5X ~ N(60, 100)B1 1 − Φ(0.5) = 0.309B1 5X multiplies one observation, so variance is 25 × 4; the sum of five independent values has variance 5 × 4

Q30Linear combinations of normals · Challenge · Paper 6 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) Floor tiles have lengths distributed N(30, 0.4²) cm. A row of 10 tiles is laid with a fixed gap of 0.3 cm between neighbouring tiles. The row must fit along a wall of length 303.5 cm. Find the probability that a row of 10 randomly chosen tiles, with gaps, fits. [5]
  2. (b) Explain why it would be wrong to use 10X, where X is the length of one tile, for the total length of the tiles. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) 0.736
    B1 9 gaps: 2.7 cmB1 mean 300 + 2.7 = 302.7B1 variance 10 × 0.16 = 1.6M1 Φ(0.8/√1.6) = Φ(0.632)A1 0.736
  2. (b) 10X assumes all ten tiles have the same length; the tiles vary independently, so the variance is 10 × 0.16, not 100 × 0.16.
    B1 independent tiles: add variances, not multiply one tile
