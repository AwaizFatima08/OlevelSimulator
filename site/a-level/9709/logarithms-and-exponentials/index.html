Q1Linearisation (ln graphs) · Core · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) The variables x and y satisfy y = Kxⁿ, where K and n are constants. The graph of ln y against ln x is a straight line passing through the points (0.5, 2.1) and (2.5, 0.9). Find the values of n and K, giving K correct to 3 significant figures. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) n = −0.6, K = 11.0
    B1 ln y = ln K + n ln x; M1 gradient (0.9 − 2.1)/(2.5 − 0.5) = −0.6; M1 ln K = 2.1 + 0.6 × 0.5 = 2.4; A1 K = e^{2.4} = 11.0.
Q2Linearisation (ln graphs) · Core · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) The variables x and y satisfy y = A × bˣ, where A and b are constants. The graph of ln y against x is a straight line through (1, 3.2) and (4, 5.0). Find the values of A and b, correct to 3 significant figures. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) A = 13.5, b = 1.82
    B1 ln y = ln A + x ln b; M1 gradient 1.8/3 = 0.6 = ln b; M1 intercept ln A = 3.2 − 0.6 = 2.6; A1 A = 13.5, b = 1.82.
Q3Linearisation (ln graphs) · Core · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) The variables x and y satisfy y = axᵇ. The graph of ln y against ln x is a straight line with gradient 2.5 that crosses the vertical axis at 1.6. Find the values of a and b. [2]
  2. (b) Find the value of y when x = 3, correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) a = 4.95, b = 2.5
    B1 b = 2.5; B1 a = e^{1.6} = 4.95.
  2. (b) 77.2
    M1 4.953 × 3^{2.5}; A1 77.2.
Q4Linearisation (ln graphs) · Core · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) For the relationship y = A bˣ, state which graph should be drawn to give a straight line, and what its gradient represents. [2]
  2. (b) The line obtained has equation ln y = 0.92 + 0.35x. Find A and b, correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) ln y against x; gradient ln b
    B1 ln y against x; B1 gradient = ln b (intercept ln A).
  2. (b) A = 2.51, b = 1.42
    B1 A = e^{0.92} = 2.51; B1 b = e^{0.35} = 1.42.
Q5Linearisation (ln graphs) · Standard · Paper 3 style · 8 marks

A laboratory culture of bacteria is counted every 2 hours. The number N of bacteria after t hours is modelled by N = N₀e^{kt}.t (hours): 0, 2, 4, 6, 8N: 250, 503, 1014, 2042, 4111

  1. (a) Show that the model N = N₀e^{kt} gives a straight line when ln N is plotted against t, and state what the gradient and intercept represent. [2]
  2. (b) Calculate the values of ln N for the table, correct to 2 decimal places, and use them to estimate k and N₀. [4]
  3. (c) Use the model to estimate the number of bacteria after 10 hours, correct to 2 significant figures, and comment on the reliability of this estimate. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) ln N = ln N₀ + kt; gradient k, intercept ln N₀
    M1 take ln of both sides; A1 gradient k, vertical intercept ln N₀.
  2. (b) ln N: 5.52, 6.22, 6.92, 7.62, 8.32; k ≈ 0.35, N₀ ≈ 250
    B1 ln N values; M1 gradient (8.32 − 5.52)/8; A1 k = 0.35; A1 N₀ = e^{5.52} ≈ 250.
  3. (c) About 8300; it is an extrapolation beyond the data, so less reliable
    B1 250e^{3.5} ≈ 8300; B1 extrapolation comment.
Q6Linearisation (ln graphs) · Standard · Paper 2 style · 6 marks

A student in Lahore times a simple pendulum. The period T seconds and the length L metres are thought to satisfy T = kLⁿ. The graph of ln T against ln L is a straight line passing through the points (−1.20, 0.096) and (0.40, 0.896).

  1. (a) Find the values of n and k, giving k correct to 3 significant figures. [4]
  2. (b) Use your values to find the period of a pendulum of length 0.75 m, correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) n = 0.5, k = 2.01
    B1 ln T = ln k + n ln L; M1 gradient 0.8/1.6 = 0.5; M1 ln k = 0.096 + 0.5 × 1.20 = 0.696; A1 k = e^{0.696} = 2.01.
  2. (b) 1.74 s
    M1 T = 2.006 × 0.75^{0.5}; A1 1.74 s.
Q7Linearisation (ln graphs) · Standard · Paper 2 style · 7 marks

A cup of tea cools in a room at 20 °C. Its temperature θ °C after t minutes is modelled by θ − 20 = Ae^{−kt}. Readings: t = 0, θ = 80.0; t = 5, θ = 60.2; t = 10, θ = 47.0; t = 15, θ = 38.07.

  1. (a) Show that the model gives a straight line when ln(θ − 20) is plotted against t, stating the gradient and intercept in terms of A and k. [2]
  2. (b) Use the readings at t = 0 and t = 15 to find the values of A and k, correct to 2 significant figures where necessary. [3]
  3. (c) Find the time taken for the tea to cool to 30 °C, correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) ln(θ − 20) = ln A − kt; gradient −k, intercept ln A
    M1 take logs; A1.
  2. (b) A = 60, k = 0.080
    M1 ln 60 = 4.094 and ln 18.07 = 2.894; M1 gradient −1.2/15; A1 k = 0.080, A = 60.
  3. (c) 22.4 minutes
    M1 60e^{−0.08t} = 10; A1 t = ln 6 ÷ 0.08 = 22.4.
Q8Linearisation (ln graphs) · Standard · Paper 2 style · 7 marks

The population P million of a large city is modelled by P = a × bᵗ, where t is the number of years after the start of 2010. The graph of ln P against t is a straight line through (2, 2.65) and (10, 2.97).

  1. (a) Find the values of a and b, correct to 3 significant figures. [4]
  2. (b) Use the model to find the value of t when the population first reaches 25 million, and state the calendar year in which this happens. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) a = 13.1, b = 1.04
    B1 ln P = ln a + t ln b; M1 gradient 0.32/8 = 0.04; A1 b = e^{0.04} = 1.04; A1 a = e^{2.57} = 13.1.
  2. (b) t = 16.2, during 2026
    M1 ln 25 = 2.57 + 0.04t; A1 t = 16.2; A1 2010 + 16.2 → 2026.
Q9Linearisation (ln graphs) · Standard · Paper 3 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) The variables x and y satisfy y = Ae^{−kx²}. Explain why the graph of ln y against x² is a straight line. [1]
  2. (b) This line passes through (1.0, 2.20) and (4.0, 1.30), where the horizontal axis shows x². Find A and k. [3]
  3. (c) Find y when x = 2. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) ln y = ln A − kx², which is linear in x²
    B1.
  2. (b) k = 0.3, A = 12.2
    M1 gradient −0.9/3 = −0.3; A1 k = 0.3; A1 ln A = 2.5, A = 12.2.
  3. (c) 3.67
    M1 12.18e^{−1.2}; A1 3.67.
Q10Linearisation (ln graphs) · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) The variables x and y satisfy the equation y² = A bˣ, where A and b are constants. The graph of ln y against x is a straight line passing through (2, 1.45) and (5, 0.70). Find the values of A and b correct to 3 significant figures. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) A = 49.4, b = 0.607
    B1 2 ln y = ln A + x ln b, so ln y = ½ ln A + (½ ln b)x; M1 gradient −0.75/3 = −0.25 = ½ ln b; A1 b = e^{−0.5} = 0.607; M1 intercept 1.45 + 0.5 = 1.95 = ½ ln A; A1 A = e^{3.9} = 49.4.
Q11Linearisation (ln graphs) · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) The variables x and y satisfy y = Axⁿ. When x = 2, y = 15 and when x = 5, y = 60. By taking natural logarithms, find the values of n and A correct to 3 significant figures. [5]
  2. (b) Hence state the gradient and the intercept on the vertical axis of the graph of ln y against ln x. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) n = 1.51, A = 5.26
    M1 ln 15 = ln A + n ln 2 and ln 60 = ln A + n ln 5; M1 subtract: ln 4 = n ln 2.5; A1 n = 1.51; M1 substitute back; A1 A = 15 ÷ 2^{1.513} = 5.26.
  2. (b) Gradient 1.51, intercept 1.66
    B1 gradient n = 1.51; B1 intercept ln A = 1.66.

Q13Log & exponential equations · Core · Paper 2 style · 3 marks

Show answer
  1. (a) x = 3
    M1 log₂((x + 5)/(x − 1)) = 2; M1 (x + 5)/(x − 1) = 4; A1 x = 3.
Q14Log & exponential equations · Core · Paper 2 style · 2 marks
  1. (a) Solve the equation ln(3x − 2) = 2, giving x in exact form and correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = (e² + 2)/3 ≈ 3.13
    M1 3x − 2 = e²; A1 (e² + 2)/3 = 3.13.
Q15Log & exponential equations · Standard · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Solve the equation 2 ln x − ln(x + 6) = ln 2. Give your answer in exact form and correct to 3 significant figures. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 1 + √13 ≈ 4.61
    M1 ln(x²/(x + 6)) = ln 2; A1 x² − 2x − 12 = 0; M1 solve: x = 1 ± √13; A1 x = 1 + √13 (4.61) only, since ln x needs x > 0.
Q16Log & exponential equations · Standard · Paper 2 style · 3 marks
  1. (a) Solve the equation e^{2x} − 5eˣ + 6 = 0, giving your answers in exact form. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = ln 2 or x = ln 3
    M1 let u = eˣ: u² − 5u + 6 = 0; A1 u = 2, 3; A1 x = ln 2, ln 3.
Q17Log & exponential equations · Standard · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Solve the equation 5^{x+1} = 3^{2x−1}. Show that x = ln 15 / ln(9/5) and give x correct to 3 significant figures. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = ln 15 / ln 1.8 ≈ 4.61
    M1 (x + 1) ln 5 = (2x − 1) ln 3; M1 collect x terms: x(2 ln 3 − ln 5) = ln 5 + ln 3; A1 x = ln 15/ln(9/5); A1 4.61.
Q18Log & exponential equations · Standard · Paper 3 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Show that log₉ x = ½ log₃ x. [2]
  2. (b) Hence solve log₃ x + log₉ x = 6. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Shown
    M1 change of base log₉ x = log₃ x / log₃ 9; A1 log₃ 9 = 2.
  2. (b) x = 81
    M1 (3/2) log₃ x = 6; A1 log₃ x = 4, x = 81.
Q19Log & exponential equations · Standard · Paper 2 style · 6 marks

A sample of a radioactive substance used in a hospital has mass m grams after t years, where m = 40e^{−0.025t}.

  1. (a) Find the initial mass. [1]
  2. (b) Find the time taken for the mass to fall to 15 g, correct to 3 significant figures. [3]
  3. (c) Find the half-life of the substance, correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 40 g
    B1 t = 0 gives m = 40.
  2. (b) 39.2 years
    M1 e^{−0.025t} = 15/40; M1 −0.025t = ln 0.375; A1 t = 39.2.
  3. (c) 27.7 years
    M1 e^{−0.025t} = ½; A1 t = ln 2/0.025 = 27.7.
Q20Log & exponential equations · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Solve the equation 2^{2x+1} − 7(2ˣ) + 3 = 0, giving any non-integer answer correct to 3 significant figures. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = −1 or x = 1.58
    B1 2^{2x+1} = 2(2ˣ)²; M1 2u² − 7u + 3 = 0; A1 u = 3 or ½; M1 2ˣ = 3 gives x = ln 3/ln 2; A1 x = 1.58 and x = −1.
Q21Log & exponential equations · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Solve the simultaneous equations log₂ x + log₂ y = 5 and x + y = 12. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 4, y = 8 or x = 8, y = 4
    M1 log₂(xy) = 5 so xy = 32; M1 substitute y = 12 − x: x² − 12x + 32 = 0; A1 x = 4 or 8; A1 y = 8 or 4; B1 both pairs give positive x, y so both valid.
Q22Log & exponential equations · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Solve the equation 3eˣ + 4e^{−x} = 8, giving your answers in exact form. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = ln 2 or x = ln(2/3)
    M1 multiply by eˣ: 3u² − 8u + 4 = 0 where u = eˣ; A1 (3u − 2)(u − 2) = 0; A1 u = 2 or 2/3; A1 x = ln 2, ln(2/3).
Q23Log & exponential equations/inequalities · Core · Paper 2 style · 3 marks
  1. (a) Find the smallest integer n such that 1.08ⁿ > 3. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) n = 15
    M1 n ln 1.08 > ln 3; A1 n > 14.27; A1 n = 15.
Q24Log & exponential equations/inequalities · Core · Paper 2 style · 3 marks
  1. (a) Solve the inequality 0.6ˣ < 0.05, giving the boundary value correct to 3 significant figures. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) x > 5.86
    M1 x ln 0.6 < ln 0.05; M1 divide by ln 0.6 (negative) and reverse the inequality; A1 x > 5.86.
Q25Log & exponential equations/inequalities · Standard · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Solve the inequality 4ˣ < 3^{x+2}, giving the boundary value correct to 3 significant figures. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) x < 7.64
    M1 x ln 4 < (x + 2) ln 3; M1 x(ln 4 − ln 3) < 2 ln 3; A1 ln 4 − ln 3 > 0 so inequality unchanged; A1 x < 7.64.
Q26Log & exponential equations/inequalities · Standard · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Solve the equation |2ˣ − 6| = 3, giving your answers correct to 3 significant figures. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 1.58 or x = 3.17
    M1 2ˣ − 6 = 3 or 2ˣ − 6 = −3; A1 2ˣ = 9 or 3; M1 x = ln 9/ln 2; A1 3.17 and 1.58.
Q27Log & exponential equations/inequalities · Standard · Paper 2 style · 4 marks

Ayesha invests Rs 200000 in a savings account in Karachi that pays 9% compound interest per year.

  1. (a) Show that the value after n years is 200000 × 1.09ⁿ rupees. [1]
  2. (b) Find the least number of complete years after which the value of the account is more than Rs 500000. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) Shown
    B1 multiply by 1.09 each year.
  2. (b) 11 years
    M1 1.09ⁿ > 2.5; M1 n > ln 2.5/ln 1.09 = 10.6; A1 11.
Q28Log & exponential equations/inequalities · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Solve the inequality |3ˣ − 10| < 5, giving the boundary values correct to 3 significant figures. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) 1.46 < x < 2.46
    M1 −5 < 3ˣ − 10 < 5; A1 5 < 3ˣ < 15; M1 take logs (base 3 or ln); A1 1.46 < x < 2.46.
Q29Log & exponential equations/inequalities · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Solve the inequality ln(2x + 1) > 1 + ln(x − 1), giving the boundary value in exact form and correct to 3 significant figures. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) 1 < x < (e + 1)/(e − 2), i.e. 1 < x < 5.18
    B1 domain x > 1; M1 ln((2x + 1)/(x − 1)) > 1, so 2x + 1 > e(x − 1) (x − 1 > 0); M1 x(2 − e) > −(e + 1); A1 divide by negative 2 − e and reverse: x < (e + 1)/(e − 2); A1 1 < x < 5.18.
Q30Log & exponential equations/inequalities · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Solve the inequality 2|eˣ − 3| < eˣ, giving the boundary values in exact form. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) ln 2 < x < ln 6
    M1 consider eˣ > 3: 2eˣ − 6 < eˣ, so eˣ < 6; M1 consider eˣ < 3: 6 − 2eˣ < eˣ, so eˣ > 2; A1 2 < eˣ < 6; M1 take ln; A1 ln 2 < x < ln 6.
