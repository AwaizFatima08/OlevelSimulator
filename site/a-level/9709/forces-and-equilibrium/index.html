  1. (a) Find the normal reaction and the frictional force acting on the box. [3]
  2. (b) Find the least possible value of the coefficient of friction. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) R = 45.3 N, F = 21.1 N up the slope
    M1 resolve perpendicular: R = 50 cos 25°; A1 45.3 N; B1 F = 50 sin 25° = 21.1 N.

Q4Friction & equilibrium on a slope · Core · Paper 4 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) A box of mass 4 kg rests in equilibrium on a rough plane inclined at 15° to the horizontal. Take g = 9.8 m s⁻². Find the normal reaction and the frictional force acting on the box. [3]
  2. (b) The coefficient of friction between the box and the plane is 0.3. Show that the box is in equilibrium. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) R = 37.9 N; F = 10.1 N (up the plane)
    Resolve perpendicular to plane: R = 4g cos 15° = 37.9 N. Resolve along plane: F = 4g sin 15° = 10.1 N. M1, M1, A1.
  2. (b) Limiting friction = 0.3 × 37.9 = 11.4 N > 10.1 N, so the box is in equilibrium
    The friction needed (10.1 N) is less than the greatest friction possible (11.4 N), so equilibrium is maintained. M1, A1.

Q9Friction & equilibrium on a slope · Standard · Paper 4 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) A block of mass 5 kg rests on a rough plane inclined at 25° to the horizontal, with coefficient of friction 0.2. A light rope, parallel to a line of greatest slope, pulls the block up the plane with tension T N. Take g = 9.8 m s⁻². Show that the block remains in equilibrium provided 11.8 ≤ T ≤ 29.6. [4]
  2. (b) State the frictional force on the block when T = 25 N, and state its direction. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Limits: T = mg sin 25° ± μmg cos 25° = 20.7 ± 8.9, so 11.8 ≤ T ≤ 29.6
    mg sin 25° = 20.71 N, μmg cos 25° = 0.2 × 44.41 = 8.88 N. Friction must act down the plane for T > 20.7 and up the plane for T < 20.7, with magnitude at most 8.89 N. M1 resolving, M1 friction limits, A1 both limits.
  2. (b) 4.3 N, acting down the plane
    With T = 25 N the block would tend to move up, so friction acts down the plane: F = T − mg sin 25° = 25 − 20.71 = 4.3 N. M1, A1.

Q15Friction & equilibrium on a slope · Challenge · Paper 4 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) A box of mass 10 kg rests on a rough plane inclined at 20° to the horizontal, with coefficient of friction 0.6. Take g = 9.8 m s⁻².   2. (b) Up the plane: 88.8 N; down the plane: 21.7 N
    Positive P is up the plane (P = 88.8 N); negative P is down the plane (P = −21.7 N, i.e. 21.7 N down). B1, B1.
