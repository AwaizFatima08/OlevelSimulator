Q1Formal letter · Core · Paper 2 style · 12 marks

Read Text 1 and Text 2, then answer the questions.Text 1: Post on the school noticeboard by a parentThe tuck shop sells crisps, fizzy drinks and sweets every single day. My son comes home with no appetite and a headache. The school teaches us about healthy eating in science lessons, so why does it sell junk food at break? Students will simply buy snacks from the vendor outside the gate, where nobody checks how clean the food is. Healthy items also cost more, and not every family can afford them.   1. (a) Before you write, identify the main ideas and opinions in Text 1 and Text 2. For each idea, note briefly how convincing you find it and why. [4]
  2. (b) Your principal is deciding whether the school tuck shop should sell only healthy food. You decide to write a letter to the principal giving your views.In your letter you should:• evaluate the ideas and opinions in both texts• give your own views, with reasons, about whether the tuck shop should sell only healthy food.Base your letter on what you have read in both texts, but use your own words.Begin your letter: 'Dear Principal,'Write 250 to 350 words. [8]
Show answer
  1. (a) Text 1: tuck shop sells unhealthy food daily – convincing, but based on one child's experience.Text 1: school is inconsistent (teaches healthy eating, sells junk) – strong point.Text 1: healthy food improves afternoon concentration – plausible but no evidence given.Text 1: other Lahore schools succeeded – vague, no names or results.Text 2: students will buy from the gate vendor – realistic and raises a hygiene risk.Text 2: healthy food costs more – fair, though some items (fruit, water) are cheap.Text 2: profits fund sports – relevant but suggests money matters more than health.Text 2: compromise of mixed choices – balanced.
    1 mark for each relevant idea correctly identified AND given a sensible comment on how convincing it is (up to 4; at least one idea from each text). An idea copied out with no comment earns no mark. Other valid ideas from the texts are also credited.
  2.
