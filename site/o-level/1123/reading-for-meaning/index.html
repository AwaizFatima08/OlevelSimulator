  5. (e) Paragraph 4: What did Sana buy on the way home, and why? [2]
Show answer

    1 mark. Accept 'before dawn, at five'.
  2. (b) Any two of: guavas, oranges, bananas.

  3. (c) Arrange oranges in a pyramid / with the brightest at the front; sprinkle water on the guavas (so they shone).

  1. (a) Paragraph 1: Identify two materials the boys used to build the oven. [1]
  2. (b) Paragraph 2: What reading did the thermometer show at ten o'clock? [1]

  5. (e) Paragraph 4: Give two improvements Rehan suggested. [2]
Show answer

    1 mark for any two. The thermometer is equipment, not a building material – do not accept on its own.
  2. (b) Sixty degrees.
    1 mark. Units not required.
  3. (c) The wind tore the plastic sheet off the top, so heat escaped.

  4. (d) Daniyal chased the plastic (past the canteen); Rehan held down the box.
    1 mark for each boy's action, correctly matched.
  5. (e) A glass lid; held down with clips.
    1 mark each. Accept 'a lid that stays in place / traps more heat' for the second only if clips or securing are mentioned.

Read Text A, then answer the questions. Paragraph numbers are shown in square brackets.Text A: The final game[1] The hall was hushed.   1. (a) Which of the following is closest in meaning to 'composed' as it is used in paragraph 1? [1]
  2. (b) Which of the following is closest in meaning to 'deliberate' as it is used in paragraph 1? [1]
  3. (c) Which of the following is closest in meaning to 'exposed' as it is used in paragraph 2? [1]
  4. (d) Which of the following is closest in meaning to 'resignation' as it is used in paragraph 3? [1]
  5. (e) Which of the following is closest in meaning to 'spontaneous' as it is used in paragraph 3? [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) B calm and self-controlled
    The champion had not lost all week and placed her pieces with decisive clicks – she was calm and in control. A and D are other meanings of 'composed' that do not describe a person; C is not supported by the text.
  2. (b) A careful and intentional
    Each move was placed carefully and on purpose. C is the verb sense ('to deliberate'), which does not fit 'her moves were'; B contradicts the careful placing.
  3. (c) B open to attack
    Sacrificing the knight would leave her king without protection, open to attack. A is a general meaning of 'exposed' that misses the danger; C describes a different situation – a king can be exposed without being cornered.

    In chess, offering a hand at this point is a sign of conceding the game. A is the workplace meaning; D is tempting because offering a hand is polite, but the gesture here means she accepted defeat.
  5. (e) A unplanned
    The spectators had been told to stay silent, so their applause was unplanned and unprompted. B may also be true of applause, but it is not the meaning of 'spontaneous'.
