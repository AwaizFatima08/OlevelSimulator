  1. (a) Show that (1 + sin x)/cos x ≡ cos x/(1 − sin x). [2]
  2. (b) Hence solve (1 + sin x)/cos x = 2 for 0° ≤ x < 360°. Give your answer correct to 1 decimal place where necessary. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) Multiply out: (1 + sin x)(1 − sin x) = 1 − sin²x = cos²x
    Cross-multiply and use 1 − sin²x = cos²x. M1, A1.
  2. (b) x = 36.9
    Rewrite as cos x/(1 − sin x) = 2, or work directly: 1 + sin x = 2 cos x. Squaring gives 5cos²x − 4cos x = 0 (after substitution sin² = 1 − cos²), so cos x = 0 (rejected, division by zero) or cos x = 0.8, giving sin x = 0.6 and x = 36.9°. Show answer
