Q58Equation of a circle · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) A family of circles has equation x² + y² − 2ax + 4y + a = 0, where a is a constant. Show that the centre of every circle lies on the line y = −2, and find the radius squared in terms of a. [4]
  2. (b) Find the value of a for which the radius of the circle is least, and give the least radius exactly. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) centre (a, −2); r² = a² − a + 4
    Completing the square: (x − a)² + (y + 2)² = a² + 4 − a. So the centre is (a, −2) for every a, which lies on y = −2. Radius squared = a² − a + 4. M1, M1, A1, A1.
