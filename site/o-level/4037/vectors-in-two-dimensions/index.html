Q1Velocity and position vectors · Core · Paper 2 style · 3 marks
  1. (a) A fishing boat leaves a jetty on Keenjhar Lake. At time t = 0 hours it is at the point with position vector (2i + 5j) km relative to the jetty, and it moves with constant velocity (4i − 3j) km/h. Write down the position vector of the boat t hours later. [1]
  2. (b) Find the position vector of the boat when t = 2.5. [1]
  3. (c) Find the speed of the boat. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) r = (2 + 4t)i + (5 − 3t)j
    B1 r = (2i + 5j) + t(4i − 3j)
  2. (b) 12i − 2.5j
    B1 2 + 10 = 12, 5 − 7.5 = −2.5
  3. (c) 5 km/h
    B1 √(4² + 3²) = 5
Q2Velocity and position vectors · Core · Paper 1 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) A particle moves with speed 26 m/s in the direction of the vector 5i − 12j. Find its velocity vector. [2]
  2. (b) At t = 0 the particle is at the point with position vector (−3i + 7j) m. Find its position vector when t = 4 s. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) (10i − 24j) m/s
    M1 |5i − 12j| = 13; A1 26/13 × (5i − 12j) = 10i − 24j
  2. (b) 37i − 89j
    M1 (−3i + 7j) + 4(10i − 24j); A1 37i − 89j
Q3Velocity and position vectors · Core · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) A camera drone in a park in Islamabad moves in a straight line with constant velocity. At t = 0 it is at the point with position vector (−6i + 8j) m and at t = 5 s it is at (18i − 2j) m. Find the velocity vector of the drone. [2]
  2. (b) Find the speed of the drone. [1]
  3. (c) Find the angle between the direction of motion and the vector i. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) (4.8i − 2j) m/s
    M1 displacement = 24i − 10j; A1 ÷ 5
  2. (b) 5.2 m/s
    B1 √(4.8² + 2²) = √27.04 = 5.2
  3. (c) 22.6° (below i)
    M1 tan⁻¹(2/4.8); A1 22.6°
Q4Velocity and position vectors · Core · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) In this question i is a unit vector due east and j a unit vector due north; distances are in kilometres. At 12:00 a ferry near Karachi is at the point with position vector (3i + 4j) km relative to a lighthouse L. It moves with constant velocity (−5i + 12j) km/h. Find the position vector of the ferry t hours after 12:00. [1]
  2. (b) Find the time at which the ferry is due north of L, and its distance from L at that time. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) (3 − 5t)i + (4 + 12t)j
    B1
  2. (b) 12:36; 11.2 km
    M1 3 − 5t = 0; A1 t = 0.6 h = 36 min, 12:36; A1 4 + 12(0.6) = 11.2 km
Q5Velocity and position vectors · Core · Paper 1 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) A delivery van moves with constant velocity across a flat plain. At 08:00 it is at the point with position vector (4i − 2j) km and at 08:15 it is at (7i + 2j) km. Find the velocity of the van in km/h and its speed. [3]
  2. (b) Assuming the velocity does not change, find the position vector of the van at 09:00. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) (12i + 16j) km/h; 20 km/h
    M1 displacement 3i + 4j in 0.25 h; A1 12i + 16j; B1 20 km/h
  2. (b) 16i + 14j
    B1 (4i − 2j) + 1 × (12i + 16j)
Q6Velocity and position vectors · Standard · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Two motor boats A and B move on a lake with constant velocities. At t = 0, A is at (2i + 10j) km and B is at (14i − 2j) km. The velocity of A is (6i + 2j) km/h and the velocity of B is (2i + 6j) km/h. Find, in terms of t, the position vectors of A and of B at time t hours. [2]
  2. (b) Show that A and B would collide, and find the time and the position vector of the point of collision. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) rA = (2 + 6t)i + (10 + 2t)j; rB = (14 + 2t)i + (−2 + 6t)j
    B1 B1
  2. (b) t = 3 h at 20i + 16j
    M1 equate i-components: 2 + 6t = 14 + 2t → t = 3; A1 j-components both 16 at t = 3 (so they collide); A1 20i + 16j
Q7Velocity and position vectors · Standard · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Particle P starts at the point with position vector (i + 2j) m and moves with velocity (3i + 4j) m/s. At the same time particle Q starts at (10i − j) m and moves with velocity (−i + 5j) m/s. Show that P and Q do not collide. [3]
  2. (b) Find the distance between P and Q when t = 2. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) i-components equal at t = 2.25, j-components equal at t = 3: different times, so no collision
    M1 1 + 3t = 10 − t → t = 2.25; M1 2 + 4t = −1 + 5t → t = 3; A1 conclusion
  2. (b) √2 m (1.41 m)
    M1 P = 7i + 10j, Q = 8i + 9j; A1 √(1² + 1²) = √2
Q8Velocity and position vectors · Standard · Paper 1 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) i is due east and j is due north. Boat A leaves port O at 10:00 and moves with velocity (8i + 6j) km/h. Boat B leaves a point with position vector (4i + 20j) km at 11:00 and moves with velocity (12i − 4j) km/h. Write down the position vectors of A and B at time t hours after 10:00, where t ≥ 1. [2]
  2. (b) Find the time at which B is due east of A, and the distance between the boats at that time. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) rA = 8ti + 6tj; rB = (4 + 12(t − 1))i + (20 − 4(t − 1))j = (12t − 8)i + (24 − 4t)j
    B1 B1 (B moves for t − 1 hours)
  2. (b) 12:24; 1.6 km
    M1 6t = 24 − 4t; A1 t = 2.4 h → 12:24; A1 A at 19.2i + 14.4j, B at 20.8i + 14.4j, distance 1.6 km
Q9Velocity and position vectors · Standard · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) i is due east and j is due north. A light aircraft flies with constant velocity (−120i + 160j) km/h. Find its speed and the bearing on which it is flying. [3]
  2. (b) At 09:00 the aircraft is at (300i − 100j) km relative to an airfield. Find its position vector after it has flown 500 km. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 200 km/h on a bearing of 323.1°
    B1 √(120² + 160²) = 200; M1 tan⁻¹(120/160) = 36.9° west of north; A1 323.1°
  2. (b) 300j km (due north of the airfield, 300 km away)
    M1 time = 500/200 = 2.5 h; A1 (300 − 300)i + (−100 + 400)j
Q10Velocity and position vectors · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 8 marks
  1. (a) i is due east and j is due north. A ship sails at 15 km/h on a bearing of 060°. Show that its velocity is (7.5√3 i + 7.5j) km/h. [2]
  2. (b) At t = 0 the ship is at (−20i + 5j) km relative to a buoy O. Find the time at which the ship is due north of O, giving your answer in minutes correct to the nearest minute, and its distance from O then. [3]
  3. (c) By writing the square of the distance from O as a quadratic in t, or otherwise, find the shortest distance between the ship and O. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) 15 sin 60° i + 15 cos 60° j = 7.5√3 i + 7.5j
    M1 resolve using the bearing; A1 exact values
  2. (b) 92 minutes; 16.5 km
    M1 −20 + 7.5√3 t = 0; A1 t = 1.540 h ≈ 92 min; A1 5 + 7.5(1.540) = 16.5 km
  3. (c) 14.3 km (at t ≈ 0.988 h)
    M1 d² = (−20 + 12.99t)² + (5 + 7.5t)²; M1 minimise: t = (20×12.99 − 37.5)/225 = 0.988; A1 d = 14.3 km
Q11Velocity and position vectors · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 4 marks

  2. (b) Find the exact speed of B. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) p = −3, q = 3

  2. (b) 3√2 m/s
    B1 √(9 + 9) = 3√2
Q12Velocity and position vectors · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) Two remote-controlled boats move on a pond. Boat P starts at (2i + 3j) m with velocity (ai + 2j) m/s, where a is a constant. Boat Q starts at the same time at (11i − 5j) m with velocity (−i + 6j) m/s. Given that the boats collide, find the time of the collision and the value of a. [4]
  2. (b) Find the position vector of the point of collision and the speed of P, correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) t = 2 s, a = 3.5
    M1 j-components: 3 + 2t = −5 + 6t; A1 t = 2; M1 i-components: 2 + 2a = 11 − 2; A1 a = 3.5
  2. (b) 9i + 7j; 4.03 m/s
    B1 9i + 7j; B1 √(3.5² + 2²) = 4.03
Q13Vector geometry · Core · Paper 1 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Relative to an origin O, the position vectors of A and B are a and b. M is the midpoint of AB and P lies on OB with OP : PB = 2 : 1. Find, in terms of a and b, the vector AB. [1]
  2. (b) Find OM. [1]
  3. (c) Find AP. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) b − a
    B1
  2. (b) ½(a + b)
    B1 a + ½(b − a)
  3. (c) ⅔b − a
    M1 OP = ⅔b; A1 AP = ⅔b − a

Q15Vector geometry · Core · Paper 2 style · 4 marks

Q16Vector geometry · Core · Paper 1 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) The points P, Q and R have position vectors i + 2j, 4i + 8j and 6i + 12j. Find PQ and PR. [2]
  2. (b) Hence show that P, Q and R lie on a straight line, and find the ratio PQ : QR. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) PQ = 3i + 6j; PR = 5i + 10j
    B1 B1
  2. (b) PR = (5/3)PQ, common point P so collinear; PQ : QR = 3 : 2
    M1 PR is a multiple of PQ, sharing P; A1 QR = 2i + 4j so 3 : 2

Q18Vector geometry · Standard · Paper 1 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Relative to O, the points A and B have position vectors a and b. C lies on AB such that AC : CB = 3 : 1. Show that OC = ¼a + ¾b. [2]
  2. (b) D is the midpoint of OC. Find OD and BD in terms of a and b. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) OC = a + ¾(b − a) = ¼a + ¾b
    M1 OA + ¾AB; A1 simplified
  2. (b) OD = ⅛a + ⅜b; BD = ⅛a − ⅝b
    B1 OD; B1 BD = OD − OB
Q19Vector geometry · Standard · Paper 1 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) OAB is a triangle with OA = a and OB = b. M is the midpoint of OB and N lies on AB with AN : NB = 2 : 1. Find ON and AM in terms of a and b. [2]
  2. (b) The lines ON and AM meet at X, where OX = λON and AX = μAM. Find two expressions for OX and hence find λ and μ. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) ON = ⅓a + ⅔b; AM = ½b − a
    B1 B1
  2. (b) λ = 3/5, μ = 4/5
    M1 OX = λ(⅓a + ⅔b); M1 OX = a + μ(½b − a) = (1 − μ)a + ½μb; M1 compare: λ/3 = 1 − μ, 2λ/3 = μ/2; A1 λ = 3/5, μ = 4/5

Q21Vector geometry · Standard · Paper 1 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) OABC is a parallelogram with OA = a and OC = c. M is the midpoint of AB. Find OM and AC in terms of a and c. [2]
  2. (b) The line OM meets the diagonal AC at X. By writing OX = λOM and AX = μAC, find λ and μ, and hence the ratio OX : XM. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) OM = a + ½c; AC = c − a
    B1 B1
  2. (b) λ = ⅔, μ = ⅓; OX : XM = 2 : 1
    M1 λ(a + ½c) = (1 − μ)a + μc; M1 λ = 1 − μ and ½λ = μ; A1 λ = ⅔, μ = ⅓; A1 2 : 1
Q22Vector geometry · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) Relative to O, OA = a and OB = b. P lies on OA with OP : PA = 1 : 2, and Q lies on OB with OQ : QB = 3 : 1. The lines AQ and BP meet at R. Given that AR = λAQ and BR = μBP, express OR in terms of λ, a and b, and also in terms of μ, a and b. [2]
  2. (b) Find λ and μ, and hence express OR in terms of a and b. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) OR = (1 − λ)a + ¾λb; OR = ⅓μa + (1 − μ)b
    B1 B1
  2. (b) λ = 8/9, μ = 1/3; OR = (1/9)a + ⅔b
    M1 1 − λ = μ/3; M1 ¾λ = 1 − μ; A1 λ = 8/9, μ = 1/3; A1 OR
Q23Vector geometry · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) Relative to O, A has position vector 6i + 2j and B has position vector 2i + 8j. P is the midpoint of OA and Q is the midpoint of AB. The lines OQ and BP meet at X. Write OX = λOQ and BX = μBP and find λ and μ. [4]
  2. (b) Find the position vector of X and the distance OX correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) λ = ⅔, μ = ⅔
    M1 OQ = 4i + 5j, BP = i − 7j; M1 4λ = 2 + μ and 5λ = 8 − 7μ; A1 A1
  2. (b) (8/3)i + (10/3)j; 4.27
    B1; B1 ⅔√41 = 4.27
Q24Vector geometry · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) Relative to O, OA = a and OB = b. The point C lies on AB and OC = ⅖a + kb. Find k and the ratio AC : CB. [3]
  2. (b) The point D is such that OD = h × OC and BD is parallel to OA. Find h and express OD in terms of a and b. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) k = 3/5; AC : CB = 3 : 2
    M1 C on AB ⇒ OC = (1 − t)a + tb, coefficients add to 1; A1 k = 3/5; A1 AC = ⅗AB so 3 : 2
  2. (b) h = 5/3; OD = ⅔a + b
    M1 BD = (2h/5)a + (3h/5 − 1)b; M1 b-coefficient = 0; A1 h = 5/3, OD = ⅔a + b

Q30Vector algebra and unit vectors · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) p = 2i + tj and q = (t − 1)i + 6j, where t is a constant. Find the values of t for which p is parallel to q. [3]
  2. (b) For the positive value of t, find |p + q| correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
