Q13Arc length and sector area · Standard · Paper 2 style · 5 marks

  1. (a) Find the area of windscreen wiped by the blade, correct to the nearest cm². [3]
  2. (b) Find the perimeter of the wiped region. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 2389 cm²
    ½ × 2.1 × (50² − 15²) M1 M1 = 2388.75 A1
  2. (b) 206.5 cm
    arcs 2.1 × 50 = 105 and 2.1 × 15 = 31.5; straight edges 2 × 35 M1; 105 + 31.5 + 70 = 206.5 A1
Q14Arc length and sector area · Standard · Paper 1 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) A sector of a circle of radius r cm and angle θ radians has perimeter 20 cm and area 24 cm². Show that r² − 10r + 24 = 0. [3]
  2. (b) Hence find the two possible pairs of values of r and θ. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) shown
    rθ + 2r = 20 B1; ½r²θ = 24 B1; ½r(20 − 2r) = 24 ⇒ r² − 10r + 24 = 0 B1
  2. (b) r = 4, θ = 3 or r = 6, θ = 4/3
    r = 4 or 6 B1; θ = 12/4 = 3 B1; θ = 8/6 = 4/3 B1

  3. (c) Find the area of this region. [3]
Show answer

Q19Arc length and sector area · Standard · Paper 1 style · 4 marks

The region R lies between two arcs with the same centre O and angle θ radians: the inner arc has radius 5 cm and the outer arc has radius 8 cm, and the ends are joined by straight lines along the radii. The area of R is 19.5 cm².

  1. (a) Find the value of θ. [2]
  2. (b) Find the perimeter of the region. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) θ = 1
    ½θ(8² − 5²) = 19.5 M1; θ = 1 A1
  2. (b) 19 cm
    arcs 8 + 5, straight edges 3 + 3 M1; 19 A1
Q20Arc length and sector area · Standard · Paper 1 style · 4 marks

Three identical cylindrical pipes, each of radius 4 cm, are placed so that each touches the other two. A tight band is wrapped once around the three pipes.

  1. (a) Explain why each arc of the band in contact with a pipe subtends an angle of 2π/3 radians at the centre of that pipe. [2]
  2. (b) Find the exact length of the band. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) the straight parts are tangents perpendicular to radii; angle = 2π − π/2 − π/2 − π/3 = 2π/3
    radii ⟂ tangents M1; 2π/3 A1
  2. (b) 24 + 8π cm
    three straight parts each 8 cm M1; three arcs total 2π × 4 = 8π A1

Q23Arc length and sector area · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 7 marks

  2. (b) Find the area of the region bounded by TA, TB and the minor arc AB. [3]

  1. (a) shown

Two circles, each of radius 6 cm, have centres P and Q with PQ = 6 cm. The circles intersect at A and B. Do not use a calculator in this question.

  1. (a) Show that the angle subtended at each centre by the common chord is 2π/3 radians. [2]
  2. (b) Find the exact area of the region common to both circles. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) shown

  2. (b) 24π − 18√3 cm²

Q25Arc length and sector area · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) A piece of wire of length 40 cm is bent to form the boundary of a sector of radius r cm. Show that the area A cm² of the sector is given by A = 20r − r². [2]
  2. (b) Find the value of r for which A is a maximum, and find the angle of the sector and the maximum area. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) shown
    arc = 40 − 2r B1; A = ½r(40 − 2r) = 20r − r² B1
  2. (b) r = 10, θ = 2 radians, A = 100 cm²
    dA/dr = 20 − 2r = 0 M1; r = 10 A1; θ = 20/10 = 2 A1; A = 100 A1

Q27Arc length and sector area · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 8 marks

OAB is a sector of a circle with centre O, radius 12 cm and angle AOB = π/3 radians. The tangent to the arc at A meets OB extended at T. Do not use a calculator in this question.

  1. (a) Find the exact length of AT and of BT. [3]
  2. (b) Find the exact perimeter of the region bounded by AT, TB and the arc AB. [2]
  3. (c) Find the exact area of this region. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) AT = 12√3 cm, BT = 12 cm
    OA ⟂ AT, AT = 12 tan(π/3) = 12√3 M1 A1; OT = 12/cos(π/3) = 24, BT = 12 A1
  2. (b) 12√3 + 12 + 4π cm
    arc 12 × π/3 = 4π B1; total B1
  3. (c) 72√3 − 24π cm²
    triangle OAT ½ × 12 × 12√3 = 72√3 B1; sector 24π B1; 72√3 − 24π B1

Q29Arc length and sector area · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 6 marks

A sector OAB has centre O, radius r cm and angle AOB = 0.8 radians. The perimeter of the minor segment bounded by the chord AB and the arc AB is 12 cm.

  2. (b) Find the area of the minor segment, correct to 3 significant figures. [3]

Q30Arc length and sector area · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 7 marks

OABC is a rhombus with sides 8 cm and angle AOC = 2π/3 radians. An arc of a circle with centre O and radius 8 cm joins A to C. Do not use a calculator in this question.

  1. (a) Show that B lies on the arc AC. [2]
  2. (b) Find the exact area of the region inside the sector but outside the rhombus. [3]
  3. (c) Find the exact perimeter of this region. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) triangle OAB is equilateral, so OB = 8
    angle OAB = π − 2π/3 = π/3 with OA = AB = 8 M1; so OB = 8 A1
  2. (b) 64π/3 − 32√3 cm²
    sector ½ × 64 × 2π/3 = 64π/3 B1; rhombus 64 sin(2π/3) = 32√3 B1; difference B1
  3. (c) 16π/3 + 16 cm
    arc AC = 8 × 2π/3 = 16π/3 B1; AB + BC = 16 B1
