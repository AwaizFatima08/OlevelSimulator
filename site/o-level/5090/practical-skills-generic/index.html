Q1Graphs & tables · Core · Paper 1 style · 1 marks
  1. (a) A student records the time taken for a colour change in a results table. Which column heading is set out correctly?A time (seconds)B time / sC time takenD s / time [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) B time / s
    The heading gives the quantity, then a solidus and the unit: time / s. C has no unit; D puts the unit first.
Q2Graphs & tables · Core · Paper 4 style · 4 marks

Four students measured the length of the same dried bean seedling root. Their results were: 4.6 cm, 4.8 cm, 7.9 cm, 4.5 cm.

  1. (a) Identify the anomalous result. [1]
  2. (b) Calculate the mean, leaving out the anomalous result. Give your answer to 1 decimal place. [2]
  3. (c) State why the anomalous result should not be included in the mean. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) 7.9 cm
    B1
  2. (b) 4.6 cm
    M1 (4.6 + 4.8 + 4.5) ÷ 3; A1 4.6 cm
  3. (c) It does not fit the pattern / is probably due to an error, so it would make the mean unrepresentative.
    B1
Q3Graphs & tables · Core · Paper 4 style · 4 marks

Pondweed bubbles at different lamp distances:distance / cm | 10 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50bubbles per minute | 64 | 35 | 20 | 13 | 8

  1. (a) State the dependent variable shown in the table. [1]
  2. (b) Describe the relationship shown by the results. [2]
  3. (c) Suggest a suitable scale for the y-axis if the grid is 10 large squares high. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) Number of bubbles per minute
    B1
  2. (b) As the distance of the lamp increases, the number of bubbles decreases (from 64 at 10 cm to 8 at 50 cm).
    B1 inverse trend; B1 data quoted with units
  3. (c) 1 large square = 10 bubbles per minute (0 to 70 / 100).
    B1 any linear scale that fits 64 and uses most of the grid
Q4Graphs & tables · Standard · Paper 1 style · 1 marks
  1. (a) Which type of graph should be drawn to show the number of students in a class with each blood group (A, B, AB, O)?A line graph with points joinedB bar chart with separate barsC histogram with touching barsD scatter graph with a line of best fit [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) B bar chart with separate bars
    Blood group is a discontinuous (categoric) variable, so a bar chart with gaps between bars is used. A histogram is for continuous data in classes.
Q5Graphs & tables · Standard · Paper 4 style · 5 marks

A student timed how long it took for starch to be digested by amylase at different temperatures. Results: 10 °C took 240 s; 20 °C took 130 s; 30 °C took 70 s; 40 °C took 55 s; 50 °C took 180 s.

  1. (a) Draw a results table for these data with suitable headings. Describe what your table must include. [2]
  2. (b) Calculate the rate of reaction at 30 °C using rate = 1000 ÷ time. Give your answer to 1 decimal place and state the unit. [2]
  3. (c) State which variable goes on the x-axis of a graph of these results. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) Two columns: temperature / °C and time for starch to disappear / s, with the values in the cells and no units in the cells.
    B1 headings with quantity and unit (°C, s) in heading; B1 all data entered in correct order, no units in body
  2. (b) 14.3 s⁻¹ (or /s)
    M1 1000 ÷ 70; A1 14.3 per second
  3. (c) Temperature (the independent variable)
    B1
Q6Graphs & tables · Standard · Paper 4 style · 3 marks

A piece of potato had a mass of 48 g. After 30 minutes in a concentrated salt solution its mass was 36 g.

  1. (a) Calculate the percentage change in mass of the potato piece. Give a negative sign for a decrease. [2]
  2. (b) Explain why percentage change is used rather than the change in mass when comparing pieces. [1]
Show answer

    M1 (36 − 48) ÷ 48 × 100; A1 -25 %
  2. (b) The pieces had different starting masses, so percentage change allows a fair comparison.

Q7Graphs & tables · Standard · Paper 4 style · 4 marks

Q8Graphs & tables · Challenge · Paper 4 style · 7 marks

Volume of oxygen collected from yeast and hydrogen peroxide:time / min | 0 | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20volume / cm³ | 0 | 12 | 22 | 30 | 36

  1. (a) Assuming a straight line between the two nearest readings, estimate the volume of gas collected at 12 minutes. [2]
  2. (b) Describe the shape of the graph that these results would give. [2]
  3. (c) Calculate the mean rate of gas production over the first 10 minutes. [1]
  4. (d) State two rules for plotting a line graph of these results. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 25.2 cm³
    M1 22 + (30 − 22) × 2/5; A1 25.2 cm³
  2. (b) Volume increases with time, but the rate of increase gets smaller (curve levels off).
    B1 increases; B1 gradient decreases / levels off
  3. (c) 2.2 cm³/min
    B1 22 ÷ 10
  4. (d) Linear scales using more than half the grid; axes labelled with units; points plotted accurately (small crosses); smooth curve / ruled lines drawn through points.
    1 mark each for two
Q9Graphs & tables · Challenge · Paper 4 style · 5 marks

Height of a radish seedling:day | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5height / cm | 2.0 | 3.5 | 5.5 | 8.0 | 11.0

  1. (a) Calculate the mean growth rate in cm per day between day 1 and day 5. [2]
  2. (b) State whether the growth rate is constant. Use the data to explain. [2]
  3. (c) Explain why a line of best fit through the origin would be wrong for these data. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) 2.25 cm/day
    M1 (11.0 − 2.0) ÷ (5 − 1); A1 2.25 cm/day
  2. (b) No: daily increases are 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0 cm, so the rate increases each day.
    B1 not constant / increasing; B1 supporting differences
  3. (c) The points do not lie on a straight line (the curve gets steeper), and there is no reading at day 0 to show the height was zero then.
    B1 either point

Q20Apparatus, measurement & safety · Standard · Paper 1 style · 1 marks

Q23Errors, reliability & improvements · Core · Paper 4 style · 4 marks

Show answer
  1. (a) 0.2 cm
    B1 12.6 − 12.4
  2. (b) Systematic error; corrected mean = 12.2 cm
    B1 systematic; B1 12.5 − 0.3 = 12.2 cm
  3. (c) Every repeat has the same error, in the same direction.
    B1
Q24Errors, reliability & improvements · Standard · Paper 4 style · 4 marks

Q25Errors, reliability & improvements · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 1 marks
  1. (a) A student repeats an experiment three times and gets very similar results each time. What does this show?A the results are accurateB the results are repeatable / reliableC there are no systematic errorsD the hypothesis is proved [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) B the results are repeatable / reliable
