A student plans to find out whether drinking a cup of strong tea affects the reaction time of students in her class, using a ruler-drop test.

  1. (a) State three variables, other than the drink, that should be controlled. [3]
  2. (b) Suggest a suitable control. [1]
  3. (c) State one ethical point the student must consider. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) time of day / how tired they are; age; whether they have had other caffeine that day; which hand is used; the same ruler and dropping method; number of practice tries

  2. (b) Students drink the same volume of warm water (or decaffeinated tea) instead.

Q73Variables, controls & planning · Challenge · Paper 4 style · 6 marks

Duckweed is a tiny floating plant that grows by producing new fronds. A student wants to find out how the concentration of nitrate ions in the water affects the growth of duckweed.

  1. (a) Plan an investigation to test this. Include the variables, the method, how you would measure growth, how you would make the results reliable and any safety points. [6]
Show answer
  1. (a) IV: nitrate concentration (e.g. 0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 g per dm³ made by dilution); DV: number of fronds after a fixed time (e.g. 14 days) or increase in number; start each beaker with the same number of fronds (e.g. 10) of similar size; same volume of solution, same light, temperature, container; count fronds every 2 days; three beakers per concentration and calculate means; present results in a table and line graph; wash hands after handling solutions.
    1 mark each for any six of: IV with at least five concentrations; DV clearly defined; same starting number of fronds; two controlled variables; time period stated; method of counting / measuring; repeats and mean; control with no nitrate; table / graph; safety.

Q75Variables, controls & planning · Challenge · Paper 4 style · 6 marks

A student wrote this plan: 'I will put a potato cylinder into each of four beakers containing 0 %, 5 %, 10 % and 20 % salt solution. I will use whatever volume of solution is in each bottle. I will leave the first beaker for 20 minutes and the others until the end of the lesson. I will measure the length of each cylinder with a ruler at the end.'

  1. (a) Identify three weaknesses in the plan. [3]
  2. (b) For each weakness you identified, suggest an improvement. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) volume of solution not controlled; times not the same; starting length not measured / not the same; only one cylinder per concentration (no repeats); cylinders may come from different potatoes
    1 mark each for three.
  2. (b) use the same volume (e.g. 30 cm³) in each beaker; leave all for the same time (e.g. 30 min); measure length at start and end and calculate % change; use three cylinders per concentration and calculate a mean; cut all cylinders from the same potato with a cork borer
    1 mark each, matching the weaknesses.
