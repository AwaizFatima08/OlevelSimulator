Q1Thematic essay on the set passages · Core · Paper 1 style · 14 marks
  1. (a) Using at least two of the set passages, describe the blessings that God has given to human beings. [10]
  2. (b) Explain one way Muslims can show thanks for these blessings. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) Creation of humans and earlier peoples, the earth as a resting place, the sky as a canopy, rain and fruit (2.21–22); creation from a clot, knowledge and the pen (96.1–5); mercy, guidance to the straight path (Sura 1); protection from evil (114); acceptance of repentance (2.37).
    Indicative content: Blessings correctly linked to a set passage, e.g.: life and creation (2.21, 96.2); the earth spread out (2.22); the sky as a canopy (2.22); rain and produce (2.22); knowledge and writing (96.4–5); guidance to the straight path (1.6); mercy (1.1–3); refuge from evil whispers (114); forgiveness of Adam (2.37); shelter, guidance and provision for the Prophet (93.6–8). An answer that uses only one passage cannot go above Level 2. Blessings must be linked to the passages, not just listed. Marked by levels out of 10. Level 4 (9–10): a full, accurate and well-organised answer with precise detail (names, dates, places, sequence) and supporting references to the Qur'an or Hadith where relevant. Level 3 (6–8): a mostly accurate answer covering the main points, with some detail but gaps or little supporting evidence. Level 2 (3–5): a general answer with some relevant points but limited detail or some errors. Level 1 (1–2): a few basic or partly relevant points. 0: nothing creditworthy.
  2. (b) For example, by using the gift of knowledge to help others, such as teaching children to read.
    Mark points to credit: Relevant action, explaining how it shows gratitude. Model answer: For example, by using the gift of knowledge to help others, such as teaching children to read. Marked by levels out of 4. Level 4 (4): a well-developed, reasoned answer (two developed points, or one fully developed point) linked to life today, with a clear judgement where the question asks for one. Level 3 (3): a reasoned answer with some development and a relevant example, but the judgement or link to today is less clear. Level 2 (2): one or two simple points with little development. Level 1 (1): a basic, general statement. 0: nothing creditworthy.

Q3Thematic essay on the set passages · Core · Paper 1 style · 8 marks
  1. (a) Describe what the set passages say about angels. [4]
  2. (b) Explain what Muslims can learn from the angels' obedience in these passages. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) God told the angels about creating a vicegerent; they questioned and admitted their knowledge came from God; they prostrated to Adam at God's command (2.30–34); they glorify God and seek forgiveness for those on earth (42.5); Jesus was strengthened by the Holy Spirit (5.110).
    1 mark each for any four: angels informed of Adam's appointment; they asked about corruption and bloodshed; they knew only what God taught them; they bowed to Adam; Iblis refused (credit as contrast); angels praise God; they ask forgiveness for people (42.5); support of Jesus by the Holy Spirit (5.110).

    Up to 4 marks for developed points, e.g.: obedience to God's commands without arrogance (1+1); humility about knowledge — saying 'I don't know' (1); praying for forgiveness for others (1).
