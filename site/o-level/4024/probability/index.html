Q35Relative & expected frequency · Challenge · Paper 2 style · calculator · 5 marks

The probability that a seed of a certain flower germinates is 0.82. The probability that a seedling that has germinated survives to flower is 0.75.

  1. (a) A gardener in Murree plants 600 seeds. Find the expected number of plants that flower. [2]
  2. (b) A park wants about 1000 flowering plants. Find the least number of seeds it should plant to expect at least 1000 flowering plants. [2]
  3. (c) Explain why the actual number of flowering plants from 1627 seeds may not be exactly 1000. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) 369
    0.82 × 0.75 = 0.615 M1; 0.615 × 600 = 369 A1
  2. (b) 1627

    B1
