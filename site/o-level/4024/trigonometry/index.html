Q13Sine & cosine rules · Challenge · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 5 marks

A triangle has sides x cm, (x + 1) cm and (x + 2) cm. The largest angle of the triangle is 120°.

  1. (a) Use the cosine rule to show that 2x² − x − 3 = 0. [cos 120° = −½] [3]
  2. (b) Solve the equation and write down the lengths of the three sides. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) (x + 2)² = x² + (x + 1)² + x(x + 1), which simplifies to 2x² − x − 3 = 0

  2. (b) x = 1.5; sides 1.5 cm, 2.5 cm, 3.5 cm
    (2x − 3)(x + 1) = 0 M1; x = 1.5 (x > 0) A1
