Q2Cumulative frequency & box plots · Core · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 5 marks

A shop tested the battery life, in hours, of phones of two brands. The results are summarised by these box plots.Brand P: minimum 9, lower quartile 13, median 16, upper quartile 18, maximum 24.Brand Q: minimum 11, lower quartile 17, median 19, upper quartile 26, maximum 30.

  1. (a) Find the interquartile range for each brand. [2]
  2. (b) For brand P, 48 phones were tested. How many of them had a battery life of more than 13 hours? [1]
  3. (c) Make two comparisons between the battery lives of the two brands. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) P: 5 hours; Q: 9 hours

    B1 median comparison in context (19 > 16); B1 spread comparison using IQR 5 < 9 or range 15 < 19
