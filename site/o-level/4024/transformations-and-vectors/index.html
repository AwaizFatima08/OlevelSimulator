Q1Vectors · Core · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 4 marks

u = (5, −3) and v = (−2, 7) are column vectors.

  1. (a) Find u − 2v as a column vector. [2]
  2. (b) Find the column vector w such that u + w = v. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) (9, −17)
    −2v = (4, −14); u − 2v = (5 + 4, −3 − 14). M1 for −2v, A1.
  2. (b) w = (−7, 10)
    w = v − u = (−2 − 5, 7 − (−3)) = (−7, 10). M1 A1.
Q2Vectors · Core · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 6 marks

A is the point (1, −4) and B is the point (7, 2).

  1. (a) Write AB as a column vector. [1]
  2. (b) Find the coordinates of the midpoint of AB. [1]
  3. (c) C is the point such that AC = 2AB. Find the coordinates of C. [2]
  4. (d) Find |AB| in the form k√2, where k is an integer. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) (6, 6)
    AB = (7 − 1, 2 − (−4)).
  2. (b) (4, −1)
    ((1 + 7)/2, (−4 + 2)/2).

  4. (d) |AB| = 6√2
    |AB| = √(36 + 36) = √72 = √(36 × 2) = 6√2. M1 A1.

Q4Vectors · Core · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 4 marks

The column vectors (5, x) and (y, 3) satisfy 2(5, x) − (y, 3) = (7, 9).

  1. (a) Find x and y. [2]
  2. (b) Find the magnitude of the column vector (x, y), giving your answer as a surd in its simplest form. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 6, y = 3
    First component: 2 × 5 − y = 7, so y = 3. Second component: 2x − 3 = 9, so x = 6. M1 A1.
  2. (b) 3√5
    √(6² + 3²) = √45 = √(9 × 5) = 3√5. M1 A1.

Q13Vectors · Standard · Paper 2 style · calculator · 7 marks

A helicopter has velocity (90, −40) km/h relative to the air. The wind has velocity (−15, 10) km/h. The x-direction is east and the y-direction is north.

  1. (a) Find the resultant velocity of the helicopter as a column vector. [1]
  2. (b) Find the speed of the helicopter, correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
  3. (c) Find the bearing of the direction of travel, correct to the nearest degree. [2]
  4. (d) The helicopter flies 300 km at this speed. Give the time taken in hours and minutes, to the nearest minute. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) (75, −30) km/h
    (90 − 15, −40 + 10).
  2. (b) 80.8 km/h
    √(75² + 30²) = √6525 = 80.78… M1 A1.
  3. (c) 112°
    Bearing = 180° − arctan(75 ÷ 30) = 180° − 68.2° = 111.8°, so 112°. M1 A1.
  4. (d) 3 hours 43 minutes
    300 ÷ 80.78 = 3.713 h; 0.713 × 60 = 42.8 min, so 3 h 43 min. M1 A1.
Q14Vectors · Standard · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 6 marks

In triangle OPQ, OP = p and OQ = q. R lies on PQ with PR : RQ = 2 : 5. S is the midpoint of OR.

  1. (a) Find PQ and OR in terms of p and q. [2]
  2. (b) Find OS in terms of p and q. [2]
  3. (c) Find PS in terms of p and q. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) PQ = q − p; OR = (5/7)p + (2/7)q
    PR = (2/7)PQ = (2/7)(q − p); OR = OP + PR = p + (2/7)(q − p). M1 A1.
  2. (b) OS = (5/14)p + (1/7)q
    OS = ½OR = ½((5/7)p + (2/7)q). M1 A1.
  3. (c) PS = −(9/14)p + (1/7)q
    PS = OS − OP = (5/14 − 1)p + (1/7)q. M1 A1.
Q15Vectors · Standard · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 6 marks
  1. (a) The vector (k, 3) is parallel to (4, 6). Find k. [2]
  2. (b) The vector (m, 2) is parallel to (−3, 5). Find m as a fraction. [2]
  3. (c) The vector (n, n + 1) has magnitude 5. Find the two possible values of n. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) k = 2
    Parallel vectors have proportional components: k × 6 = 3 × 4, so k = 2. M1 A1.
  2. (b) m = −6/5
    m × 5 = 2 × (−3), so m = −6/5. M1 A1.
  3. (c) n = 3 or n = −4
    n² + (n + 1)² = 25, so 2n² + 2n − 24 = 0, n² + n − 12 = 0, (n − 3)(n + 4) = 0. M1 A1.

Q24Vectors · Challenge · Paper 2 style · calculator · 8 marks

    O − C = (1.5, −1.5); rotating: (1.5, 1.5); add C: (0, 3). M1 A1.

  2. (b) Find the area of triangle ABC and the area of its image in part (a). [2]

Show answer

  2. (b) 12 square units and 3 square units
