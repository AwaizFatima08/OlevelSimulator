Q1Money & interest · Core · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 4 marks
  1. (a) A monthly electricity bill is made up of a fixed charge of Rs 350 plus Rs 28 for each unit used. Work out the bill for a month in which 240 units are used. [2]
  2. (b) In the next month the bill is Rs 5 670. How many units were used? [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Rs 7 070
    240 × 28 = 6 720 M1; 6 720 + 350 = 7 070 A1.
  2. (b) 190 units
    (5 670 − 350) ÷ 28 M1; = 190 A1.

  2. (b) Work out the total amount in her account at the end of the 3 years. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) Rs 1 800
    12 000 × 5/100 × 3 M1; = 1 800 A1.
  2. (b) Rs 13 800
    12 000 + 1 800 B1.

Q4Money & interest · Core · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 4 marks
  1. (a) The cash price of a television is Rs 84 000. It can also be bought on hire purchase with a deposit of 20% of the cash price and 12 monthly payments of Rs 6 200. Work out the total hire purchase price. [3]
  2. (b) How much more does it cost to buy the television on hire purchase than for cash? [1]

Q7Money & interest · Standard · Paper 2 style · calculator · 4 marks
  1. (a) Rs 85 000 earns Rs 15 300 simple interest in 4 years. Work out the rate of interest per year. [2]
  2. (b) At the same rate of simple interest, how many years does it take Rs 120 000 to earn Rs 27 000 interest? [2]
Show answer

  2. (b) 5 years
    27 000 ÷ (120 000 × 0.045) M1; = 5 A1.
Q8Money & interest · Standard · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 5 marks

Q10Money & interest · Standard · Paper 2 style · calculator · 5 marks
  1. (a) Hassan invests Rs 240 000 at 6.5% per year simple interest. Work out the interest he earns in 4 years. [2]
  2. (b) Sana invests Rs 240 000 at 6% per year compound interest. Work out the value of her investment after 4 years. Give your answer to the nearest rupee. [2]
  3. (c) Whose investment is worth more after 4 years, and by how much? [1]
