  2. (b) Find the total surface area of the closed tin, correct to 3 significant figures. [3]

  3. (c) Find the length of a diagonal of the rectangle. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) 2(l + w) = 46, so l + w = 23; and lw = 120.
