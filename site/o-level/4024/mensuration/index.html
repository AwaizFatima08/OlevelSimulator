Q1Surface area & volume · Core · Paper 2 style · calculator · 5 marks

A closed cylindrical tin has radius 4 cm and height 10 cm.

  1. (a) Find the volume of the tin, correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
  2. (b) Find the total surface area of the closed tin, correct to 3 significant figures. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) 503 cm³
    π × 4² × 10 M1; = 502.65… ≈ 503 A1.
  2. (b) 352 cm²
    2π × 4² M1; + 2π × 4 × 10 M1; 112π = 351.9 ≈ 352 A1.

  1. (a) A water tank on a roof in Hyderabad is a cuboid measuring 1.2 m by 0.8 m by 0.6 m. Calculate the volume of the tank in m³. [1]
  2. (b) Write the volume in litres (1 m³ = 1000 litres). [1]
  3. (c) The empty tank is filled from a pipe at 12 litres per minute. How long does it take to fill? Give your answer in minutes. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 0.576 m³
    1.2 × 0.8 × 0.6 B1
  2. (b) 576 litres
    × 1000 B1
  3. (c) 48 minutes
    576 ÷ 12 M1 A1
Q3Surface area & volume · Core · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 6 marks
  1. (a) A tent is a triangular prism of length 3 m. Its cross-section is an isosceles triangle with base 2.4 m and perpendicular height 1.6 m. Find the volume of air inside the tent. [2]
  2. (b) Show that each sloping side of the triangle is 2 m long. [1]
  3. (c) The tent has a groundsheet, so all five faces are made of fabric. Find the total area of fabric. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) 5.76 m³
    Area of triangle ½ × 2.4 × 1.6 = 1.92 M1; × 3 A1
  2. (b) √(1.2² + 1.6²) = √4 = 2 m
    Half base 1.2, Pythagoras √(1.44 + 2.56) B1
  3. (c) 23.04 m²
    Triangles 2 × 1.92 = 3.84; base 2.4 × 3 = 7.2; sloping faces 2 × 2 × 3 = 12 M2 (M1 for two of these); total A1

Q12Surface area & volume · Challenge · Paper 2 style · calculator · 6 marks

A solid metal cube of side 10 cm is melted down and made into solid balls, each of radius 1.5 cm.

  1. (a) Find the volume of one ball, in terms of π. [1]
  2. (b) Find the greatest number of balls that can be made, and the volume of metal left over, correct to 3 significant figures. [3]
  3. (c) All 70 balls are dropped into a tall cylinder of radius 6 cm partly filled with water, and they are all covered by water. Find how much the water level rises. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 4.5π cm³
    4/3 × π × 1.5³ = 4.5π B1.
  2. (b) 70 balls; 10.4 cm³

  3. (c) 8.75 cm
    70 × 4.5π = 315π M1; 315π ÷ 36π = 8.75 A1.
