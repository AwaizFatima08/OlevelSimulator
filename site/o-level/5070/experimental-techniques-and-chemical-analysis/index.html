Q6Tests for ions, gases & water · Core · Paper 2 style · 5 marks

A farmer near Sahiwal buys a bag of white fertiliser labelled 'ammonium sulfate, (NH₄)₂SO₄'. A school student dissolves a little of it in distilled water to check the label.

  1. (a) Describe a test to show that the fertiliser contains ammonium ions. Give the result. [2]
  2. (b) Describe a test to show that the fertiliser contains sulfate ions. Give the result. [2]
  3. (c) Write the ionic equation, with state symbols, for the reaction in (b). [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) Warm with aqueous sodium hydroxide; the gas given off turns damp red litmus paper blue (ammonia).
    B1 warm with NaOH(aq); B1 gas turns damp red litmus blue.
  2. (b) Acidify with dilute nitric acid, then add aqueous barium nitrate; a white precipitate forms.
    B1 acidify (dilute HNO₃) then aqueous barium nitrate (barium chloride also accepted); B1 white precipitate.
  3. (c) Ba²⁺(aq) + SO₄²⁻(aq) → BaSO₄(s)
    B1 correct species and state symbols.
