Q15Strong/weak acids & pH · Standard · Paper 2 style · 5 marks

A student compares 1.0 mol/dm³ hydrochloric acid with 1.0 mol/dm³ ethanoic acid.

  1. (a) Predict which acid has the lower pH. [1]
  2. (b) Equal pieces of magnesium ribbon are added to equal volumes of each acid. Describe and explain the difference in what is seen. [3]
  3. (c) Explain why the two acids can have the same concentration but different pH values. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) hydrochloric acid
    B1
  2. (b) Faster fizzing with hydrochloric acid; HCl is fully ionised but ethanoic acid is only partly ionised; so HCl has a higher concentration of H⁺ ions and more frequent collisions.
    HCl fizzes faster B1; HCl fully / ethanoic acid partially ionised B1; higher H⁺ concentration in HCl B1
  3. (c) Concentration is the amount of acid dissolved; pH depends on the concentration of H⁺ ions, which depends on how much the acid ionises.
    B1
Q16Strong/weak acids & pH · Standard · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) State what is meant by an acid and a base in terms of proton transfer. [2]
  2. (b) Hydrogen chloride gas reacts with ammonia gas: HCl + NH₃ → NH₄Cl (which contains NH₄⁺ and Cl⁻ ions). Identify the acid and the base in this reaction. Explain your answer. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) An acid is a proton (H⁺) donor; a base is a proton acceptor.
    Each B1
  2. (b) HCl is the acid: it donates a proton (H⁺) to NH₃; NH₃ is the base: it accepts the proton to form NH₄⁺.
    HCl acid, donates H⁺ B1; NH₃ base, accepts H⁺ B1
Q17Strong/weak acids & pH · Standard · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Write an equation to show hydrochloric acid ionising in water and an equation to show ethanoic acid ionising in water. [2]
  2. (b) Explain the difference between the arrows in your two equations. [1]
  3. (c) Explain the difference between a dilute solution of a strong acid and a concentrated solution of a weak acid. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) HCl → H⁺ + Cl⁻CH₃COOH ⇌ CH₃COO⁻ + H⁺
    Each B1 (⇌ needed for ethanoic acid)
  2. (b) The single arrow shows HCl is completely ionised (strong); the reversible arrow shows ethanoic acid is only partly ionised (weak).
    B1
  3. (c) Dilute/concentrated refers to how much acid is dissolved per dm³; strong/weak refers to how fully the acid ionises.
    Concentration = amount per volume B1; strength = degree of ionisation B1
Q18Strong/weak acids & pH · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 6 marks
