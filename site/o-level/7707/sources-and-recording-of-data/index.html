Q1Cash book and petty cash book · Core · Paper 1 style · 1 mark
  1. (a) Zeeshan keeps a three-column cash book. A customer pays a debt by cheque and is allowed a cash discount. Where is the discount recorded in the cash book? [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) B debit side, discount column
    Discount allowed is recorded on the debit side (the same side as the receipt) in the discount column; the total is later debited to the discount allowed account. Credit side discount column is for discount received, so A is wrong.
Q2Cash book and petty cash book · Core · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Explain what is meant by an imprest system of petty cash. [2]
  2. (b) State two advantages to a business of using an imprest system. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) A fixed float is given to the petty cashier; at the end of each period the amount spent is reimbursed so the float returns to the same fixed amount.
    B1 fixed amount (float) at the start of each period. B1 restored by the exact amount spent.
  2. (b) Any two: limits the cash held by the petty cashier; spending can be checked easily (cash + vouchers = imprest); small payments are kept out of the main cash book; frees the main cashier's time; trains junior staff.
    1 mark each for any two.

  2. (b) State the entries in Haroon's three-column cash book when the cheque is received. [2]
  3. (c) State the entry in Rehman Stores' account in Haroon's sales ledger. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) $2,340
    2,400 × 97.5% = 2,340 (B1).
  2. (b) Debit side: bank column $2,340 and discount allowed column $60.
    B1 bank column debit 2,340. B1 discount column (debit side) 60.
  3. (c) Credit $2,340 bank and credit $60 discount allowed (total $2,400)
    B1 both credits; the account is cleared.

Q13Cash book and petty cash book · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 8 marks

On 1 May 2026 her three-column cash book showed cash in hand $420 and a bank balance of $3,150 (debit).May 3 Cash sales $1,260May 6 Received a cheque from Kamran Ltd in full settlement of a debt of $2,000, less 2.5% cash discountMay 9 Paid Zaib Suppliers by cheque for a debt of $1,600, less 4% cash discountMay 12 Paid wages in cash $680May 15 Paid $800 cash into the bankMay 20 Bought office equipment, paying by cheque $2,450May 24 Received $735 cash from Hina in full settlement of her debt of $750May 28 Paid electricity by bank transfer $390

  2. (b) Calculate the balance on the bank column on 31 May 2026. [2]

Show answer

Q26Double entry and ledger accounts · Standard · Paper 2 style · 5 marks

State the normal balance (debit or credit) of each account in the ledger.

  1. (a) Drawings. [1]
  2. (b) Sales. [1]
  3. (c) Trade payables. [1]
  4. (d) Motor vehicles. [1]
  5. (e) Discount received. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) Debit
    Withdrawals by the owner reduce capital, so they are debited.
  2. (b) Credit
    Income accounts increase with a credit.
  3. (c) Credit
    Amounts owed to suppliers are liabilities, which increase with credits.
  4. (d) Debit
    Assets increase with debits.
  5. (e) Credit
    Discount received is a gain (income) and is credited.
