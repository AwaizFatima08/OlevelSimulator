Q87Purpose and features of absorption costing · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 8 marks

The managing director of a manufacturing company says: 'Absorption costing gives the full cost of every product, so we should use it for all our decisions, including whether to accept special orders and which products to drop.'

  1. (a) Discuss the managing director's statement. [8]
Show answer
  1. (a) Agree: full cost shows the long-run cost that prices must cover; useful for routine pricing and for inventory valuation (IAS 2); consistent with published accounts.
