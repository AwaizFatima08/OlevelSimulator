A bakery in Lahore makes cakes. The standard is 3 kg of flour mix per batch at $4 per kg. In March it made 2,000 batches, using 6,300 kg that cost $24,570.

  1. (a) Calculate the material price variance. [2]
  2. (b) Calculate the material usage variance. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 6,300 × 4 − 24,570 = $630 favourable
    M1 AQ × SP − actual cost; A1 with F/A.
  2. (b) Standard quantity 2,000 × 3 = 6,000 kg; (6,000 − 6,300) × 4 = $1,200 adverse
    M1 (SQ − AQ) × SP; A1 with F/A.

Q19Reasons for variances and decisions · Core · Paper 4 style · 6 marks

The owner of a biscuit factory in Lahore is considering introducing standard costing.

  1. (a) Explain two benefits to a business of using standard costing. [4]
  2. (b) State two limitations of standard costing. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Any two, explained: it gives targets for planning and budgeting; variances show where performance differs from plan so managers can act (management by exception); it helps control costs and motivate staff; it makes inventory valuation and pricing easier.

Q20Reasons for variances and decisions · Core · Paper 4 style · 4 marks

The production manager of a shoe factory in Sialkot is setting new labour standards.

  1. (a) Explain the difference between an ideal standard and an attainable standard. [2]
  2. (b) Explain which type of standard is more likely to motivate employees. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) An ideal standard assumes perfect conditions with no waste, idle time or breakdowns; an attainable standard allows for normal waste and inefficiency and can be achieved with reasonable effort.
    1 mark each.
  2. (b) Attainable: workers can reach it with effort, so it is a realistic target; ideal standards are almost never met, so constant adverse variances demotivate.
    1 mark choice; 1 mark reason.

Q27Reasons for variances and decisions · Challenge · Paper 4 style · 7 marks

Q30Reasons for variances and decisions · Challenge · Paper 4 style · 7 marks

Malik Packaging installed a new cutting machine seven months ago. Since then the material usage variance has been adverse by about 8% of standard material cost every month. Production staff say the machine design wastes more card than the old one and complain that their bonus, which depends on meeting the standard, is never paid.

  1. (a) Advise the directors whether the material usage standard should be revised. Justify your answer. [7]
Show answer
  1. (a) A justified recommendation, e.g. revise the standard to an attainable level, because the adverse variance is permanent (caused by the new machine) and is demotivating staff, while keeping a target that encourages improvement.
    Revise (max 4): adverse usage variance of about 8% every month for six months shows the standard is out of date; the cause (new machine design) is outside workers' control; unrealistic standards demotivate and make variance reports useless; budgets and prices based on the old standard understate costs. Do not revise (max 3): the machine may be adjusted or staff trained to reduce waste; revising too easily builds in inefficiency; cost and time of setting new standards; supplier may be responsible. Decision (1).
