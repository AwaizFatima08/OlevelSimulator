Q5Partnership or sole trader acquired by a company · Core · Paper 3 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) Give two reasons why a limited company may buy an existing business rather than expand by opening new premises. [2]
  2. (b) Give two reasons why a sole trader may accept shares rather than cash for the business. [2]
  3. (c) Explain why purchased goodwill is shown in the buying company's statement of financial position, but the goodwill the sole trader had built up was not shown in the sole trader's books. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Any two: it gains an established customer base and reputation at once; removes a competitor; gains skilled staff, suppliers and premises; quicker than organic growth; economies of scale.
    1 mark each.
  2. (b) Any two: continues to share in future profits through dividends; shares may rise in value; limited liability; shares can be sold later; the company may not have enough cash.
    1 mark each.
  3. (c) Purchased goodwill has a reliable cost – the amount actually paid above the fair value of net assets. Internally generated goodwill has no reliable cost or measurable value, so it is not recognised.
    1 mark each side.
