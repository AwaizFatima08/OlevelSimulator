Show answer
  1. (a) B $83,000
    Cost of sales = 14,200 + 86,500 − 2,300 + 1,400 − 16,800 = 83,000. Carriage outwards is a selling expense, not part of cost of sales (A adds it); C adds the returns instead of deducting them; D leaves out carriage inwards.

  1. (a) During the year the owner of a shoe shop took shoes costing $650 for her family. No entry has been made. Which entry records this?A Debit purchases, credit drawingsB Debit capital, credit inventoryC Debit drawings, credit purchasesD Debit drawings, credit revenue at cost [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) C Debit drawings, credit purchases

Q9Statement of profit or loss and SOFP · Standard · Paper 2 style · 7 marks

Q10Statement of profit or loss and SOFP · Standard · Paper 2 style · 6 marks

Mei Ling's draft statement of profit or loss for the year ended 31 May 2026 shows gross profit $64,200 and profit for the year $23,900. It was then found that carriage outwards of $1,850 had been included in cost of sales.

  1. (a) Calculate the corrected gross profit and the corrected profit for the year. [2]
  2. (b) Explain why the profit for the year does not change. [2]
  3. (c) Carriage outwards is a cost of delivering goods to customers. Explain why it is not part of the cost of sales. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Gross profit $66,050; profit for the year $23,900 (unchanged)
    B1 gross profit 66,050; B1 profit unchanged at 23,900.
  2. (b) Carriage outwards is still an expense; it is only moved from cost of sales to the expenses section, so total costs deducted are the same.
    1 mark: still deducted / still an expense; 1 mark: only its position changes, total deductions unchanged.
  3. (c) Cost of sales includes the cost of buying goods and bringing them to their present location and condition (e.g. carriage inwards). Carriage outwards arises after the goods are sold, in distributing them, so it is a selling and distribution expense.
    1 mark: cost of sales = cost of getting goods into saleable condition/location; 1 mark: carriage outwards is a distribution cost incurred after sale.

Q14Statement of profit or loss and SOFP · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 8 marks

Omar borrowed $30,000 from a bank on 1 October 2025 at 8% interest per annum. The loan is repayable in five equal annual instalments, the first paid on 30 September 2026. Interest paid during the year ended 30 September 2026 was $1,800, and this was the only interest charged in the draft statement of profit or loss, which showed a profit of $27,400.

  1. (a) Calculate the loan interest for the year and the amount accrued at 30 September 2026. [2]
  2. (b) Show how the loan and the interest appear in the statement of financial position at 30 September 2026. [3]
  3. (c) Calculate the corrected profit for the year. [1]
  4. (d) Explain why the accrued interest must be included even though it has not been paid. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Interest $2,400; accrued $600
    B1 30,000 × 8% = 2,400 (the loan was outstanding all year); B1 accrual 600.
