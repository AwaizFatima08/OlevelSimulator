Q2Statement of profit or loss and SOFP · Core · Paper 2 style · 5 marks

  1. (a) Calculate the gross profit. [1]
  2. (b) Calculate the profit from operations. [2]
  3. (c) Calculate the profit for the year. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) $736,000
    1,840,000 − 1,104,000 = 736,000.
  2. (b) $292,000
    736,000 − 196,000 − 248,000 = 292,000. M1 A1.
  3. (c) $192,000

Q3Statement of profit or loss and SOFP · Core · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) State two items that appear in the statement of profit or loss of a limited company but not in that of a sole trader. [2]
  2. (b) Explain why dividends paid are not shown as an expense in a company's statement of profit or loss. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Any two: tax (corporation tax / income tax on company profits); directors' remuneration; auditors' fees; loan note (debenture) interest as finance costs.
    1 mark each.
  2. (b) Dividends are an appropriation (distribution) of profit to the owners, not a cost of earning profit. They are deducted from retained earnings in the statement of changes in equity.
    1 mark: appropriation/distribution to owners, not an expense; 1 mark: shown in SOCE/retained earnings.

Q102Dividends · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 9 marks

Okara Dairy Products Limited has retained earnings of $680,000. It has current assets of $312,000 (including cash $48,000) and current liabilities of $214,000. The directors want to pay a dividend of 5 cents on each of its 2,000,000 shares, as last year. They also plan to build a new cold-storage warehouse next year.

  1. (a) Calculate the current ratio before and after paying the proposed dividend, to two decimal places. [3]
  2. (b) Explain how the dividend would be paid given the bank balance. [1]
  3. (c) Evaluate whether the directors should pay the dividend. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) Before 1.46:1; after 0.99:1
    Dividend 2,000,000 × 5c = 100,000 (B1); 312,000 ÷ 214,000 = 1.46 (B1); (312,000 − 100,000) ÷ 214,000 = 0.99 (B1).
