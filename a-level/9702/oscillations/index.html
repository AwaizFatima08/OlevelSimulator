Q1Simple harmonic motion · Core · Paper 4 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Define simple harmonic motion. [2]
  2. (b) Write down the defining equation of simple harmonic motion and state the meaning of each symbol. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Motion in which the acceleration is proportional to the displacement from a fixed point and is always directed towards that point (opposite to the displacement).
    B1 a ∝ x; B1 directed towards the fixed point / opposite to x
  2. (b) a = −ω²x; a acceleration, ω angular frequency, x displacement from equilibrium.
    B1 equation; B1 symbols

Q60Damping & resonance · Challenge · Paper 4 style · 7 marks

The pendulum of a clock has period 2.0 s and swings with simple harmonic motion with a constant amplitude of 5.0 cm (measured as a horizontal displacement of the bob). The bob has mass 0.50 kg. Damping removes 1.0% of the energy of the oscillation in each cycle, and the clock's mechanism replaces it with a small push each cycle. The mechanism is driven by a 2.0 kg mass that falls slowly through 1.2 m. g = 9.81 m s⁻².

  1. (a) Explain why the pendulum needs a push every cycle to keep its amplitude constant. [1]
  2. (b) Calculate the total energy of the oscillation. [2]
  3. (c) Calculate the mean power that the mechanism must supply to the pendulum. [2]
  4. (d) Assuming all the energy from the falling mass is passed to the pendulum, calculate how many days the clock runs before the mass must be raised again. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Damping (air resistance, friction at the pivot) continually transfers energy away from the pendulum, so energy must be supplied to keep the amplitude constant.
    B1 damping removes energy each cycle, which must be replaced
  2. (b) 6.2 × 10⁻³ J
    C1 ω = 2π/T = π rad s⁻¹; A1 E = ½mω²x₀² = ½ × 0.50 × π² × 0.050² = 6.17 × 10⁻³ J
  3. (c) 3.1 × 10⁻⁵ W
    C1 energy per cycle = 0.010 × 6.17 × 10⁻³ = 6.17 × 10⁻⁵ J; A1 P = 6.17 × 10⁻⁵/2.0 = 3.1 × 10⁻⁵ W
  4. (d) 8.8 days
    C1 energy available = mgh = 2.0 × 9.81 × 1.2 = 23.5 J; A1 t = 23.5/3.08 × 10⁻⁵ = 7.6 × 10⁵ s = 8.8 days
