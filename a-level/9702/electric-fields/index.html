Q1Electric potential & potential energy · Core · Paper 4 style · 4 marks

Take ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹² F m⁻¹, e = 1.60 × 10⁻¹⁹ C, mₑ = 9.11 × 10⁻³¹ kg, mₚ = 1.67 × 10⁻²⁷ kg where needed.

  1. (a) Define electric potential at a point. [2]
  2. (b) Calculate the electric potential 0.30 m from a point charge of +5.0 nC. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) The work done per unit positive charge in bringing a small test charge from infinity to the point.
    B1 work done per unit positive charge; B1 from infinity
  2. (b) 150 V
    V = Q/(4πε₀r) = 8.99 × 10⁹ × 5.0 × 10⁻⁹/0.30 (C1) = 150 V (A1)

  1. (a) Explain what is meant by an equipotential surface. [1]
  2. (b) State the angle between field lines and equipotential surfaces, and explain why no work is done moving a charge along an equipotential. [2]

Q30Uniform electric fields & charged particles · Challenge · Paper 4 style · 4 marks

A proton and an alpha particle, both initially at rest, are accelerated through the same potential difference V.

  3. (c) Calculate the ratio (kinetic energy of proton)/(kinetic energy of alpha particle). [1]
