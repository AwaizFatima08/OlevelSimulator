Q16Group 2 solubility & thermal stability trends (A Level) · Core · Paper 4 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) State the trend in thermal stability of the Group 2 carbonates down the group. [1]
  2. (b) Explain this trend. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) increases (decomposition temperature increases)
    B1.
  2. (b) Cation radius increases down the group, so charge density decreases; the cation polarises the carbonate ion (distorts its electron cloud) less; the C–O bonds are weakened less, so more energy is needed to decompose
    B1 ionic radius of M²⁺ increases / charge density decreases. B1 less polarisation of the CO₃²⁻ anion. B1 C–O bond less weakened, so higher temperature needed. Do not credit 'polarisation of the cation'.
Q17Group 2 solubility & thermal stability trends (A Level) · Core · Paper 4 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Write an expression for the enthalpy change of solution of MgSO₄ in terms of its lattice energy and the enthalpy changes of hydration of its ions. [1]
  2. (b) Explain why the solubility of the Group 2 sulfates decreases down the group. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) ΔHsol = ΔHhyd(Mg²⁺) + ΔHhyd(SO₄²⁻) − ΔHlatt(MgSO₄)
