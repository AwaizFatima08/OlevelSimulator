  2. (b) Find the coordinates of the image S′ of S in the mirror (the reflection of S in Π). [3]
  3. (c) A camera is at C(1, −3, 2). Find the distance travelled by light that goes from S to the mirror and is reflected to C, using the shortest such path. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) 17/3 m
    M1: |10 − 2 + 12 − 3| / √9A1: 17/3 m
  2. (b) (−23/9, 52/9, −14/9)

Q11Distances (point, plane, skew lines) · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) The lines l₁ and l₂ have equationsl₁: r = i + 2k + s(i + j)l₂: r = pj + k + t(j + k)where p is a constant. Find a vector perpendicular to both lines. [2]

  1. (a) i − j + k (or any multiple)
    M1: (1, 1, 0) × (0, 1, 1)A1: (1, −1, 1)

Q30Lines & planes: intersection, parallel · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 8 marks
  1. (a) The line l has equation r = i + pj + 2k + t(2i − j + k), where p is a constant, and the plane Π₁ has equation x + 3y + z = 9.Show that l is parallel to Π₁ for every value of p. [1]
  2. (b) Find the value of p for which l lies in Π₁. [2]
  3. (c) For the value of p found in (b), the plane Π₂ contains l and is perpendicular to Π₁. Find the Cartesian equation of Π₂. [3]
  4. (d) For p = 5, find the distance between l and Π₁. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) (2, −1, 1)·(1, 3, 1) = 0
    B1: direction · normal = 2 − 3 + 1 = 0
  2. (b) p = 2
    M1: (1, p, 2) in Π₁: 1 + 3p + 2 = 9A1: p = 2
  3. (c) 4x + y − 7z = −8
