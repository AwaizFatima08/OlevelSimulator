Q1Two-sample tests for means · Core · Paper 4 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) The lifetimes of AA batteries of brand A are normally distributed with standard deviation 4.2 hours, and those of brand B are normally distributed with standard deviation 3.8 hours. A random sample of 30 brand A batteries has mean lifetime 52.3 hours, and a random sample of 25 brand B batteries has mean lifetime 50.1 hours.Test, at the 5% significance level, whether the mean lifetime of brand A batteries is greater than that of brand B batteries. [6]
Show answer
  1. (a) z = 2.038 > 1.645: reject H₀; there is sufficient evidence that brand A batteries last longer on average.
    B1: H₀: μ_A = μ_B, H₁: μ_A > μ_BM1: standard error √(4.2²/30 + 3.8²/25) = 1.080A1: z = 2.2/1.080 = 2.038B1: critical value 1.645M1: compare: 2.038 > 1.645, reject H₀A1: sufficient evidence at 5% that brand A has a greater mean lifetime
