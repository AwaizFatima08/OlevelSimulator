Q1Two projectiles · Core · Paper 3 style · 5 marks

Take g = 10 m s⁻² and ignore air resistance. Give non-exact answers correct to 3 significant figures and angles correct to 1 decimal place.

  1. (a) Particle A is projected from a point O on horizontal ground with speed 25 m s⁻¹ at an angle α above the horizontal, where tan α = ¾. At the same instant particle B is released from rest at a point vertically above the point C on the ground, where OC = 40 m. The particles collide. Find the time at which they collide. [2]
  2. (b) Find the height above the ground at which B was released, and the height at which the particles collide. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) 2 s
    M1 horizontal component 25 × 0.8 = 20 m s⁻¹, 20t = 40; A1 t = 2.
  2. (b) Released at 30 m; collision at 10 m
    M1 height of A at t = 2: 15(2) − 5(4) = 10; M1 B has fallen 5(4) = 20 m; A1 h = 30 m (note tan α = 30/40: A was aimed directly at B's starting point).
Q2Two projectiles · Core · Paper 3 style · 7 marks

Take g = 10 m s⁻² and ignore air resistance. Give non-exact answers correct to 3 significant figures and angles correct to 1 decimal place.

  1. (a) Points A and B are on horizontal ground, 60 m apart. Particle P is projected from A towards B with speed 26 m s⁻¹ at an angle θ above the horizontal, where tan θ = 12/5. At the same instant particle Q is projected from B towards A with initial velocity components 20 m s⁻¹ horizontally and 24 m s⁻¹ vertically upwards. Show that P and Q are always at the same height while both are in the air. [2]
  2. (b) Find the time at which the particles collide and the height of the collision. [3]
  3. (c) Find the speed and angle of projection of Q. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Shown
    M1 P's vertical component 26 × 12/13 = 24 m s⁻¹; A1 both have height 24t − 5t².
  2. (b) t = 2 s; 28 m
    M1 10t + 20t = 60; A1 t = 2; A1 24(2) − 5(4) = 28 m.
  3. (c) 31.2 m s⁻¹ at 50.2° above the horizontal
    M1 √(20² + 24²); A1 tan⁻¹(24/20).
Q3Two projectiles · Core · Paper 3 style · 3 marks

Take g = 10 m s⁻² and ignore air resistance. Give non-exact answers correct to 3 significant figures and angles correct to 1 decimal place.

  1. (a) Two stones are thrown horizontally, in the same direction, from the same point at the top of a tower 20 m high. One has speed 8 m s⁻¹ and the other 14 m s⁻¹. Find the time each stone takes to reach the ground. [1]
  2. (b) Find the distance between the points where the stones land. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 2 s
    B1 20 = 5t², t = 2 for both.
  2. (b) 12 m
    M1 14 × 2 − 8 × 2; A1 12 m.
Q4Two projectiles · Standard · Paper 3 style · 7 marks

Take g = 10 m s⁻² and ignore air resistance. Give non-exact answers correct to 3 significant figures and angles correct to 1 decimal place.

  1. (a) A stone A is thrown horizontally with speed 12 m s⁻¹ from the top of a vertical cliff 45 m above the sea. Find the horizontal distance from the foot of the cliff to the point where A hits the sea. [2]
  2. (b) One second after A is thrown, a second stone B is thrown from the same point with speed 20 m s⁻¹ at 30° above the horizontal, in the same vertical plane and the same horizontal direction as A. Find the distance between the points where the two stones hit the sea, and the time between their impacts. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) 36 m
    M1 45 = 5t², t = 3; A1 12 × 3 = 36.
  2. (b) 36.1 m; 2.16 s
    M1 −45 = 10t − 5t² for B; M1 t² − 2t − 9 = 0; A1 t = 1 + √10 = 4.162 s after B is thrown; M1 horizontal distance 10√3 × 4.162 = 72.09 m; A1 separation 36.1 m; B lands 5.162 s after A was thrown, i.e. 2.16 s after A lands.
Q5Two projectiles · Standard · Paper 3 style · 6 marks

Take g = 10 m s⁻² and ignore air resistance. Give non-exact answers correct to 3 significant figures and angles correct to 1 decimal place.

  1. (a) Particle P is projected vertically upwards from a point A on horizontal ground with speed 30 m s⁻¹. Find the time taken for P to reach its greatest height, and that height. [2]
  2. (b) At the instant P is projected, a particle Q is projected from a point B on the ground, 40 m from A, with speed u m s⁻¹ at an angle θ above the horizontal. Q hits P when P is at its greatest height. Find u and θ. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) 3 s; 45 m
    B1 t = 30/10; B1 30²/20 = 45.
  2. (b) u = 32.8, θ = 66.0°
    M1 horizontal: 3u cos θ = 40; M1 vertical: 3u sin θ − 45 = 45, so u sin θ = 30; A1 u = √(30² + (40/3)²) = 32.8; A1 tan θ = 2.25, θ = 66.0°.
Q6Two projectiles · Standard · Paper 3 style · 6 marks

Take g = 10 m s⁻² and ignore air resistance. Give non-exact answers correct to 3 significant figures and angles correct to 1 decimal place.

  1. (a) Particle A is projected horizontally with speed 15 m s⁻¹ from the top of a vertical tower of height 40 m. At the same instant particle B is projected from the foot of the tower with speed V m s⁻¹ at an angle θ above the horizontal, in the same vertical plane and direction as A. The particles collide. Show that V cos θ = 15. [2]
  2. (b) The collision occurs 2 s after projection. Find V, θ and the height above the ground of the collision. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) Shown
    M1 both start on the same vertical line, so equal horizontal displacements at all times; A1 V cos θ t = 15t (AG).
  2. (b) V = 25, θ = 53.1°, height 20 m
    M1 vertical separation: 40 − 5t² = V sin θ t − 5t², so V sin θ × 2 = 40; A1 V sin θ = 20; A1 V = 25, θ = tan⁻¹(4/3) = 53.1°; A1 40 − 5(4) = 20 m (A has not yet landed, since it lands at √8 ≈ 2.83 s).
Q7Two projectiles · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 7 marks

  1. (a) Two golf balls are struck from the same point O on horizontal ground, each with speed 30 m s⁻¹, at angles α and β to the horizontal, where α < β. Both land at the same point, 72 m from O. Show that sin 2α = 0.8 and find α and β. [4]
  2. (b) The ball at angle β is struck first. Find how long after it the other ball must be struck so that both land at the same instant. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) α = 26.6°, β = 63.4°
    M1 range u² sin 2θ/g = 72; A1 sin 2θ = 720/900 = 0.8 (AG); A1 α = ½ sin⁻¹ 0.8; A1 β = 90° − α.
  2. (b) 2.68 s
    M1 time of flight 2u sin θ/g = 6 sin θ; M1 sin α = 1/√5, sin β = 2/√5, so times 2.68 s and 5.37 s; A1 difference 6/√5 = 2.68 s.
Q8Two projectiles · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 8 marks

Take g = 10 m s⁻² and ignore air resistance. Give non-exact answers correct to 3 significant figures and angles correct to 1 decimal place.

  1. (a) Particle A is projected from a point O on horizontal ground with velocity components 10 m s⁻¹ horizontally and 25 m s⁻¹ vertically. One second later particle B is projected from O, in the same vertical plane, with velocity components 15 m s⁻¹ horizontally and 25 m s⁻¹ vertically. Show that the particles collide, and find the time after A is projected and the position of the collision. [5]
  2. (b) Find the angle between the directions of motion of A and B at the moment of collision. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) t = 3 s after A is projected; at 30 m horizontally from O and 30 m above the ground
    M1 horizontal: 10t = 15(t − 1); A1 t = 3; M1 A's height 25(3) − 5(9) = 30; M1 B's height 25(2) − 5(4) = 30; A1 equal, so they collide at (30, 30).
  2. (b) 45°
    M1 A's velocity (10, −5), B's velocity (15, 5); M1 angles tan⁻¹(0.5) below and tan⁻¹(1/3) above the horizontal; A1 26.57° + 18.43° = 45°.
Q9Two projectiles · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 7 marks

Take g = 10 m s⁻² and ignore air resistance. Give non-exact answers correct to 3 significant figures and angles correct to 1 decimal place.

  1. (a) Two balls are kicked at the same instant from the same point O on horizontal ground. Ball P has initial velocity components 18 m s⁻¹ horizontally and 24 m s⁻¹ vertically; ball Q, in the same vertical plane and direction, has components 24 m s⁻¹ horizontally and 7 m s⁻¹ vertically. Show that, while both are in the air, the distance between them increases at a constant rate, and find this rate. [4]
  2. (b) Find which ball lands first, and the distance between the balls at that instant. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) √325 = 18.0 m s⁻¹
    M1 positions (18t, 24t − 5t²) and (24t, 7t − 5t²); M1 difference (6t, −17t) has no t² term; A1 distance t√(6² + 17²); A1 rate √325.
  2. (b) Q, after 1.4 s; 25.2 m
    M1 flight times 2 × 24/10 = 4.8 s and 2 × 7/10 = 1.4 s; A1 Q lands first; A1 1.4√325 = 25.2 m.

Q12Equation of trajectory · Core · Paper 3 style · 5 marks

Take g = 10 m s⁻² and ignore air resistance. Give non-exact answers correct to 3 significant figures and angles correct to 1 decimal place.

  1. (a) A ball is thrown from a point O on horizontal ground at 45° above the horizontal and hits a vertical wall at a point 3 m above the ground. The wall is 12 m from O. Using the equation of the trajectory, find the speed of projection. [3]
  2. (b) Determine whether the ball is still rising when it hits the wall. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 12.6 m s⁻¹
    M1 y = x − gx²/u² for θ = 45°; M1 3 = 12 − 1440/u²; A1 u² = 160, u = 12.6.
  2. (b) No: the highest point is at x = u²/(2g) = 8 m < 12 m, so the ball is descending
    M1 dy/dx = 1 − 20x/u² at x = 12 is −0.5 (or compare with x of top); A1 descending.

  1. (a) A shot-putter's practice ball is projected from ground level with speed 25 m s⁻¹ and lands 60 m away on horizontal ground. Find the two possible angles of projection. [3]
  2. (b) Find the time of flight for each angle. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 36.9° or 53.1°
    M1 625 sin 2α/10 = 60; A1 sin 2α = 0.96; A1 2α = 73.74° or 106.26°, so α = 36.9° or 53.1°.
  2. (b) 3 s and 4 s
    M1 T = 2(25 sin α)/10 = 5 sin α with sin α = 0.6 or 0.8; A1 3 s and 4 s.

Q30Range, height & time of flight · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 7 marks

Take g = 10 m s⁻² and ignore air resistance. Give non-exact answers correct to 3 significant figures and angles correct to 1 decimal place.

  1. (a) A particle is projected from a point on horizontal ground with speed u at an angle α above the horizontal. Its greatest height is H and its range is R. Show that R = 4H cot α. [3]
  2. (b) Given that the greatest height equals the range, find tan α. For u = 20 m s⁻¹ find this height and the time of flight. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) Shown
    M1 H = u² sin² α/(2g), R = 2u² sin α cos α/g; M1 R/H = 4 cos α/sin α; A1 (AG).
  2. (b) tan α = 4; H = R = 18.8 m; time 3.88 s
    B1 tan α = 4; M1 sin² α = 16/17; A1 H = 400(16/17)/20 = 18.8; A1 T = 2(20)(4/√17)/10 = 3.88 s.
