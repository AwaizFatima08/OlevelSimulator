Q1Connected particles (pulley) · Core · Paper 4 style · 4 marks

Take g = 10 m s⁻². Particles A and B, of masses 5 kg and 3 kg, are attached to the ends of a light inextensible string which passes over a fixed smooth pulley. The particles hang vertically and are released from rest.

  1. (a) Find the acceleration of the particles and the tension in the string. [4]
