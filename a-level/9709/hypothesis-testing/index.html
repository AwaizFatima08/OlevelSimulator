  1. (a) A rice mill in Sindh fills bags with a nominal mass of 5 kg. The masses are normally distributed with standard deviation 0.08 kg. An inspector suspects that the mean mass is less than 5 kg. A random sample of 20 bags has mean mass 4.962 kg. Test at the 5% significance level whether the inspector's suspicion is justified. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) z = −2.12 < −1.645, so reject H₀: there is evidence that the mean mass is less than 5 kg.
    B1 H₀: μ = 5, H₁: μ < 5; M1 standardise with 0.08/√20; A1 z = −2.12 (or p = 0.0168); M1 compare with −1.645 (or 0.0168 < 0.05); A1 reject H₀, conclusion in context, not over-assertive.

Q4Hypothesis test (normal mean) · Core · Paper 6 style · 6 marks

A machine fills bags of rice that are nominally 2 kg. The masses of the bags are normally distributed with standard deviation 0.05 kg. A random sample of 25 bags has mean mass 2.012 kg. Test, at the 5% significance level, H₀: μ = 2 against H₁: μ ≠ 2, where μ is the population mean mass.

  1. (a) Explain why a two-tailed test is appropriate here. [1]
  2. (b) Carry out the test, giving the test statistic and the critical values. [3]
  3. (c) State your conclusion in context. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) The question is whether the mean differs from 2 kg in either direction (it could be too high or too low), so H₁: μ ≠ 2.
    B1 a difference in either direction is being tested
  2. (b) z = 1.20; critical values ±1.96
    M1 z = (x̄ − μ) ÷ (σ ÷ √n) = (2.012 − 2) ÷ (0.05 ÷ 5)A1 z = 1.20B1 two-tailed 5% critical values ±1.96
  3. (c) Since 1.2 lies between −1.96 and 1.96, H₀ is not rejected: there is no evidence at the 5% level that the mean mass of the bags differs from 2 kg.
    B1 not significant, H₀ not rejectedB1 conclusion in context (mean mass not shown to differ from 2 kg)

Q6Hypothesis test (normal mean) · Standard · Paper 6 style · 9 marks
  1. (a) The lifetimes, x hours, of a random sample of 50 batteries of a certain brand were recorded, giving Σx = 1218 and Σx² = 29 900. Calculate unbiased estimates of the population mean and variance. [3]
  2. (b) The manufacturer claims that the mean lifetime is 25 hours. Test at the 2% significance level whether the mean lifetime differs from 25 hours. [5]
  3. (c) Explain why it was not necessary to assume that the lifetimes are normally distributed. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) x̄ = 24.36; s² = 4.68 (4.684)
    B1 24.36; M1 (29900 − 1218²/50)/49; A1 4.684.
  2. (b) z = −2.09; |z| < 2.326, so do not reject H₀: insufficient evidence that the mean lifetime differs from 25 hours.

  3. (c) The sample is large, so by the Central Limit Theorem the sample mean is approximately normally distributed.
    B1.
