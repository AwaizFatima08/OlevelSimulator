  1. (a) Find ∫ (5 sin 3x + 2 sec² 2x) dx. [3]
  2. (b) Hence find the exact value of ∫₀^{π/6} (5 sin 3x + 2 sec² 2x) dx. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) −(5/3) cos 3x + tan 2x + c
    B1 −(5/3) cos 3x; B1 tan 2x (from 2 × ½ tan 2x); B1 + c with both terms correct.
  2. (b) √3 + 5/3 (≈ 3.399)
    M1 substitute both limits: (−(5/3)cos(π/2) + tan(π/3)) − (−5/3 + 0). A1 √3 + 5/3.
Q2Integration of trig functions · Core · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Express sin² 2x in terms of cos 4x. [1]
  2. (b) Hence find the exact value of ∫₀^{π/8} sin² 2x dx. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) sin² 2x = ½(1 − cos 4x)
    B1 using cos 2A ≡ 1 − 2 sin²A with A = 2x.
  2. (b) π/16 − 1/8 (≈ 0.0713)
    M1 integrate to ½(x − ¼ sin 4x); M1 apply limits, sin(π/2) = 1; A1 π/16 − 1/8.

Q5Integration of trig functions · Standard · Paper 2 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) Find ∫ (3 cos 2x − 4 sin(½x)) dx. [3]
  2. (b) Hence find the exact value of ∫₀^{π/3} (3 cos 2x − 4 sin(½x)) dx. Give your answer in the form a√3 + b. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) (3/2) sin 2x + 8 cos(½x) + c
    B1 (3/2) sin 2x; B1 +8 cos(½x) (coefficient 8 and sign); B1 + c.

    M1 substitute both limits into the result of (a); sin(2π/3) = √3/2, cos(π/6) = √3/2, cos 0 = 1; (3√3/4 + 4√3) − 8 = 19√3/4 − 8 A1.

Show answer

Q10Integration of trig functions · Standard · Paper 3 style · 5 marks

Show answer

Q11Integration of trig functions · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) By expanding sin(3x + x) and sin(3x − x), show that sin 3x cos x ≡ ½(sin 4x + sin 2x). [2]
  2. (b) Hence find the exact value of ∫₀^{π/6} sin 3x cos x dx. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) Shown
    M1 sin(3x+x) + sin(3x−x) = 2 sin 3x cos x; A1 rearrange to the given result.
  2. (b) 5/16
