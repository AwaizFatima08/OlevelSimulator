Q1Variable acceleration (calculus) · Core · Paper 4 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) A particle moves in a straight line. Its velocity t seconds after leaving a fixed point O is v m s⁻¹, where v = 6t − t² for 0 ≤ t ≤ 6. Find the acceleration of the particle when t = 2. [2]
  2. (b) Find the maximum velocity of the particle. [2]
  3. (c) Find the distance travelled by the particle in the 6 seconds. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) 2 m s⁻²
    a = dv/dt = 6 − 2t M1; = 2 m s⁻² A1
  2. (b) 9 m s⁻¹
    6 − 2t = 0 ⇒ t = 3 M1; v = 18 − 9 = 9 m s⁻¹ A1
  3. (c) 36 m
    s = 3t² − t³/3 M1 A1; 108 − 72 = 36 m A1
Q2Variable acceleration (calculus) · Core · Paper 4 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) A particle starts from rest at a point O and moves in a straight line. Its acceleration t seconds later is (4 − 2t) m s⁻². Find an expression for the velocity of the particle in terms of t. [2]
  2. (b) Find the displacement of the particle from O when it next comes to instantaneous rest. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) v = 4t − t²
    v = ∫(4 − 2t) dt = 4t − t² + c M1; v = 0 at t = 0 so c = 0 A1
  2. (b) 32/3 m (10.7 m)
    4t − t² = 0 ⇒ t = 4 B1; s = 2t² − t³/3 M1; s(4) = 32 − 64/3 = 32/3 m A1
Q3Variable acceleration (calculus) · Core · Paper 4 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) A particle P moves along a straight line so that its displacement from a fixed point O after t seconds is s m, where s = t³ − 9t² + 24t. Find the values of t when P is at instantaneous rest. [3]
  2. (b) Find the total distance travelled by P in the first 4 seconds. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) t = 2 and t = 4
    v = 3t² − 18t + 24 M1; = 3(t − 2)(t − 4) = 0 M1; t = 2, 4 A1
  2. (b) 24 m
    s(2) = 20 B1; s(4) = 16 B1; distance = 20 + (20 − 16) = 24 m A1
Q4Variable acceleration (calculus) · Core · Paper 4 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) A car moves between two sets of traffic lights in Karachi. It starts from rest and its velocity t seconds later is v m s⁻¹, where v = 0.3t² − 0.02t³. Show that the car comes to rest again when t = 15. [1]
  2. (b) Find the maximum speed of the car. [3]
  3. (c) Find the distance between the two sets of traffic lights. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) v(15) = 67.5 − 67.5 = 0
    0.3(225) − 0.02(3375) = 0 B1
  2. (b) 10 m s⁻¹
    dv/dt = 0.6t − 0.06t² = 0 M1; t = 10 A1; v = 30 − 20 = 10 m s⁻¹ A1
  3. (c) 84.375 m (84.4 m)
    s = 0.1t³ − 0.005t⁴ M1 A1; s(15) = 337.5 − 253.125 = 84.375 m A1

Q6Variable acceleration (calculus) · Standard · Paper 4 style · 9 marks
  1. (a) A particle starts from rest at O and moves in a straight line. For 0 ≤ t ≤ 4 its velocity is v = 1.5t² − 0.25t³ m s⁻¹. For t ≥ 4 it decelerates uniformly, its velocity being v = 24 − 4t, until it comes to rest. Find the maximum acceleration of the particle for 0 ≤ t ≤ 4. [3]
  2. (b) Show that the velocity is continuous at t = 4 and find the time at which the particle comes to rest. [2]
  3. (c) Find the total distance travelled by the particle. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) 3 m s⁻²
    a = 3t − 0.75t² M1; da/dt = 3 − 1.5t = 0 ⇒ t = 2 M1; a = 6 − 3 = 3 m s⁻² A1
  2. (b) v(4) = 8 in both; rest at t = 6
    1.5(16) − 0.25(64) = 8 and 24 − 16 = 8 B1; 24 − 4t = 0 ⇒ t = 6 B1
  3. (c) 24 m
    ∫₀⁴(1.5t² − 0.25t³) dt = [0.5t³ − t⁴/16] M1; = 32 − 16 = 16 A1; second stage ½ × 2 × 8 = 8 M1; total 24 m A1

Q9Variable acceleration (calculus) · Standard · Paper 4 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) A train travels between two stations. Its velocity t seconds after leaving the first station is v m s⁻¹, where v = kt(100 − t) and k is a constant; it stops at the second station when t = 100. The distance between the stations is 2500 m. Find k. [4]
  2. (b) Find the maximum speed of the train. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) k = 0.015
    ∫₀¹⁰⁰ k(100t − t²) dt = k[50t² − t³/3] M1 A1; k(500 000 − 1 000 000/3) = 2500 M1; k = 0.015 A1
  2. (b) 37.5 m s⁻¹
    Maximum at t = 50 M1; v = 0.015 × 50 × 50 = 37.5 m s⁻¹ A1
Q10Variable acceleration (calculus) · Standard · Paper 4 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) A particle starts from rest at a point O and moves in a straight line. Its velocity t seconds later is v m s⁻¹, where v = 4 − 16/(t + 2)². Find the initial acceleration of the particle. [2]
  2. (b) Find the displacement of the particle from O when t = 6. [3]
  3. (c) State the value that the velocity approaches as t becomes large. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) 4 m s⁻²
    a = 32/(t + 2)³ M1; at t = 0, a = 32/8 = 4 m s⁻² A1
  2. (b) 18 m
    s = 4t + 16/(t + 2) + c M1; s(0) = 0 ⇒ c = −8 A1; s(6) = 24 + 2 − 8 = 18 m A1
  3. (c) 4 m s⁻¹
    16/(t + 2)² → 0 so v → 4 B1
Q11Variable acceleration (calculus) · Standard · Paper 4 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) A lift in a tall building in Dubai starts from rest and moves upwards. For 0 ≤ t ≤ 2 its velocity is v = t − 0.25t² m s⁻¹. Find the velocity and the acceleration of the lift when t = 2. [2]
  2. (b) Find the distance travelled by the lift in the first 2 seconds. [2]
  3. (c) The lift then moves at constant speed 1 m s⁻¹ and finally slows down to rest, the final stage taking 2 seconds and covering 4/3 m. The lift rises 30 m in total. Find the total time of the journey. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) v = 1 m s⁻¹, a = 0
    v(2) = 2 − 1 = 1 B1; a = 1 − 0.5t = 0 at t = 2 B1
  2. (b) 4/3 m
    s = t²/2 − t³/12 M1; = 2 − 2/3 = 4/3 m A1
  3. (c) 94/3 s (31.3 s)
    Constant-speed distance = 30 − 8/3 = 82/3 m M1; time at constant speed = 82/3 s A1; total = 4 + 82/3 = 94/3 s A1
Q12Variable acceleration (calculus) · Challenge · Paper 4 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) A cyclist P passes a point O at time t = 0 and continues along a straight road at a constant speed of 9 m s⁻¹. Two seconds later a motorcyclist Q starts from rest at O and follows P along the same road. The velocity of Q is (t − 2)² m s⁻¹ for t ≥ 2, where t is the time in seconds after P passed O. Writing τ = t − 2, show that Q catches up with P when τ³ − 27τ − 54 = 0. [3]
  2. (b) Hence find the time at which Q catches up with P, the distance from O at that moment, and the speed of Q at that moment. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) τ³ − 27τ − 54 = 0
    Displacement of Q = ∫(t − 2)² dt = (t − 2)³/3 = τ³/3 M1 A1; P: 9t = 9(τ + 2); τ³/3 = 9τ + 18 ⇒ τ³ − 27τ − 54 = 0 A1
  2. (b) t = 8 s; 72 m from O; Q's speed 36 m s⁻¹
    (τ − 6)(τ + 3)² = 0 M1; τ = 6, so t = 8 s A1; distance = 9 × 8 = 72 m A1; v_Q = 6² = 36 m s⁻¹ A1

  1. (a) A particle starts from rest at O and moves in a straight line. Its velocity, in m s⁻¹, at time t s is v = 6t − 1.5t² for 0 ≤ t ≤ 4 and v = −0.5(t − 4)(10 − t) for 4 ≤ t ≤ 10. Find the greatest speed of the particle during the 10 seconds. [4]
  2. (b) Find the total distance travelled by the particle and its final distance from O. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) 6 m s⁻¹ (at t = 2)
    First stage: 6 − 3t = 0, t = 2, v = 6 M1 A1; second stage: minimum at t = 7, v = −4.5, speed 4.5 M1; greatest speed 6 m s⁻¹ A1
  2. (b) Total distance 34 m; finally 2 m from O (on the opposite side of O)
    ∫₀⁴ = [3t² − 0.5t³] = 16 M1 A1; ∫₄¹⁰ −0.5(t − 4)(10 − t) dt = −18 M1 A1; distance 16 + 18 = 34 m, displacement −2, so 2 m from O A1

Find the time at which the particle is instantaneously at rest for the first time, and the total distance travelled in the 15 seconds. [4]

  1. (a) A bus starts from rest at one stop and accelerates uniformly at a m s⁻² until its speed is 15 m s⁻¹. It travels at 15 m s⁻¹ and then decelerates uniformly at 2a m s⁻² to rest at the next stop. The journey takes 120 s and the distance between the stops is 1575 m. Show that the total time spent accelerating and decelerating is 22.5/a seconds. [2]
  2. (b) Use a velocity-time graph to find a. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) 15/a + 7.5/a = 22.5/a
    Accelerating time 15/a B1; decelerating time 15/(2a) = 7.5/a B1
  2. (b) a = 0.75
    Area = 15 × 120 − ½ × 15 × (22.5/a) M1 A1; 1800 − 168.75/a = 1575 M1; a = 0.75 A1

  1. (a) Take g = 10 m s⁻². A ball is thrown vertically upwards from ground level with speed 14 m s⁻¹. Find the greatest height reached by the ball. [2]
  2. (b) Find the time taken for the ball to return to the ground. [2]
  3. (c) Find the length of time for which the ball is more than 5 m above the ground, correct to 3 significant figures. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) 9.8 m
    0 = 14² − 20h M1; h = 9.8 m A1
  2. (b) 2.8 s
    0 = 14t − 5t² M1; t = 2.8 s A1
  3. (c) 1.96 s
    5t² − 14t + 5 = 0 M1; t = 0.420 or 2.380 A1; 1.96 s A1
Q24Vertical motion under gravity · Standard · Paper 4 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Take g = 10 m s⁻². A stone is thrown vertically upwards with speed 8 m s⁻¹ from a point at the top of a vertical sea cliff, 40 m above the sea. Find the time taken for the stone to reach the sea, correct to 3 significant figures. [3]
  2. (b) Find the speed with which the stone hits the sea, correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 3.74 s
    −40 = 8t − 5t² M1; 5t² − 8t − 40 = 0 A1; t = (8 + √864)/10 = 3.74 s A1
  2. (b) 29.4 m s⁻¹
    v² = 8² + 2 × 10 × 40 = 864 M1; v = 29.4 m s⁻¹ A1
Q25Vertical motion under gravity · Standard · Paper 4 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) Take g = 10 m s⁻². A particle A is projected vertically upwards from a point O with speed 20 m s⁻¹. One second later a particle B is projected vertically upwards from O with speed 25 m s⁻¹. Find the time after A is projected at which the two particles are at the same height. [4]
  2. (b) Find this height and the velocity of each particle at that moment. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) t = 2 s
    s_A = 20t − 5t² B1; s_B = 25(t − 1) − 5(t − 1)² B1; equate: 20t = 35t − 30 M1; t = 2 A1
  2. (b) 20 m; A at rest (0 m s⁻¹), B moving upwards at 15 m s⁻¹
    h = 40 − 20 = 20 m B1; v_A = 20 − 20 = 0 B1; v_B = 25 − 10 = 15 m s⁻¹ upwards B1
Q26Vertical motion under gravity · Challenge · Paper 4 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) Take g = 10 m s⁻². A ball P is released from rest at the top of a building 45 m high. At the same instant a ball Q is thrown vertically upwards with speed 18 m s⁻¹ from the ground directly below. Show that the balls meet 2.5 s later, and find their height above the ground when they meet. [4]
  2. (b) Show that Q is moving downwards when the balls meet, and find the speed of each ball at that moment. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) t = 2.5 s; 13.75 m
    Distance fallen by P = 5t², height of Q = 18t − 5t² M1; 5t² + 18t − 5t² = 45 M1; t = 2.5 A1; height = 45 − 5(2.5)² = 13.75 m A1
  2. (b) v_Q = −7 m s⁻¹ (downwards, speed 7); P speed 25 m s⁻¹
    Q reaches its highest point at t = 1.8 s < 2.5 s, so it is moving down B1; v_Q = 18 − 25 = −7, speed 7 m s⁻¹ B1; v_P = 10 × 2.5 = 25 m s⁻¹ B1
Q27Constant acceleration (suvat) · Core · Paper 4 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) A car slows down uniformly from 25 m s⁻¹ to 10 m s⁻¹ while travelling 105 m along a straight road. Find the deceleration of the car. [2]
  2. (b) Find the time taken. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 2.5 m s⁻²
    10² = 25² + 2a(105) M1; a = −2.5, deceleration 2.5 m s⁻² A1
  2. (b) 6 s
    10 = 25 − 2.5t M1; t = 6 s A1
Q28Constant acceleration (suvat) · Core · Paper 4 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) A cyclist passes a point A at 4 m s⁻¹ and accelerates uniformly at 0.8 m s⁻² for 10 s. Find her speed at the end of the 10 s and the distance travelled in that time. [3]
  2. (b) Find the distance she travels during the tenth second. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 12 m s⁻¹; 80 m
    v = 4 + 0.8 × 10 = 12 B1; s = 4 × 10 + ½ × 0.8 × 100 M1; = 80 m A1
  2. (b) 11.6 m
    s(9) = 36 + 32.4 = 68.4 M1; 80 − 68.4 = 11.6 m A1
Q29Constant acceleration (suvat) · Standard · Paper 4 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) A particle moves along a straight line with constant acceleration. It passes point A, then point B 3 s later, then point C a further 2 s later. AB = 24 m and BC = 26 m. Find the acceleration of the particle and its speed at A. [5]
  2. (b) Find the speed of the particle at C. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) a = 2 m s⁻²; u = 5 m s⁻¹
    A to B: 24 = 3u + 4.5a M1 A1; A to C: 50 = 5u + 12.5a M1; solving: a = 2 A1, u = 5 A1
  2. (b) 15 m s⁻¹
    v = 5 + 2 × 5 = 15 m s⁻¹ B1
Q30Constant acceleration (suvat) · Challenge · Paper 4 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) Car P passes a point O travelling at 30 m s⁻¹ and immediately decelerates uniformly at 1.5 m s⁻² until it stops. Car Q passes O 4 s after P, travelling at a constant 18 m s⁻¹ in the same direction along the same straight road. Find the time taken for P to stop and the distance from O at which it stops. [2]
  2. (b) A student writes 30t − 0.75t² = 18(t − 4) to find when Q reaches P. Explain why this equation does not give the correct answer. [2]
  3. (c) Find the time, measured from when P passes O, at which Q reaches P. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 20 s; 300 m
    0 = 30 − 1.5t, t = 20 B1; s = 30²/(2 × 1.5) = 300 m B1
  2. (b) Its positive root is t ≈ 20.6 s > 20 s, but P has already stopped at t = 20, so the formula for P's displacement no longer applies
    Solves t² − 16t − 96 = 0, t = 8 + 4√10 ≈ 20.6 M1; this is after P stops at t = 20, so the equation is invalid A1
  3. (c) 62/3 s (20.7 s)
    18(t − 4) = 300 M1; t = 4 + 50/3 = 62/3 = 20.7 s A1
