Q1Arc length & sector area · Core · Paper 1 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) A sector OAB of a circle has centre O, radius 12 cm and angle AOB = 0.85 radians. Find the length of the arc AB. [2]
  2. (b) Find the area of the sector OAB. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 10.2 cm
    s = rθ = 12 × 0.85 M1; = 10.2 cm A1
  2. (b) 61.2 cm²
    A = ½r²θ = ½ × 144 × 0.85 M1; = 61.2 cm² A1

Q21Arc length & sector area · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 8 marks
  1. (a) Two circles intersect at A and B. The first has centre P and radius 6 cm; the second has centre Q and radius 8 cm, and PQ = 10 cm. Show that angle PAQ is a right angle, and find angles APB and AQB in radians, correct to 4 significant figures. [4]

Show answer
  1. (a) APB = 1.855 rad, AQB = 1.287 rad
    6² + 8² = 10² so PAQ = 90° (converse of Pythagoras) B1; cos APQ = 6/10 M1; APB = 2 cos⁻¹ 0.6 = 1.855 A1; AQB = 2 cos⁻¹ 0.8 = 1.287 A1

Q22Arc length & sector area · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) A farmer in Sindh uses 24 m of fencing to enclose a sector-shaped pen of radius r m and angle θ radians. The fencing goes along both radii and the arc. Show that the area A m² of the pen is given by A = 12r − r². [3]
  2. (b) Find the maximum area of the pen and the value of θ for which it occurs. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) A = 12r − r²
    2r + rθ = 24 ⇒ θ = (24 − 2r)/r M1; A = ½r²θ = ½r(24 − 2r) M1; = 12r − r² A1
  2. (b) Maximum area 36 m² when r = 6 and θ = 2
    dA/dr = 12 − 2r = 0 (or complete the square: 36 − (r − 6)²) M1; r = 6, A = 36 A1; θ = (24 − 12)/6 = 2 A1
