Q1Parametric differentiation · Core · Paper 3 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) A curve is defined by the parametric equations x = t² + 3t, y = t³ − 2t. Find dy/dx in terms of t. [3]
  2. (b) Find the equation of the tangent to the curve at the point where t = 1, giving your answer in the form ax + by + c = 0. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) dy/dx = (3t² − 2)/(2t + 3)
    B1 dx/dt = 2t + 3, dy/dt = 3t² − 2M1 dy/dx = (dy/dt) ÷ (dx/dt)A1 (3t² − 2)/(2t + 3)
  2. (b) x − 5y − 9 = 0
    B1 point (4, −1)B1 gradient 1/5M1 y + 1 = (1/5)(x − 4)A1 x − 5y − 9 = 0 (any equivalent integer form)
Q2Parametric differentiation · Core · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) A curve is defined for t > 0 by x = 1 + ln(2t), y = t² + 2/t. Show that dy/dx = 2t² − 2/t. [3]
  2. (b) Find the exact coordinates of the stationary point of the curve. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) dx/dt = 1/t, dy/dt = 2t − 2/t², dy/dx = t(2t − 2/t²) = 2t² − 2/t
    B1 dx/dt = 1/tB1 dy/dt = 2t − 2/t²A1 divide to obtain the given result
  2. (b) (1 + ln 2, 3)
    M1 2t² − 2/t = 0 so t³ = 1, t = 1A1 x = 1 + ln 2, y = 3
Q3Parametric differentiation · Standard · Paper 3 style · 8 marks
  1. (a) The parametric equations of a curve are x = e²ᵗ − 1, y = t eᵗ. Show that dy/dx = (1 + t)/(2eᵗ). [4]
  2. (b) Find the exact coordinates of the point on the curve at which the tangent is parallel to the x-axis. [2]
  3. (c) Find the equation of the normal to the curve at the origin. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) dy/dx = (eᵗ + t eᵗ)/(2e²ᵗ) = (1 + t)/(2eᵗ)
    B1 dx/dt = 2e²ᵗB1 dy/dt = eᵗ + t eᵗ (product rule)M1 divide and cancel eᵗ, A1 given answer
  2. (b) (e⁻² − 1, −e⁻¹)
    M1 1 + t = 0 so t = −1A1 x = e⁻² − 1, y = −e⁻¹
  3. (c) y = −2x
    B1 origin when t = 0, gradient 1/2A1 normal gradient −2, y = −2x
Q4Parametric differentiation · Standard · Paper 3 style · 8 marks
  1. (a) A curve has parametric equations x = 4 sin θ, y = 3 cos 2θ, for −½π < θ < ½π. Show that dy/dx = −3 sin θ. [3]
  2. (b) Find the equation of the tangent to the curve at the point where θ = π/6, giving your answer in the form ax + by = c where a, b and c are integers. [3]
  3. (c) Find the Cartesian equation of the curve in the form y = f(x). [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) dy/dx = −6 sin 2θ ÷ 4 cos θ = −12 sin θ cos θ ÷ 4 cos θ = −3 sin θ
    B1 dx/dθ = 4 cos θ, B1 dy/dθ = −6 sin 2θM1 use sin 2θ = 2 sin θ cos θ and simplify to the given answer
  2. (b) 3x + 2y = 9
    B1 point (2, 3/2) and gradient −3/2M1 y − 3/2 = −3/2(x − 2)A1 3x + 2y = 9
  3. (c) y = 3 − 3x²/8
    M1 cos 2θ = 1 − 2 sin²θ = 1 − 2(x/4)²A1 y = 3 − 3x²/8
Q5Parametric differentiation · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 9 marks
  1. (a) A spiral curve is given by x = eᵗ cos t, y = eᵗ sin t, for 0 ≤ t ≤ π. Show that dy/dx = (sin t + cos t)/(cos t − sin t). [3]
  2. (b) Find the exact coordinates of the point on the curve where the tangent is parallel to the y-axis. [3]
  3. (c) Find the exact value of t at which the tangent is parallel to the x-axis, and find the equation of the tangent at the point where t = 0. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) dx/dt = eᵗ(cos t − sin t), dy/dt = eᵗ(sin t + cos t); divide
    B1 both derivatives by the product ruleM1 divide and cancel eᵗA1 given answer
  2. (b) t = π/4; (e^(π/4)/√2, e^(π/4)/√2)
    M1 cos t − sin t = 0A1 t = π/4A1 x = y = e^(π/4)/√2 (or ½√2 e^(π/4))
  3. (c) t = 3π/4; tangent at t = 0: y = x − 1
    B1 sin t + cos t = 0 gives t = 3π/4B1 point (1, 0), gradient 1B1 y = x − 1
Q6Implicit differentiation · Core · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) The equation of a curve is x² + 3xy + 2y² = 6. Show that the point (1, 1) lies on the curve and find dy/dx in terms of x and y. [3]
  2. (b) Find the equation of the tangent to the curve at (1, 1), giving your answer in the form ax + by = c. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 1 + 3 + 2 = 6; dy/dx = −(2x + 3y)/(3x + 4y)
    B1 verifyM1 differentiate 3xy as a product: 3y + 3x dy/dx, and 2y² as 4y dy/dxA1 dy/dx = −(2x + 3y)/(3x + 4y)
  2. (b) 5x + 7y = 12
    M1 gradient −5/7A1 5x + 7y = 12
Q7Implicit differentiation · Standard · Paper 3 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) A curve has equation y³ + x²y − 2x = 8. Find dy/dx in terms of x and y. [3]
  2. (b) Find the equation of the normal to the curve at the point where it crosses the y-axis. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) dy/dx = (2 − 2xy)/(3y² + x²)
    M1 implicit: 3y² dy/dx + 2xy + x² dy/dx − 2 = 0A1 correct derivative of each termA1 rearranged
  2. (b) y = 2 − 6x
    B1 x = 0 gives y³ = 8, y = 2M1 gradient at (0, 2) = 2/12 = 1/6, so normal gradient −6A1 y = 2 − 6x
Q8Implicit differentiation · Standard · Paper 3 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) The equation of a curve is 2x² − xy + y² = 28. Show that dy/dx = (y − 4x)/(2y − x). [3]
  2. (b) Find the exact coordinates of the two points on the curve at which the tangent is parallel to the x-axis. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) 4x − y − x dy/dx + 2y dy/dx = 0, so dy/dx = (y − 4x)/(2y − x)
    M1 implicit differentiation including product rule on xyA1 4x − (y + x dy/dx) + 2y dy/dx = 0A1 given answer
  2. (b) (√2, 4√2) and (−√2, −4√2)
    M1 y = 4xM1 substitute: 2x² − 4x² + 16x² = 28A1 x² = 2A1 both points
Q9Implicit differentiation · Challenge · Paper 3 style · 8 marks
  1. (a) A curve has equation x³ + y³ = 6xy. Find dy/dx in terms of x and y. [3]
  2. (b) Find the exact coordinates of the point, other than the origin, at which the tangent to the curve is parallel to the x-axis. Give your answer in the form (p∛2, q∛4) where p and q are integers. [4]
  3. (c) Use the symmetry of the equation to state the coordinates of the point where the tangent is parallel to the y-axis. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) dy/dx = (2y − x²)/(y² − 2x)
    M1 3x² + 3y² dy/dx = 6y + 6x dy/dxA1 A1 rearranged and simplified
  2. (b) (2∛2, 2∛4)
    M1 2y = x²M1 substitute: x³ + x⁶/8 = 3x³A1 x³ = 16, x = 2∛2A1 y = x²/2 = 2∛4
  3. (c) (2∛4, 2∛2)
    B1 swap x and y
Q10Stationary points (product/chain/quotient) · Core · Paper 3 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) The curve y = x³e^(−x/2) has two stationary points. Find dy/dx. [2]
  2. (b) Find the exact coordinates of the stationary point other than the origin. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) dy/dx = e^(−x/2)(3x² − ½x³)
    M1 product ruleA1 3x²e^(−x/2) − ½x³e^(−x/2)
  2. (b) (6, 216e⁻³)
    M1 3x² − ½x³ = 0, x = 6A1 y = 216e⁻³
