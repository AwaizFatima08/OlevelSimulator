Q16Strategic choice (Ansoff, decision trees, force field) · Core · Paper 3 style · 6 marks

RideNow (an invented company) is a ride-hailing app in Pakistan with 20 % market share. Appendix 1: three rivals; fares fell 10 % last year in a price war. Appendix 4: survey — 52 % of women passengers say safety is their main concern when choosing an app. Appendix 5: a new metro bus line has opened in two cities.

  1. (a) Using Porter's five forces and other strategic analysis, advise RideNow on its strategy for the next three years. [20]
Show answer
  1. (a) Five forces: rivalry is intense (three rivals, price wars — Appendix 1); buyer power is high (passengers switch apps easily, low switching costs); supplier power (drivers) is growing — drivers work for several apps and demand higher shares, and fuel costs rise; threat of substitutes (buses, metro, motorcycles, car-pooling) is moderate to high; threat of entry is moderate (capital needed for marketing, but technology is easy to copy; a foreign app is planning entry).
