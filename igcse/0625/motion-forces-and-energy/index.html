A bus on the Grand Trunk Road starts from rest at a bus stop. It accelerates uniformly to 15 m/s in 10 s, travels at 15 m/s for 40 s and then decelerates uniformly to rest in 6.0 s.

  1. (a) Calculate the acceleration of the bus during the first 10 s. [2]
  2. (b) Calculate the total distance travelled by the bus. [3]
  3. (c) Calculate the average speed of the bus for the whole journey. Give your answer to 3 significant figures. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 1.5 m/s²
    a = Δv/Δt (C1) = 15 ÷ 10 = 1.5 m/s² (A1)

  3. (c) 12.9 m/s
    average speed = total distance ÷ total time = 720 ÷ 56 (C1) = 12.9 m/s (A1)
