Q1Review · Core · Paper 1 style · 15 marks

Your class recently went to see a new family film at the cinema. Your teacher has asked you to write a review of the film for the school magazine.Here are some comments:'The story was exciting from start to finish.''Some scenes were too long and I got bored.''The special effects were amazing.''It was too expensive for a family to see.'

  1. (a) Write a review of the film for the school magazine, giving your opinion.The comments above may give you some ideas, and you should also use some ideas of your own. Write about 120 to 160 words.You will receive up to 6 marks for the content of your review, and up to 9 marks for the language used. [15]
Show answer
  1. (a) Own review of about 120–160 words (6 content + 9 language). See mark scheme.
    Possible content points (any well-developed relevant ideas are credited): brief outline of the story without spoiling the ending; what made it exciting / memorable scene; acting or characters; special effects / music; weaknesses such as long scenes or ticket price; who would enjoy it and a clear recommendation. Do not credit comments copied word for word. Content (6): 5–6 = all of the task covered with a clear verdict/recommendation, ideas well developed beyond the comments, register suits the readers of a review; 3–4 = relevant with some development, verdict present but thin, may lean on the prompts; 1–2 = partly relevant, little development, prompts copied, no clear opinion. Language (9): 8–9 = wide range of precise vocabulary (e.g. evaluative adjectives), varied complex structures used accurately, well-linked paragraphs; 5–7 = reasonable range, mostly accurate, some linking and paragraphing; 2–4 = simple vocabulary and structures, frequent errors that sometimes blur meaning, little organisation; 1 = very limited, meaning often unclear.
