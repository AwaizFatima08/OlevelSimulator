Q1Short-answer questions · Core · Paper 2 style · 2 marks

You will hear a telephone message from a dentist's receptionist. Answer the questions in a word or short phrase.Transcript (read it aloud, or read it silently)Hello, this is Saima calling from the Smile Dental Clinic for Mr Faisal Raza. I'm afraid Dr Hamid is ill this week, so we need to move your appointment from Wednesday morning. We can offer you Friday at a quarter past four in the afternoon instead. If that isn't convenient, please call us back before Thursday lunchtime. Oh, and as it's your first visit, please remember to bring your identity card and arrive ten minutes early to fill in a short form. Thank you.

  1. (a) At what time is Mr Raza's new appointment? [1]
  2. (b) What must Mr Raza bring to the appointment? [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) a quarter past four / 4.15 p.m. (on Friday)
    Do not accept 'Wednesday morning'.
  2. (b) his identity card

Q16Short-answer questions · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 2 marks

You will hear the beginning of a science podcast. Answer the questions in a word or short phrase.Transcript (read it aloud, or read it silently)Welcome to 'Curious Minds'. Today's question comes from a listener in Quetta: why do onions make us cry? When you cut an onion, you break its cells, and chemicals inside them mix to form a gas. When this gas reaches the surface of your eyes, it irritates them, and your eyes produce tears to wash it away. So what can you do? Chilling the onion in the fridge for half an hour helps, because the gas is released more slowly when it's cold. And using a sharp knife is better than a blunt one, which crushes more cells.

  1. (a) Why do our eyes produce tears when we cut onions? [1]
  2. (b) Why is a sharp knife better than a blunt one? [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) to wash away the gas / because the gas irritates them
  2. (b) a blunt knife crushes more cells (releasing more gas)
