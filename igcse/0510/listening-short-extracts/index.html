Q1Short-answer questions · Core · Paper 2 style · 2 marks

You will hear a telephone message from a dentist's receptionist. Answer the questions in a word or short phrase.Transcript (read it aloud, or read it silently)Hello, this is Saima calling from the Smile Dental Clinic for Mr Faisal Raza. I'm afraid Dr Hamid is ill this week, so we need to move your appointment from Wednesday morning. We can offer you Friday at a quarter past four in the afternoon instead. If that isn't convenient, please call us back before Thursday lunchtime. Oh, and as it's your first visit, please remember to bring your identity card and arrive ten minutes early to fill in a short form. Thank you.

  1. (a) At what time is Mr Raza's new appointment? [1]
  2. (b) What must Mr Raza bring to the appointment? [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) a quarter past four / 4.15 p.m. (on Friday)
    Do not accept 'Wednesday morning'.
  2. (b) his identity card

Choose the correct answer, A, B, C or D.Transcript (read it aloud, or read it silently)Reporter: Pupils from Grade 8 at the Government High School returned yesterday from a week at the forest reserve. Despite heavy rain on the second day, the trip was described as a success. The students planted seventy saplings and learned how to identify local birds. The headteacher said the only regret was that the coach was late on the final morning.

  1. (a) What did the headteacher regret? [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) C the coach arriving late
    The headteacher regretted only the late coach on the final morning. The rain was not a regret (the trip was still a success), and the saplings and birds were positive parts of the trip.
Q60Multiple choice (A–D) · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 1 mark

You will hear a man giving directions to a visitor. Choose the correct answer, A, B, C or D.Transcript (read it aloud, or read it silently)Man: Go straight past the mosque, then take the second turning on the left. You'll see a white gate, but don't go through it; the entrance to the library is round the side, next to the car park.

  1. (a) Where is the entrance to the library? [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) C beside the car park
    The entrance is round the side, next to the car park. The white gate is a distractor: he says not to go through it. The turning on the left is the second one, not the entrance itself.
