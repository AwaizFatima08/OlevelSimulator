Q1Recounting a recent experience · Core · Paper 1 style · 15 marks
  1. (a) You recently took part in a cooking competition at your school.Write an email to a friend about the competition.In your email:• Explain why you decided to enter the competition.• Describe what you cooked and how you prepared it.• Say what you learned from the experience.Write about 120 to 160 words. Show answer
  1. (a) Model answer (one of many acceptable): Hi Ali, Guess what? Last Friday I entered the school cooking competition! Halfway through I realised I'd forgotten the salt, so I was really nervous when the judges tasted it! In the end I came third, which I'm proud of. The biggest lesson was that planning matters: if I'd written a list of steps, I wouldn't have panicked. Next year I'm going to practise with a timer. Why don't you enter with me? Write soon, Hamza
