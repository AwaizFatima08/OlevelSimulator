You will hear a talk about the history of chocolate. Complete the notes. Write no more than three words or a number in each gap.Transcript (read it aloud, or read it silently)Chocolate is made from the seeds of the cacao tree, which grows only in hot, wet regions near the equator. The seeds, often called beans, grow inside large pods that sprout directly from the tree's trunk.Some of the earliest people to use cacao lived in Central America thousands of years ago. The Maya and later the Aztecs made a drink from ground cacao beans mixed with water and chilli. It was bitter, not sweet, and was usually drunk by rulers and warriors. Cacao beans were so valuable that the Aztecs used them as money.Spanish explorers brought cacao to Europe in the sixteenth century. Europeans added sugar and later milk, and drinking chocolate became fashionable among the rich. Solid chocolate bars only appeared in the nineteenth century, when new machines made it possible to press cacao into a smooth, solid form.Today, most of the world's cacao is grown not in the Americas but in West Africa, especially Côte d'Ivoire and Ghana. Many cacao farmers earn very little, and some people choose to buy chocolate with a Fairtrade label, which aims to guarantee farmers a fairer price.

  6. (f) Solid bars made possible by new ______ [1]
  7. (g) Main growing region today: ______ [1]
  8. (h) Fairtrade aims to give farmers a fairer ______ [1]

In recent years, camel milk has also become popular in cities because it contains about three times as much vitamin C as cow's milk.

Keep them away from heaters, which dry the air.If you're a beginner, start with an easy plant. The snake plant and the money plant are both very forgiving. They can survive in low light, and they won't die if you forget them for a couple of weeks.Finally, wipe dusty leaves with a damp cloth now and then. Dust blocks light and stops the plant from making food.
