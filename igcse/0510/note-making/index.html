You are going to give a talk to your class about rooftop gardens. Read the article. Then complete the notes below. Write one short note on each bullet point. Your notes should be brief, and must come from the text.Growing food above the cityIn Karachi, more and more families are turning the flat roofs of their houses into small vegetable gardens. Saima Qureshi, a retired teacher in Gulshan-e-Iqbal, started with three plastic buckets of tomatoes. Today her roof has twenty containers of chillies, spinach, mint and bitter gourd.Saima says the biggest benefit is the money she saves. 'Vegetable prices go up every month, but my spinach costs me almost nothing,' she explains. She also knows exactly what is in her food, because she uses no chemical sprays. The plants have another effect, too: the leaves shade the concrete, so the rooms below stay noticeably cooler in summer.Rooftop gardening is not always easy, however. Water is the main difficulty, because the city often has shortages and plants on a hot roof dry out quickly. Saima collects the water she uses for washing vegetables and pours it on her plants. Strong sea winds can also break young plants, so she ties them to wooden sticks. Pests are a further problem: pigeons and crows peck at ripe tomatoes, so she covers them with old fishing nets. Finally, soil is heavy, and some older roofs cannot carry the weight of many large pots. Saima advises beginners to ask a builder to check their roof before they start.
