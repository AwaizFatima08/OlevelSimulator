Q1Air pollutants & catalytic converters · Core · Paper 2 style · 1 mark
  1. (a) What is the main source of sulfur dioxide in the air? [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) B the combustion of fossil fuels that contain sulfur compounds
    Fuels such as coal and some oils contain sulfur compounds, which burn to form SO₂. C is the source of oxides of nitrogen, not sulfur dioxide.
Q2Air pollutants & catalytic converters · Core · Paper 4 style · 5 marks

Traffic in large cities such as Karachi releases several air pollutants.

  1. (a) Carbon monoxide: give its source and one adverse effect. [2]
  2. (b) Particulates: give one source and one adverse effect. [2]
  3. (c) Oxides of nitrogen: give one adverse effect. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) Incomplete combustion of carbon-containing fuels; toxic (reduces the blood's ability to carry oxygen).
    B1 source; B1 effect.
  2. (b) Incomplete combustion of carbon-containing fuels (e.g. diesel engines, burning crop waste); increased risk of respiratory problems and cancer.
    B1 source; B1 effect.
  3. (c) Acid rain / photochemical smog / respiratory problems.
    B1 any one.
Q3Air pollutants & catalytic converters · Standard · Paper 4 style · 6 marks

Catalytic converters are fitted to car exhausts.

  1. (a) Explain how nitrogen monoxide, NO, is formed in a car engine. [2]
  2. (b) Complete and balance the equation for the reaction in the catalytic converter: __CO + __NO → __CO₂ + N₂ [2]
  3. (c) In this reaction, state which substance is reduced. Explain your answer. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Nitrogen and oxygen from the air react at the high temperature inside the engine.
    B1 N₂ + O₂ from air; B1 high temperature.
  2. (b) 2CO + 2NO → 2CO₂ + N₂
    B1 products; B1 balanced.
  3. (c) Nitrogen monoxide; it loses oxygen (to form N₂).
    B1 NO; B1 loses oxygen / oxidation number of N decreases from +2 to 0.
Q4Air pollutants & catalytic converters · Standard · Paper 4 style · 6 marks

A coal-fired power station releases sulfur dioxide.

  1. (a) Explain how sulfur dioxide from the power station leads to acid rain. [2]
  2. (b) Give two effects of acid rain. [2]
  3. (c) Flue gas desulfurisation uses calcium oxide. Write the equation for the reaction of calcium oxide with sulfur dioxide to form calcium sulfite. [1]
  4. (d) Give one other way to reduce sulfur dioxide emissions. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) Sulfur dioxide dissolves in rainwater / reacts with water and oxygen to form sulfuric acid.
    B1 dissolves in water; B1 acid (sulfurous/sulfuric) forms.
  2. (b) Damages buildings and statues made of limestone/marble; corrodes metal structures; harms trees; makes lakes acidic and kills fish.
    1 mark each for any two.
  3. (c) CaO + SO₂ → CaSO₃
    B1
  4. (d) Use low-sulfur fuels / remove sulfur from fuels before burning.
    B1

Q6Air pollutants & catalytic converters · Standard · Paper 4 style · 5 marks

Each winter Lahore suffers from smog. Sources include vehicle exhausts, factories and the burning of rice straw on farms.

  1. (a) Name two pollutants released by vehicles, factories and burning straw that cause breathing (respiratory) problems during smog. [2]
  2. (b) Burning rice straw in a limited supply of air produces carbon monoxide. Explain why carbon monoxide forms. [1]
  3. (c) Suggest two actions that could reduce smog in the city. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Any two of: particulates; oxides of nitrogen; sulfur dioxide.
    1 mark each for any two of: particulates; oxides of nitrogen (NO/NO₂); sulfur dioxide. Do not accept carbon dioxide.
  2. (b) Incomplete combustion: there is not enough oxygen to convert all the carbon into carbon dioxide.
    B1
  3. (c) Ban crop-residue burning / use the straw for animal feed or compost; fit catalytic converters; use public transport; control factory emissions; switch to cleaner fuels.
    1 mark each for any two.
Q7Air pollutants & catalytic converters · Challenge · Paper 4 style · 6 marks

Catalytic converters also oxidise unburned hydrocarbons such as octane. 1 mol of gas occupies 24 dm³ at room temperature and pressure.

  1. (a) Balance the equation for the complete combustion of octane in the converter: __C₈H₁₈ + __O₂ → __CO₂ + __H₂O [2]
  2. (b) A car without a converter releases 2.4 dm³ of carbon monoxide per km at room temperature and pressure. Calculate the mass of carbon dioxide formed when this carbon monoxide is fully oxidised. Mr CO₂ = 44. [3]
  3. (c) Catalytic converters do not reduce the release of carbon dioxide. Explain why. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) 2C₈H₁₈ + 25O₂ → 16CO₂ + 18H₂O
    B1 16 and 18; B1 2 and 25.
  2. (b) 4.4 g
    M1 mol CO = 2.4 ÷ 24 = 0.10; M1 2CO + O₂ → 2CO₂ so mol CO₂ = 0.10; A1 × 44 = 4.4 g.
  3. (c) They convert CO and unburned hydrocarbons into carbon dioxide (and water), so CO₂ output is not reduced (may increase).
    B1
Q8Air pollutants & catalytic converters · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 1 mark
  1. (a) Which statements about catalytic converters are correct?1 Carbon monoxide is oxidised to carbon dioxide.2 Oxides of nitrogen are reduced to nitrogen.3 They reduce the amount of carbon dioxide released.4 They contain transition-metal catalysts such as platinum and rhodium. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) B 1, 2 and 4
    1, 2 and 4 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong: converters turn CO and hydrocarbons into CO₂, so they do not reduce carbon dioxide output.

  2. (b) Calculate the mass of sulfur dioxide formed when this sulfur burns. S + O₂ → SO₂ [2]
  3. (c) Calculate the minimum mass of calcium oxide needed to remove all of this sulfur dioxide. CaO + SO₂ → CaSO₃ [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 20 tonnes
    B1
  2. (b) 40 tonnes

  3. (c) 35 tonnes

Q12Climate change, carbon cycle & photosynthesis · Standard · Paper 4 style · 5 marks

The increase in greenhouse gases in the atmosphere is linked to climate change.

  1. (a) Explain how greenhouse gases cause the Earth's surface to warm. [3]
  2. (b) Give two possible effects of climate change on a country such as Pakistan. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Thermal energy (infrared radiation) is emitted by the Earth's surface; greenhouse gases absorb this energy; they re-emit it in all directions, including back towards the Earth, so less thermal energy escapes to space.
    B1 Earth emits thermal/infrared energy; B1 absorbed by greenhouse gases; B1 re-emitted back / reduced heat loss to space.
  2. (b) Melting glaciers and more floods; more extreme heatwaves; droughts and water shortages; lower crop yields; rising sea levels affecting coastal areas.
    1 mark each for any two.

Q14Climate change, carbon cycle & photosynthesis · Standard · Paper 4 style · 5 marks

Q15Climate change, carbon cycle & photosynthesis · Challenge · Paper 4 style · 5 marks

6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂. Mr: CO₂ = 44, C₆H₁₂O₆ = 180. 1 mol of gas occupies 24 dm³ at room temperature and pressure.

  1. (a) Name one process in the carbon cycle that adds carbon dioxide to the atmosphere and one process that removes it. [2]
  2. (b) Calculate the mass of glucose made when 132 g of carbon dioxide is used in photosynthesis. [2]
  3. (c) Calculate the volume of oxygen released, in dm³ at room temperature and pressure. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) Adds: respiration or combustion (or decomposition); removes: photosynthesis.
    B1 each.
  2. (b) 90 g
    M1 mol CO₂ = 132 ÷ 44 = 3.0; mol glucose = 3.0 ÷ 6 = 0.50; A1 × 180 = 90 g.
  3. (c) 72 dm³
    B1 3.0 mol O₂ × 24 = 72 dm³.
Q16Climate change, carbon cycle & photosynthesis · Challenge · Paper 4 style · 5 marks

Biogas plants in rural Punjab collect methane from animal dung. Mr: CH₄ = 16, CO₂ = 44.

  1. (a) Write the balanced equation for the complete combustion of methane. [2]
  2. (b) Calculate the mass of carbon dioxide, in kg, formed when 1.0 kg of methane burns completely. Give your answer to 3 significant figures. [2]
  3. (c) Methane is a stronger greenhouse gas than carbon dioxide. Suggest why burning methane collected from a landfill site is better for the climate than letting it escape. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O
    B1 formulae; B1 balanced.
  2. (b) 2.75 kg
    M1 mol CH₄ = 1000 ÷ 16 = 62.5 = mol CO₂; A1 62.5 × 44 = 2750 g = 2.75 kg.
  3. (c) It converts methane into carbon dioxide, which has a smaller greenhouse effect per molecule (and the energy can replace another fuel).
    B1

Q27Composition of air · Challenge · Paper 6 style · 6 marks

  1. (a) Calculate the percentage of oxygen in the air from these results. [2]
  2. (b) Explain why the water rises up the tube. [2]
  3. (c) Suggest why the apparatus is left for a week before the final reading. [1]
  4. (d) Suggest one reason why the result is slightly lower than 21%. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) 20.8%
    M1 (50.0 − 39.6) = 10.4 cm³; A1 10.4 ÷ 50.0 × 100 = 20.8%.

    B1 oxygen used in rusting; B1 volume of gas falls.
  3. (c) Rusting is slow; time is needed for all the oxygen to be used up.
    B1
  4. (d) Not all the oxygen had reacted / the temperature or pressure changed / reading error.
    B1
