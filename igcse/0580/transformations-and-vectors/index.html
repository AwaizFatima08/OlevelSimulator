Q20Transformations · Challenge · Paper 2 style · no calculator · 5 marks

Do not use a calculator in this question. Triangle L has vertices A(2, 2), B(4, 2) and C(2, 4).

  1. (a) Triangle L is enlarged by scale factor −2 with centre (1, 1). Write down the coordinates of the image vertices A′, B′ and C′. [3]
  2. (b) Calculate the area of the image triangle, and state the ratio of the area of the image to the area of triangle L. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) A′(−1, −1), B′(−5, −1), C′(−1, −5)
    M1 use P′ = centre + k(P − centre) with k = −2; M1 check one vertex, e.g. A′ = (1, 1) + (−2)(1, 1); A1 all three vertices.
  2. (b) Area of image = 8 square units; ratio 8 : 2 = 4 : 1 (area scale factor = (−2)² = 4)
    M1 image sides are twice as long as the original sides, so area is multiplied by (−2)² = 4; A1 image area 8 square units.
