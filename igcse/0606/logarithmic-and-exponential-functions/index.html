Q4Logarithmic and exponential equations · Core · Paper 2 style · calculator · 5 marks

A cup of tea in a Lahore café cools so that its temperature, T °C, t minutes after it is poured is given by T = 22 + 68e^(−0.04t).

  1. (a) Find the temperature of the tea when it is poured. [1]
  2. (b) Find the time taken for the tea to cool to 50 °C. Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures. [3]
  3. (c) State the temperature that the tea approaches as t becomes very large. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) 90 °C
    22 + 68 = 90 B1
  2. (b) 22.2 minutes
    68e^(−0.04t) = 28 M1; −0.04t = ln(28/68) M1; t = 22.2 A1
  3. (c) 22 °C
    B1

Q16Logarithmic and exponential equations · Challenge · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 5 marks

Q17Logarithmic and exponential equations · Challenge · Paper 2 style · calculator · 6 marks

The mass, m grams, of a radioactive substance in a hospital laboratory is given by m = 80e^(−kt), where t is the time in days and k is a positive constant. After 12 days the mass is 40 g.

  1. (a) Show that k = (ln 2)/12. [2]
  2. (b) Find the mass after 30 days, correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
  3. (c) Find the time taken for the mass to fall to 5 g. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Shown
    40 = 80e^(−12k) → e^(−12k) = ½ M1; −12k = ln ½ = −ln 2 A1
  2. (b) 14.1 g
    80e^(−30 ln2/12) = 80 × 2^(−2.5) M1; = 14.1 A1
  3. (c) 48 days
    e^(−kt) = 1/16 M1; t = 12 ln 16/ln 2 = 48 A1

Q22Laws of logarithms and change of base · Core · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 6 marks

It is given that logₐ 2 = p and logₐ 3 = q.

  1. (a) Express logₐ 18 in terms of p and q. [2]
  2. (b) Express logₐ(8/9) in terms of p and q. [2]
  3. (c) Express logₐ(a√6) in terms of p and q. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) p + 2q
    18 = 2 × 3² M1 A1
  2. (b) 3p − 2q
    B2 (B1 for one term)
  3. (c) 1 + ½(p + q)
    logₐ a = 1 B1; ½ logₐ 6 B1
Q23Laws of logarithms and change of base · Standard · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 4 marks
  1. (a) Use the change of base rule to show that log₉ x = ½ log₃ x. [2]
  2. (b) Do not use a calculator in this part.Hence solve log₃ x + log₉ x = 6. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Shown
    log₉ x = log₃ x/log₃ 9 M1; log₃ 9 = 2 A1
  2. (b) x = 81
    (3/2) log₃ x = 6 → log₃ x = 4 M1; x = 81 A1
Q24Laws of logarithms and change of base · Standard · Paper 2 style · calculator · 4 marks
  1. (a) Use the change of base rule to evaluate log₇ 50, giving your answer correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
  2. (b) Evaluate log₀.₅ 10 correct to 3 significant figures, and explain why the answer is negative. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 2.01
    lg 50/lg 7 M1; = 2.01 A1
  2. (b) −3.32; negative because 0.5 raised to a positive power is less than 1, so a power giving 10 must be negative
    lg 10/lg 0.5 = −3.32 B1; reason B1
Q25Laws of logarithms and change of base · Challenge · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 3 marks

Q28Graphs of exponential and log functions · Standard · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 5 marks
  1. (a) For the curve y = ln(x + 2) + 1, state the equation of the asymptote. [1]
  2. (b) Find the exact coordinates of the points where the curve meets the axes. [3]
  3. (c) Describe the shape of the curve. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = −2
    B1
  2. (b) (0, 1 + ln 2) and (e⁻¹ − 2, 0)
    x = 0: y = ln 2 + 1 B1; ln(x + 2) = −1 M1; x = e⁻¹ − 2 A1
  3. (c) Increasing for all x > −2, with gradient decreasing; it approaches x = −2 downwards
    B1
Q29Graphs of exponential and log functions · Standard · Paper 2 style · calculator · 6 marks
  1. (a) The curve y = 3e⁻ˣ + 1 is drawn for all real x. State the coordinates of the y-intercept, the equation of the asymptote and the range of y. [3]
  2. (b) By considering the graphs of y = 3e⁻ˣ + 1 and y = x, explain why the equation 3e⁻ˣ + 1 = x has exactly one real root, and state an interval of the form n < x < n + 1 (n an integer) in which it lies. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) (0, 4); y = 1; y > 1
    B1 B1 B1
  2. (b) One root, between x = 1 and x = 2
    y = 3e⁻ˣ + 1 decreases, y = x increases, so they cross once B1; at x = 1: 3e⁻¹ + 1 = 2.10 > 1; at x = 2: 3e⁻² + 1 = 1.41 < 2 M1; root in 1 < x < 2 A1
Q30Graphs of exponential and log functions · Challenge · Paper 2 style · calculator · 6 marks

The curve C has equation y = 2 ln(3x − 4), for x > 4/3.

  1. (a) State the equation of the asymptote of C. [1]
  2. (b) Find the coordinates of the point where C crosses the x-axis. [2]
  3. (c) Find the value of x for which y = 4, giving your answer in exact form and correct to 3 significant figures. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 4/3
    B1
  2. (b) (5/3, 0)
    3x − 4 = 1 M1; x = 5/3 A1
  3. (c) x = (e² + 4)/3 = 3.80
    ln(3x − 4) = 2 M1; 3x − 4 = e² M1; x = (e² + 4)/3 = 3.80 A1
