Q1Linear form (log graphs) · Core · Paper 2 style · calculator · 4 marks
  1. (a) The variables x and y are related by y = Abˣ, where A and b are constants. When lg y is plotted against x, a straight line is obtained that passes through (1, 0.9) and (5, 2.1). Find the value of A and the value of b, giving each correct to 2 significant figures. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) A = 4.0, b = 2.0
    lg y = lg A + x lg b B1; gradient lg b = 1.2 ÷ 4 = 0.3, b = 10^0.3 = 2.0 M1 A1; intercept lg A = 0.9 − 0.3 = 0.6, A = 10^0.6 = 4.0 A1
Q2Linear form (log graphs) · Core · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 4 marks
  1. (a) When ln y is plotted against x, a straight line is obtained passing through the points (2, 5) and (6, 13). Find ln y in terms of x. [2]
  2. (b) Hence express y in terms of x. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) ln y = 2x + 1
    gradient = 8 ÷ 4 = 2 M1; ln y = 2x + 1 A1
  2. (b) y = e^(2x + 1) = e·e^(2x)
    y = e^(2x+1) M1 A1
Q3Linear form (log graphs) · Core · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 5 marks
  1. (a) The variables x and y are such that when xy is plotted against x², a straight line is obtained passing through (1, 7) and (5, 19). Express y in terms of x. [4]
  2. (b) Find the value of y when x = 2. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) y = 3x + 4/x
    gradient = 12 ÷ 4 = 3 M1; xy = 3x² + 4 A1; divide by x M1; y = 3x + 4/x A1
  2. (b) y = 8
    6 + 2 = 8 B1
Q4Linear form (log graphs) · Core · Paper 2 style · calculator · 5 marks
  1. (a) The variables x and y are related by y = Axⁿ. When ln y is plotted against ln x, a straight line of gradient −0.5 is obtained, passing through the point (2, 3). Find the value of n and the exact value of A. [3]
  2. (b) Find the value of y when x = 4, giving your answer correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) n = −0.5, A = e⁴
    ln y = n ln x + ln A B1; n = −0.5 B1; ln A = 3 + 0.5(2) = 4, A = e⁴ B1
  2. (b) y = 27.3
    y = e⁴ × 4^(−0.5) M1 = 27.3 A1

  2. (b) Find the value of y when x = 100. [1]
Show answer

    10⁶ ÷ 10⁴ = 100 B1

Q7Linear form (log graphs) · Standard · Paper 2 style · calculator · 6 marks
  1. (a) The variables x and y are related by y = Axⁿ, where A and n are constants. When lg y is plotted against lg x, a straight line is obtained that passes through the points (0.4, 1.3) and (1.2, 2.9). Find the value of n and the value of A, giving A correct to 3 significant figures. [4]
  2. (b) Find the value of y when x = 5. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) n = 2, A = 3.16
    lg y = n lg x + lg A B1; n = (2.9 − 1.3) ÷ (1.2 − 0.4) = 2 M1 A1; lg A = 1.3 − 2(0.4) = 0.5, A = 10^0.5 = 3.16 A1
  2. (b) y = 79.1
    y = 3.162 × 5² M1 = 79.1 A1 (3 s.f.)
Q8Linear form (log graphs) · Standard · Paper 2 style · calculator · 7 marks

The table shows experimental values of x and y, which are believed to be related by y = kxⁿ, where k and n are constants.x: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10y: 7.1, 20.0, 36.7, 56.6, 79.1

  1. (a) Calculate the values of lg x and lg y for each pair of readings, giving values correct to 2 decimal places. [2]
  2. (b) Plot lg y against lg x and draw a straight line. Use your graph to estimate the value of n and the value of k. [4]
  3. (c) Estimate the value of y when x = 5. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) lg x: 0.30, 0.60, 0.78, 0.90, 1.00; lg y: 0.85, 1.30, 1.56, 1.75, 1.90
    B2 all correct (B1 for at least 7 correct values)
  2. (b) n ≈ 1.5, k ≈ 2.5 (accept n 1.45–1.55, k 2.4–2.6)
    lg y = n lg x + lg k B1; gradient = (1.90 − 0.85) ÷ (1.00 − 0.30) ≈ 1.5 M1 A1; intercept lg k ≈ 0.40, k ≈ 2.5 A1
  3. (c) y ≈ 28 (accept 27–29)
    2.5 × 5^1.5 = 27.95 B1
Q9Linear form (log graphs) · Standard · Paper 2 style · calculator · 6 marks

In a laboratory in Karachi the number N of bacteria in a sample is counted every 2 hours. It is believed that N = N₀e^(kt), where t is the time in hours and N₀ and k are constants.t: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8N: 150, 302, 608, 1225, 2467

  1. (a) Explain why a graph of ln N against t should be a straight line. [1]
  2. (b) Using the first and last readings, estimate the value of k and the value of N₀. [3]
  3. (c) Estimate the time at which the number of bacteria first reaches 5000. Give your answer correct to 1 decimal place. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) ln N = ln N₀ + kt, which has the form Y = mX + c
    B1 taking logs correctly
  2. (b) k ≈ 0.35, N₀ ≈ 150
    gradient = (ln 2467 − ln 150) ÷ 8 = 0.350 M1 A1; intercept ln N₀ = ln 150, N₀ = 150 A1
  3. (c) t ≈ 10.0 hours
    ln 5000 = ln 150 + 0.35t M1; t = 10.0 A1
Q10Linear form (log graphs) · Standard · Paper 2 style · calculator · 6 marks

The value, V rupees, of a car in Lahore t years after it was bought is modelled by V = pqᵗ, where p and q are constants. When lg V is plotted against t, a straight line is obtained passing through (1, 6.42) and (5, 6.10).

  1. (a) Find the value of p and the value of q, giving p correct to 3 significant figures and q correct to 3 decimal places. [4]
  2. (b) Find the age of the car when its value is Rs 1 500 000. Give your answer correct to 1 decimal place. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) q = 0.832, p = 3 160 000 (3.16 × 10⁶)
    lg V = lg p + t lg q B1; lg q = (6.10 − 6.42) ÷ 4 = −0.08 M1, q = 0.832 A1; lg p = 6.42 + 0.08 = 6.50, p = 3.16 × 10⁶ A1
  2. (b) t = 4.0 years
    lg 1 500 000 = 6.50 − 0.08t M1; t = 4.0 A1
Q11Linear form (log graphs) · Standard · Paper 2 style · calculator · 7 marks

It is thought that the variables x and y are related by y = a ln x + b. Values recorded are:x: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16y: 2.0, 4.08, 6.16, 8.24, 10.32

  1. (a) State which two variables should be plotted to obtain a straight line, and say how a and b can be found from the line. [2]
  2. (b) Find the values of a and b. [3]
  3. (c) Find the value of x for which y = 9, correct to 3 significant figures. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Plot y against ln x; gradient = a, intercept on the y-axis = b
    B1 variables; B1 gradient a, intercept b
  2. (b) a = 3, b = 2
    ln x values 0, 0.693, 1.386, 2.079, 2.773 M1; gradient = (10.32 − 2.0) ÷ 2.773 = 3.00 A1; intercept b = 2 A1
  3. (c) x = 10.3
    ln x = 7/3 M1; x = 10.3 A1
Q12Linear form (log graphs) · Challenge · Paper 2 style · calculator · 7 marks

The table shows values of x and y which are related by y = a ÷ (x + b), where a and b are constants.x: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5y: 3.0, 2.4, 2.0, 1.714, 1.5

  1. (a) Show that, if y = a ÷ (x + b), then a graph of 1/y against x is a straight line, and state the gradient and intercept in terms of a and b. [2]
  2. (b) Use the table to find the value of a and the value of b. [4]
  3. (c) Find the value of x for which y = 0.8. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) 1/y = (1/a)x + b/a; gradient 1/a, intercept b/a
    invert: 1/y = (x + b)/a M1; gradient 1/a, intercept b/a A1
  2. (b) a = 12, b = 3
    1/y values: 0.333, 0.417, 0.5, 0.583, 0.667 M1; gradient = 1/12 so a = 12 M1 A1; intercept 0.25 = b/12 so b = 3 A1
  3. (c) x = 12
    12 ÷ (x + 3) = 0.8 gives x + 3 = 15, x = 12 B1
Q13Linear form (log graphs) · Challenge · Paper 2 style · calculator · 7 marks
  1. (a) The variables x and y are related by y = Ae^(bx²). When ln y is plotted against x², a straight line passes through (1, 2.2) and (4, 0.7). Find the value of b and the value of A, giving A correct to 3 significant figures. [4]
  2. (b) Find the positive value of x for which y = 5, giving your answer correct to 3 significant figures. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) b = −0.5, A = 14.9
    ln y = ln A + bx² B1; b = (0.7 − 2.2) ÷ 3 = −0.5 M1 A1; ln A = 2.7, A = e^2.7 = 14.9 A1
  2. (b) x = 1.48
    ln 5 = 2.7 − 0.5x² M1; x² = 2.181 A1; x = 1.48 A1
Q14Linear form (log graphs) · Challenge · Paper 2 style · calculator · 7 marks

A space agency records the orbital radius r (in millions of km) and the orbital period T (in days) of five moons of a distant planet. It is believed that T = krⁿ.r: 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0T: 1.13, 3.2, 5.88, 9.05, 16.63

  1. (a) By plotting lg T against lg r, find the value of n and the value of k, each correct to 2 significant figures. [5]
  2. (b) Another moon of the same planet has an orbital period of 12 days. Estimate its orbital radius. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) n = 1.5, k = 3.2
    T = kr^n so lg T = n lg r + lg k B1; lg r: −0.301, 0, 0.176, 0.301, 0.477; lg T: 0.053, 0.505, 0.769, 0.957, 1.221 M1; gradient ≈ 1.5 M1 A1; intercept 0.505 so k = 3.2 A1
  2. (b) r ≈ 2.4 × 10⁶ km
    12 = 3.2r^1.5 M1; r = 3.75^(2/3) = 2.41 A1

Q21Lines, midpoints and distances · Challenge · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 6 marks

The points A(−2, 1), B(4, 3), C(6, 9) and D(0, 7) are the vertices of a quadrilateral ABCD.

  1. (a) Show that ABCD is a rhombus. [3]
  2. (b) Find the area of ABCD. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) All four sides equal √40 (and diagonals bisect each other at (2, 5))
    AB = √(6² + 2²) = √40, BC = √(2² + 6²) = √40 M1; CD = √40, DA = √40 A1; all sides equal so rhombus A1
  2. (b) 32 square units
    AC = 8√2, BD = 4√2 M1 A1; area = ½ × 8√2 × 4√2 = 32 A1
