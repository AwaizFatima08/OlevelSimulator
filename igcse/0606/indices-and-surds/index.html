Q11Surds · Standard · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 4 marks
  1. (a) Given that (a + b√2)² = 27 − 10√2, where a and b are integers and a > 0, find a and b. Hence write √(27 − 10√2) in the form p + q√2. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) a = 5, b = −1; √(27 − 10√2) = 5 − √2
    M1 a² + 2b² = 27 and 2ab = −10; M1 solve; A1 a = 5, b = −1; A1 5 − √2 (positive since 5 > √2)

Q14Surds · Challenge · Paper 2 style · calculator · 6 marks
