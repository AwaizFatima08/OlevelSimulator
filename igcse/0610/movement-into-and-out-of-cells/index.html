Q15Osmosis & water potential · Standard · Paper 4 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) Sliced strawberries are sprinkled with sugar and left in a bowl for 30 minutes. A red juice collects in the bottom of the bowl. Explain, in terms of water potential, why juice collects. [3]
