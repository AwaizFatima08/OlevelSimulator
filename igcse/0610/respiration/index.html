  1. (a) What are the products of anaerobic respiration in yeast?A lactic acid onlyB carbon dioxide and waterC ethanol and carbon dioxideD lactic acid and carbon dioxide [1]

  1. (a) C ethanol and carbon dioxide
    Yeast respires anaerobically to ethanol (alcohol) and carbon dioxide. Lactic acid is the product in human muscle; carbon dioxide and water are the products of aerobic respiration.

    B1.
Q3Anaerobic respiration & oxygen debt · Core · Paper 4 style · 2 marks
  1. (a) State two differences between aerobic and anaerobic respiration in humans. [2]

Q6Anaerobic respiration & oxygen debt · Standard · Paper 2 style · 1 marks
  1. (a) After vigorous exercise, what happens to the lactic acid that has built up in the muscles?A It is converted to glucose in the muscles and stored.B It is carried in the blood to the liver and broken down by aerobic respiration.C It is excreted unchanged by the kidneys.D It is converted to ethanol in the liver. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) B It is carried in the blood to the liver and broken down by aerobic respiration.

  1. (a) less oxygen enters the blood (less haemoglobin oxygenated); less oxygen reaches muscle cells; less aerobic respiration so less energy released; more anaerobic respiration, lactic acid builds up causing fatigue
    1 mark each for any four: less oxygen absorbed into blood; less delivered to muscles; less aerobic respiration in mitochondria; less energy available for contraction; muscles respire anaerobically; lactic acid builds up / oxygen debt / fatigue.
