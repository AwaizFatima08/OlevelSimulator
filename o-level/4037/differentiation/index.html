  1. (a) Find the equation of the tangent to the curve at the point where x = 1. [3]
  2. (b) Show that this tangent touches the curve at only one point. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) y = x − 1
    At x = 1, y = 0 so the point is (1, 0); dy/dx = 6x − 5 = 1 at x = 1. M1 differentiate, M1 use point and gradient, A1 y = x − 1.

Q10Tangents and normals · Standard · Paper 2 style · 8 marks

Q15Tangents and normals · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 5 marks

The curve C has equation y = 1/(x + 2) for x ≠ −2. A point P on C has x-coordinate a, where a ≠ −2.

  1. (a) Show that the gradient of C at P is −1/(a + 2)². [2]
  2. (b) Show that the tangent to C at P does not pass through the point (−2, 0). [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) dy/dx = −(x + 2)⁻² = −1/(x + 2)², so the gradient at P is −1/(a + 2)².
    M1 differentiate (x + 2)⁻¹ using the chain rule; A1 result.

    M1 tangent equation from part (a); M1 substitute x = −2 and simplify to 2/(a + 2); A1 never zero, so the point is never on the tangent.

  1. (a) The base has sides 24 − 2x and the height is x, so V = x(24 − 2x)² = x(576 − 96x + 4x²) = 4x³ − 96x² + 576x.

  1. (a) 2πr² + 2πrh = 96π gives rh = 48 − r², so V = πr²h = πr(48 − r²) = 48πr − πr³.

Show answer
  1. (a) x = 6, maximum profit Rs 32 000

  2. (b) Show that the volume V cm³ of the cylinder is V = 12π(r² − r³/5). [1]

Show answer
  1. (a) D = (x − 0)² + (x² − 3)² = x² + x⁴ − 6x² + 9 = x⁴ − 5x² + 9.
