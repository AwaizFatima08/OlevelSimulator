Q1Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Core · Paper 1 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Solve the simultaneous equations y = x + 1 and x² + y² = 25. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 3, y = 4 and x = −4, y = −3
    x² + (x + 1)² = 25 M1; x² + x − 12 = 0 A1; x = 3 or −4 M1 (factorise and substitute back); y = 4, −3 A1
Q2Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Core · Paper 1 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Solve the simultaneous equations x + y = 7 and xy = 12. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 3, y = 4 and x = 4, y = 3
    x(7 − x) = 12 M1; x² − 7x + 12 = 0 A1; x = 3 or 4 A1; corresponding y A1
Q3Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Core · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Find the coordinates of the points where the line y = 2x − 3 meets the curve y = x² − 4x + 5. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) (2, 1) and (4, 5)
    x² − 4x + 5 = 2x − 3 M1; x² − 6x + 8 = 0 A1; x = 2, 4 A1; y = 1, 5 A1
Q4Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Core · Paper 1 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Solve the simultaneous equations 2x + y = 5 and x² + y² = 10. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 1, y = 3 and x = 3, y = −1
    y = 5 − 2x, x² + (5 − 2x)² = 10 M1; 5x² − 20x + 15 = 0 A1; x = 1 or 3 A1; y = 3, −1 A1
Q5Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Core · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Solve the simultaneous equations x − y = 2 and x² − 2y² = 7. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 3, y = 1 and x = 5, y = 3
    (y + 2)² − 2y² = 7 M1; y² − 4y + 3 = 0 A1; y = 1 or 3 A1; x = 3, 5 A1
Q6Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Core · Paper 1 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Two positive numbers differ by 3 and their product is 40. Find the two numbers. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) 8 and 5
    x − y = 3 and xy = 40 B1; y(y + 3) = 40 M1; y² + 3y − 40 = 0 A1; (y + 8)(y − 5) = 0, y = 5 (reject −8), x = 8 A1
Q7Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Core · Paper 1 style · 3 marks
  1. (a) Solve the simultaneous equations 3x + 2y = 12 and 5x − 3y = 1. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 2, y = 3
    eliminate y: 9x + 6y = 36 and 10x − 6y = 2 M1; 19x = 38, x = 2 A1; y = 3 A1
Q8Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Core · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) A cinema in Karachi sells adult tickets at Rs 800 and child tickets at Rs 500. For one show, 45 tickets were sold for a total of Rs 30 000. Find the number of adult tickets and the number of child tickets sold. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) 25 adult tickets and 20 child tickets
    a + c = 45 B1; 800a + 500c = 30 000 B1; 300a = 7500 M1; a = 25, c = 20 A1
Q9Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Core · Paper 1 style · 3 marks
  1. (a) Solve the simultaneous equations 4x − 3y = 18 and 2x + 5y = −4. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 3, y = −2
    4x + 10y = −8 M1; subtracting, −13y = 26 so y = −2 A1; x = 3 A1
Q10Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Standard · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) The line y = 3 − x meets the curve x² + xy + y² = 7 at the points A and B. Find the coordinates of A and B. [4]
  2. (b) Find the exact length of AB. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) (1, 2) and (2, 1)
    x² + x(3 − x) + (3 − x)² = 7 M1; x² − 3x + 2 = 0 A1; x = 1, 2 A1; y = 2, 1 A1
  2. (b) √2
    √(1² + 1²) = √2 B1
Q11Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Standard · Paper 1 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) The line 2y = x + 4 meets the curve xy = 6 at the points P and Q. Find the coordinates of P and Q. [4]
  2. (b) Find the coordinates of the midpoint of PQ and the length of PQ in the form k√5. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) (2, 3) and (−6, −1)
    x(x + 4)/2 = 6 M1; x² + 4x − 12 = 0 A1; x = 2 or −6 A1; y = 3, −1 A1
  2. (b) midpoint (−2, 1); PQ = 4√5
    midpoint B1; √(8² + 4²) = √80 M1; 4√5 A1
Q12Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Standard · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) A rectangular photo frame has a perimeter of 46 cm and a diagonal of length 17 cm. Taking the sides as x cm and y cm, write down two equations and solve them to find the dimensions of the frame. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) 15 cm by 8 cm
    x + y = 23 B1; x² + y² = 289 B1; x² + (23 − x)² = 289 M1; x² − 23x + 120 = 0 A1; x = 15 or 8, so 15 cm by 8 cm A1
Q13Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Standard · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) A farmer in Multan fences a rectangular plot of area 1200 m² against a straight wall. The wall forms one long side, and the other three sides use 100 m of fencing. Find the two possible pairs of dimensions of the plot. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) width 20 m, length 60 m or width 30 m, length 40 m
    2w + l = 100 B1; wl = 1200 B1; w(100 − 2w) = 1200 M1; w² − 50w + 600 = 0 A1; w = 20, l = 60 or w = 30, l = 40 A1
Q14Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Standard · Paper 1 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Solve the simultaneous equations xy = 12 and x² + y² = 25. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) (3, 4), (4, 3), (−3, −4), (−4, −3)
    y = 12/x, x² + 144/x² = 25 M1; x⁴ − 25x² + 144 = 0 A1; x² = 9 or 16 M1; x = ±3, ±4 A1; matching y values A1
Q15Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Standard · Paper 1 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Find the exact coordinates of the points where the line x + y = 5 meets the curve y = x² − x − 3. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) (2√2, 5 − 2√2) and (−2√2, 5 + 2√2)
    x² − x − 3 = 5 − x M1; x² = 8 A1; x = ±2√2 A1; y = 5 ∓ 2√2 A1
Q16Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Standard · Paper 2 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) The line y = x − 4 meets the circle x² + y² − 6x + 4y = 12 at the points C and D. Find the coordinates of C and D. [4]
  2. (b) Find the length of the chord CD in the form k√2. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) (6, 2) and (−1, −5)
    x² + (x − 4)² − 6x + 4(x − 4) = 12 M1; x² − 5x − 6 = 0 A1; x = 6, −1 A1; y = 2, −5 A1
  2. (b) 7√2
    √(7² + 7²) M1; 7√2 A1
Q17Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Standard · Paper 1 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Solve the simultaneous equations 4x² − y² = 39 and 2x + y = 13. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 4, y = 5
    (2x − y)(2x + y) = 39 M1; 2x − y = 3 A1; adding: 4x = 16 M1; x = 4, y = 5 A1
Q18Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Standard · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Two square plots of land in a village have a total area of 1300 m². The side of the larger plot is 10 m longer than the side of the smaller plot. Find the length of the side of each plot. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) 30 m and 20 m
    x² + y² = 1300 and x − y = 10 B1 B1; (y + 10)² + y² = 1300 M1; y² + 10y − 600 = 0 A1; y = 20 (reject −30), x = 30 A1
Q19Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Standard · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Find the coordinates of the points of intersection of the line y = 5 − 2x and the curve y = 3/x. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) (1, 3) and (1.5, 2)
    x(5 − 2x) = 3 M1; 2x² − 5x + 3 = 0 A1; x = 1, 1.5 A1; y = 3, 2 A1
Q20Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Standard · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) In a market model for school bags, the price p rupees and the quantity q thousand bags satisfy p = 1700 − 20q² (demand) and p = 200 + 200q (supply). Find the equilibrium quantity and price. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) q = 5 (5000 bags), p = Rs 1200
    1700 − 20q² = 200 + 200q M1; q² + 10q − 75 = 0 A1; (q − 5)(q + 15) = 0, q = 5 (reject −15) A1; p = 1200 A1
Q21Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Solve the simultaneous equations lg x + lg y = 2 and x − y = 15. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 20, y = 5
    lg(xy) = 2 so xy = 100 M1 A1; y(y + 15) = 100 M1; y² + 15y − 100 = 0, y = 5 or −20 A1; reject −20 (log undefined), x = 20 A1

    x + y = 5 B1; xy = 6 B1; x(5 − x) = 6 M1; x² − 5x + 6 = 0, x = 2 or 3 A1; y = 3, 2 A1
Q23Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 5 marks

  1. (a) x = 4, y = 8 and x = 8, y = 4
    log₂(xy) = 5 M1; xy = 32 A1; x(12 − x) = 32 M1; x² − 12x + 32 = 0, x = 4 or 8 A1; y = 8, 4 A1
Q24Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) The line y = 2x + 1 meets the curve y² = 8x + 1 at the points A and B. Find the coordinates of A and B. [4]
  2. (b) Find the equation of the perpendicular bisector of AB, giving your answer in the form ax + by = c, where a, b and c are integers. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) (0, 1) and (1, 3)
    (2x + 1)² = 8x + 1 M1; 4x² − 4x = 0 A1; x = 0, 1 A1; y = 1, 3 A1
  2. (b) 2x + 4y = 9
    midpoint (1/2, 2) B1; gradient −1/2 M1; y − 2 = −½(x − ½), i.e. 2x + 4y = 9 A1
Q25Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Solve the simultaneous equations y = x − 1 and x² + 2xy − y² = 7. [4]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 2, y = 1 and x = −2, y = −3
    x² + 2x(x − 1) − (x − 1)² = 7 M1; 2x² − 1 = 7 A1; x = ±2 A1; y = 1, −3 A1
Q26Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) A cyclist rides 60 km at a constant speed of v km/h, taking t hours. If her speed were 5 km/h greater, the journey would take 1 hour less. Write down two equations in v and t and solve them to find v and t. [6]
Show answer
  1. (a) v = 15 km/h, t = 4 hours
    vt = 60 B1; (v + 5)(t − 1) = 60 B1; −v + 5t − 5 = 0 M1; t(5t − 5) = 60 M1; t² − t − 12 = 0, t = 4 (reject −3) A1; v = 15 A1
Q27Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Solve the simultaneous equations 1/x + 1/y = 9/20 and x + y = 9. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 4, y = 5 and x = 5, y = 4
    (x + y)/(xy) = 9/20 M1; xy = 20 A1; x(9 − x) = 20 M1; x² − 9x + 20 = 0, x = 4 or 5 A1; y = 5, 4 A1
Q28Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Challenge · Paper 1 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Solve the simultaneous equations eˣ + eʸ = 5 and x + y = ln 6, giving your answers in exact form. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = ln 2, y = ln 3 and x = ln 3, y = ln 2
    eˣeʸ = 6 M1 A1; let u = eˣ: u + 6/u = 5, u² − 5u + 6 = 0 M1; u = 2 or 3 A1; x = ln 2, y = ln 3 or x = ln 3, y = ln 2 A1
Q29Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 6 marks
  1. (a) The line 2x − y = 4 meets the curve y² = 4x at the points A and B. Find the coordinates of A and B. [4]
  2. (b) Find the area of triangle OAB, where O is the origin. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) (4, 4) and (1, −2)
    x = (y + 4)/2 so y² = 2(y + 4) M1; y² − 2y − 8 = 0 A1; y = 4, −2 A1; x = 4, 1 A1
  2. (b) 6
    ½|4 × (−2) − 4 × 1| M1; 6 A1
Q30Linear and non-linear simultaneous equations · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Solve the simultaneous equations log₃ x − log₃ y = 1 and x² − y² = 72. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 9, y = 3
    log₃(x/y) = 1 M1; x = 3y A1; 9y² − y² = 72 M1; y = 3 (reject −3 as logs undefined) A1; x = 9 A1
