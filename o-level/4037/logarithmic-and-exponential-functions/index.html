Q60Graphs of exponential and log functions · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Show that the equation eˣ + x = 3 has exactly one real root, and find that root correct to 2 decimal places. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) x = 0.79
