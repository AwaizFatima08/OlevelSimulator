Q15Velocity and position vectors · Standard · Paper 2 style · 7 marks
  1. (a) Two boats A and B move on a lake, with position vectors measured in km and t in hours: r_A = (2i + 3j) + t(i − j) and r_B = (8i − 3j) + t(−i + 2j). Show that the position vector of B relative to A is (6 − 2t)i + (3t − 6)j. [2]
  2. (b) Find the time at which the boats are closest together and the minimum distance between them, correct to 3 significant figures. [5]
Show answer
  1. (a) (8 − t − 2 − t)i + (−3 + 2t − 3 + t)j = (6 − 2t)i + (3t − 6)j
    Subtract r_A from r_B and simplify each component. M1, A1.
  2. (b) t = 2.31 h; minimum distance 1.66 km
    d² = (6 − 2t)² + (3t − 6)² = 13t² − 60t + 72. Minimum at t = 60/26 = 30/13 = 2.31 h. d²min = 72 − 60²/52 = 2.769, so d = 1.66 km. M1 for d², M1 expansion, M1 minimum time, A1 time, A1 distance.

Q19Velocity and position vectors · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 8 marks
  1. (a) At time t hours, ship A has position vector (1 + 2t)i + (4 − t)j km and ship B has position vector (9 − 3t)i + (2 + t)j km, relative to the same origin. Find the position vector of B relative to A at time t. [2]
