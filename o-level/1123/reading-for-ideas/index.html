Read the text below.Growing more than vegetablesWhen Gulshan Girls' School turned a dusty corner of its playground into a vegetable garden three years ago, few teachers expected much. Watering during the summer holidays depends on a rota of volunteer families, and one year the whole crop dried out when the rota broke down. That lesson lasts longer than any test score.'

  1. (a) According to the text, give two ways the garden is used in lessons. [2]

  2. (b) For: lessons become practical and memorable (biology, maths); students who find classrooms difficult can succeed; it is self-funding through sales; teaches respect for food and less waste. Against: needs reliable watering in holidays (crop failed once); takes time away from exam preparation; costs for fencing and tools; depends on volunteers. A developed answer might support it but suggest a firm holiday rota and limit garden time in exam years.
