Q1Money & interest · Core · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 2 marks
  1. (a) Ali buys 3 notebooks at Rs 85 each and 2 pens at Rs 40 each. Work out the total cost. [1]
  2. (b) He pays with a Rs 500 note. How much change does he receive? [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) Rs 335
    3 × 85 + 2 × 40 = 255 + 80 = 335 B1
  2. (b) Rs 165
    500 − 335 = 165 B1 (ft their (a))
Q2Money & interest · Standard · Paper 2 style · calculator · 5 marks
  1. (a) Hassan invests Rs 240 000 at 6.5% per year simple interest. Work out the interest he earns in 4 years. [2]
  2. (b) Sana invests Rs 240 000 at 6% per year compound interest. Work out the value of her investment after 4 years. Give your answer to the nearest rupee. [2]
  3. (c) Whose investment is worth more after 4 years, and by how much? [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) Rs 62 400
    240 000 × 6.5 × 4 ÷ 100 M1; 62 400 A1
  2. (b) Rs 302 994
    240 000 × 1.06⁴ M1; 302 994.47… → 302 994 A1
  3. (c) Sana's, by Rs 594
    302 994 − (240 000 + 62 400) = 594 B1 (ft)
Q3Money & interest · Challenge · Paper 2 style · calculator · 6 marks
  1. (a) A sum of Rs 500 000 is invested at r% per year compound interest. After 3 years it is worth Rs 595 508. Find r. [3]
  2. (b) The investment continues at the same rate. After how many complete years is it first worth more than Rs 700 000? [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) r = 6
    (595 508 ÷ 500 000) = 1.191016 M1; ∛1.191016 = 1.06 M1; r = 6 A1
  2. (b) 6 years
    500 000 × 1.06ⁿ > 700 000 M1; 1.06⁵ × 500 000 = 669 113, 1.06⁶ × 500 000 = 709 260 M1 (both values seen); 6 A1

Q5Percentages · Standard · Paper 2 style · calculator · 4 marks
  1. (a) A shopkeeper buys a ceiling fan for Rs 8 400 and sells it for Rs 10 290. Work out his percentage profit. [2]
  2. (b) In a sale, the selling price of Rs 10 290 is reduced by 12%. Work out the sale price. [2]
Show answer

  2. (b) Rs 9 055.20

Q6Percentages · Challenge · Paper 2 style · calculator · 5 marks
  1. (a) After an increase of 15%, the monthly rent of a flat is Rs 52 900. Work out the monthly rent before the increase. [2]
  2. (b) The following year the rent of Rs 52 900 is decreased by 15%. Work out the overall percentage change from the original rent in part (a), stating whether it is an increase or a decrease. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) Rs 46 000
    52 900 ÷ 1.15 M1; 46 000 A1
  2. (b) 2.25% decrease
    1.15 × 0.85 = 0.9775 (or 52 900 × 0.85 = 44 965) M1; (46 000 − 44 965)/46 000 × 100 M1; 2.25% decrease A1

Q8Fractions & decimals · Standard · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 4 marks
  1. (a) Write 0.375 as a fraction in its simplest form. [1]

Q9Fractions & decimals · Challenge · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 5 marks
  1. (a) Write the recurring decimal 0.454545… (the digits 45 repeat) as a fraction in its simplest form. Show your working. [2]
  2. (b) Write the recurring decimal 0.1666… (only the 6 repeats) as a fraction in its simplest form. Show your working. [2]
  3. (c) Hence write 0.454545… + 0.1666… as a single fraction in its simplest form. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) 5/11
    x = 0.4545…, 100x = 45.4545…, 99x = 45 M1; 45/99 = 5/11 A1
  2. (b) 1/6
    10x = 1.666…, 100x = 16.666…, 90x = 15 M1; 15/90 = 1/6 A1
  3. (c) 41/66
    30/66 + 11/66 = 41/66 B1
Q10Sets & Venn diagrams · Core · Paper 1 style · no calculator · 3 marks
