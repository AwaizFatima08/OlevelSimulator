  1. (a) Calculate the resistance R of the wire for each length, using R = V/I. [2]

    B1 yes; B1 justification using two pairs or constant ratio
  3. (c) 3.15 Ω
    0.042 × 75 = 3.15 Ω B1

Q5Graph & data analysis · Core · Paper 4 style · 6 marks

A student heats crushed ice in a beaker and records the temperature every 30 s.t / s: 0, 30, 60, 90, 120, 150, 180, 210, 240θ / °C: −10, −4, 0, 0, 0, 0, 3, 7, 11

  1. (a) State the melting point of the ice. [1]
  2. (b) State the time interval during which the ice was melting. [1]
  3. (c) Explain why the temperature does not change while the ice melts, even though the heater is on. [2]
  4. (d) Calculate the average rate of temperature rise from 150 s to 240 s. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 0 °C
    B1
  2. (b) From 60 s until between 150 s and 180 s (the temperature stays at 0 °C while the ice melts).
    B1 melting starts at 60 s and ends somewhere between 150 s and 180 s (the temperature is 0 °C throughout; it is 3 °C at 180 s)

    B1 energy used to change state; B1 kinetic energy (temperature) unchanged
  4. (d) 0.12 °C/s
    M1 (11 − 0) ÷ (240 − 150); A1 0.12 °C/s

    B1 curve, gradient decreasing; B1 approaches room temperature
