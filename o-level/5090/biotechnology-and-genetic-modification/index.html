Q1Microorganisms in biotechnology & fermenters · Core · Paper 1 style · 1 mark
  1. (a) Why does bread dough rise when yeast is added and the dough is left in a warm place? [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) B Yeast respires and produces carbon dioxide, which forms bubbles in the dough.
    Yeast respires the sugars in the dough, releasing carbon dioxide gas that is trapped as bubbles. Ethanol (A) is made but it evaporates during baking and does not make the dough rise.
Q2Microorganisms in biotechnology & fermenters · Core · Paper 1 style · 1 mark
  1. (a) Which enzyme is added to crushed fruit to increase the volume of juice extracted? [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) C pectinase
    Pectinase breaks down pectin in plant cell walls, so the cells release more juice and the juice is clearer. Amylase (A) digests starch, not the cell wall material that holds juice in.

Q4Microorganisms in biotechnology & fermenters · Core · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) Explain why bread dough containing yeast is left in a warm place before baking. [2]
  2. (b) State what happens to the yeast and the ethanol when the bread is baked. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Warmth gives a temperature near the optimum for yeast enzymes; so yeast respires faster and produces more carbon dioxide, making the dough rise faster.
    B1 enzymes near optimum temperature; B1 faster respiration/more CO₂
  2. (b) The yeast is killed by the high temperature; the ethanol evaporates.
    1 mark each

Q13Microorganisms in biotechnology & fermenters · Standard · Paper 1 style · 1 mark
  1. (a) An industrial fermenter has a jacket of cold water flowing around it. What is the main reason for this? [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) B to remove heat released by the respiring microorganisms and keep the optimum temperature
    Respiration by large numbers of microorganisms releases heat, which would denature their enzymes; the cold water jacket removes it. C is wrong because the jacket cools rather than heats.

Q48Genetic modification · Standard · Paper 2 style · 4 marks
  1. (a) One sticky end on the cut plasmid has the unpaired bases 5' to 3': AGTC. State the sequence of unpaired bases, written 5' to 3', on the sticky end of the human gene that pairs with it. [1]
  2. (b) Explain why the human gene and the plasmid must have complementary sticky ends. [2]
  3. (c) Name the enzyme that seals the joins. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) 5' to 3': GACT
    Pairing is A with T, G with C, T with A, C with G. The partner overhang is the reverse complement of AGTC, which is GACT written 5' to 3'. Accept GACT if the direction is given correctly; TCAG (written 3' to 5') is the same bases.

    1 mark each.
  3. (c) DNA ligase
    B1.
