Q73Codominance & blood groups · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 7 marks

Two babies were born in a hospital on the same night and their name tags were lost. Mr and Mrs Ahmed are both blood group O. Mrs Khan is blood group A and Mr Khan is blood group B. Baby X is blood group A and baby Y is blood group O.

  1. (a) Explain which baby belongs to the Ahmed family and which belongs to the Khan family. [4]
  2. (b) State the genotype that Mr Khan must have for baby X to be his child. [1]
  3. (c) Explain why blood groups alone could not have solved the problem if both couples had been group AB. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Mr and Mrs Ahmed are both group O (ii), so all their children must be ii, group O; baby Y (group O) is theirs. Baby X (group A) cannot be the Ahmeds' child, so it is the Khans' baby; the Khans can have a group A child (Iᴬi) if Mrs Khan passes on Iᴬ and Mr Khan (Iᴮi) passes on i.
    1 mark: Ahmeds both ii; 1 mark: can only have group O children so baby Y; 1 mark: baby X to Khans; 1 mark: Khans can produce Iᴬi if Mr Khan carries i.
  2. (b) Iᴮi
    Baby X must receive i from the group B father and Iᴬ from the mother; 1 mark.
  3. (c) AB × AB can produce children of groups A, B and AB; so both babies could belong to either couple.
    1 mark: same possible groups for both couples; 1 mark: no way to tell them apart.
Q74Codominance & blood groups · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 6 marks

In cattle, the allele for red hair (Cᴿ) and the allele for white hair (Cᵂ) are codominant. Heterozygous cattle are roan. A roan bull was mated with many white cows. There were 58 calves: 31 roan and 27 white.

  1. (a) Draw a genetic diagram to show the expected genotypes and phenotypes of the calves, with their ratio. [3]
  2. (b) Calculate the expected number of roan calves out of 58. [1]
  3. (c) The farmer says the roan bull must be pure-breeding. Use the results to explain why he is wrong. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Parents CᴿCᵂ (roan) × CᵂCᵂ (white); gametes Cᴿ and Cᵂ from the bull, Cᵂ only from the cows; calves CᴿCᵂ roan : CᵂCᵂ white = 1 : 1.
    1 mark: parental genotypes; 1 mark: gametes; 1 mark: offspring genotypes, phenotypes and 1 : 1 ratio.
  2. (b) 29
    A1 58 × 1/2 = 29.
  3. (c) Roan cattle are heterozygous (CᴿCᵂ), so they cannot be pure-breeding; the bull passed Cᵂ to the white calves and Cᴿ to the roan calves, so he produces two kinds of gamete.
    1 mark: roan = heterozygous; 1 mark: bull gives both alleles (white and roan calves) so not homozygous.
