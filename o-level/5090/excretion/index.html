Show answer

Q54Kidney & nephron (option) · Standard · Paper 2 style · 3 marks
  1. (a) Explain how ultrafiltration takes place in the glomerulus. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) the afferent arteriole is wider than the efferent arteriole, so blood pressure is high in the glomerulus; this forces small molecules such as water, glucose, salts and urea through the capillary walls into Bowman's capsule; proteins and blood cells are too large and stay in the blood
    1 mark each: high pressure from arteriole diameter; small molecules forced out; large proteins and cells retained

    1 mark each for any three: salt pumped out; low water potential in medulla; water leaves collecting duct by osmosis
