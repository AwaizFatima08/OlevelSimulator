Q9Energy profiles & activation energy · Standard · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) A mixture of hydrogen and oxygen can be kept at room temperature without reacting, but it explodes when a spark is applied. Explain this using the idea of activation energy. [2]
  2. (b) The reaction is 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O and it is strongly exothermic. Describe the reaction pathway diagram for this reaction. Say where reactants and products are and how ΔH and the activation energy are shown. [3]
Show answer
  1. (a) At room temperature too few colliding molecules have energy equal to or greater than the activation energy, so the rate is negligible. The spark gives molecules enough energy to exceed the activation energy; the energy released then supplies other molecules.
    B1 particles at room temperature do not have energy ≥ Ea; B1 spark supplies the activation energy (then the reaction sustains itself).
  2. (b) Reactants (2H₂ + O₂) on the left at a higher level; a curve rising to a peak then falling to products (2H₂O) at a lower level. Activation energy: arrow from reactants up to the peak. ΔH: arrow from reactants down to products (negative).
    B1 products below reactants; B1 Ea from reactant level to top of curve; B1 ΔH arrow from reactants down to products.
