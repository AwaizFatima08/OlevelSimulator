Q2Air quality & pollutants · Core · Paper 1 style · 1 marks
  1. (a) A faulty gas heater in a closed room produces a colourless, odourless, toxic gas. What is the gas?A carbon dioxideB carbon monoxideC nitrogen dioxideD sulfur dioxide [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) B carbon monoxide
    Incomplete combustion of the fuel in a shortage of oxygen gives carbon monoxide, which is toxic and has no colour or smell. Sulfur dioxide and nitrogen dioxide are toxic but have strong smells.

Q5Air quality & pollutants · Standard · Paper 2 style · 4 marks

Q6Air quality & pollutants · Standard · Paper 2 style · 4 marks

Q8Air quality & pollutants · Standard · Paper 1 style · 1 marks
  1. (a) Which gas in car exhaust is NOT removed by a catalytic converter?A carbon dioxideB carbon monoxideC nitrogen monoxideD unburned hydrocarbons [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) A carbon dioxide

Q9Air quality & pollutants · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 5 marks

A brick kiln burns 1000 kg of coal that contains 1.6 % sulfur by mass. All the sulfur is converted to sulfur dioxide: S + O₂ → SO₂[Ar: O, 16; S, 32; Ca, 40]

  2. (b) Calculate the mass of sulfur dioxide produced. [2]
  3. (c) Calculate the mass of calcium oxide needed to remove all of this sulfur dioxide (CaO + SO₂ → CaSO₃). [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) 16 kg

  2. (b) 32 kg

  3. (c) 28 kg

Q16Water (treatment, tests, pollution) · Standard · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Excess nitrate fertiliser washes from farmland into a lake. Describe how this can lead to the death of fish. [4]

Show answer
  1. (a) Nitrates cause rapid growth of algae (algal bloom) on the surface; algae block light so plants below die; algae and plants die and are decomposed by bacteria; the bacteria use up dissolved oxygen, so fish die (deoxygenation).

    B1

Q21Climate change & carbon cycle · Standard · Paper 2 style · 5 marks
  1. (a) Explain how greenhouse gases cause the Earth's surface to warm up. [3]
  2. (b) State two possible effects of climate change. [2]
Show answer
  1. (a) Thermal energy from the Sun warms the Earth's surface, which emits infrared radiation; greenhouse gases absorb this infrared radiation; they re-emit it in all directions, including back towards the Earth, so less escapes to space and the surface warms.
    Earth's surface emits infrared/thermal energy B1; greenhouse gases absorb it B1; re-emit it back towards Earth (less escapes to space) B1
  2. (b) Any two of: rising sea levels; melting glaciers/ice caps; more frequent floods, droughts or heatwaves; changes in crop yields; loss of habitats.
    1 mark each
