Q4Transition elements · Core · Paper 1 style · 1 mark
  1. (a) Which property is shown by transition elements but NOT by Group I metals? [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) C They have high densities.
    Transition elements are dense (e.g. iron about 7.9 g/cm³) while Group I metals have low densities (lithium, sodium and potassium float on water). A, B and D are true of all metals, so they do not distinguish the two groups.

Melting points (°C): lithium 181; sodium 98; potassium 63; rubidium 39; caesium 28.

  1. (a) Describe the trend in melting point down Group I. [1]
  2. (b) Explain the trend in terms of metallic bonding. [3]
  3. (c) Francium is at the bottom of Group I. Predict whether it is solid or liquid at 30 °C. Explain using the data. [1]
Show answer
  1. (a) decreases
    B1
  2. (b) ions get larger down the group; delocalised electrons are further from the nuclei of the positive ions; weaker attraction between ions and delocalised electrons so less energy is needed to melt
    B1 each
  3. (c) liquid: its melting point is predicted to be below 28 °C, so it is molten at 30 °C
    B1 melting point falls down the group, so it is below 28 °C and therefore liquid at 30 °C
