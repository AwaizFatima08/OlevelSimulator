Q14Polymers (addition, condensation, proteins) · Challenge · Paper 2 style · 5 marks

PVC is made from chloroethene, CH₂=CHCl.[Ar: H, 1; C, 12; Cl, 35.5]

  1. (a) Calculate the relative formula mass of the repeat unit –CH₂–CHCl–. [1]
