(a) Yasir, his wife Sumayya and their son 'Ammar were among the earliest Muslims and had no powerful tribe to protect them; they were tortured by Abu Jahl and others of Banu Makhzum in the heat of Makka; the Prophet (pbuh) passed by and told them to be patient as their promised place was Paradise; Sumayya was killed by Abu Jahl, becoming the first martyr of Islam; Yasir also died from the torture; 'Ammar, under extreme torture, said words against Islam, and the Qur'an excused one forced to disbelieve while his heart remains firm in faith (Sura 16.106).
