About Cambridge IGCSE Question Simulator

A free, non-profit study aid that helps students practise past paper questions topic by topic.

What it is

We tag every question part in recent Cambridge past papers with its syllabus topic, subtopic and marks. Each topic page lists the questions year by year and groups similar questions, so students can see what is asked, how often, and practise one skill at a time.

What it is not

We do not publish complete question papers, mark schemes or examiner reports. For the papers themselves, use the official Cambridge website or your school.

This is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Cambridge University Press & Assessment.

Updates

New questions are added twice a year, after the May/June and Oct/Nov exam series. Current release: 2026-10.2 (May/June 2022 to May/June 2026).

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no cookies. The site stores nothing about you. Filters you choose stay in your own browser.

Contact

Found a wrong tag or a missing question? Email homilabs.smc@gmail.com. Rights holders can use the same address to ask for any content to be removed; we act within one working day.
