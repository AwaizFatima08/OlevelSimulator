# Cambridge O Level / IGCSE / AS & A Level past papers: where to find them online

Audience: students in Pakistan. Checked on **19 Oct 2026** with live page reads (WebFetch/WebSearch). No files were downloaded.
How sure each fact is: **[V]** = seen on the live page on 19 Oct 2026. **[K]** = from background knowledge or a search snippet, not opened on a live page. Check [K] facts before you rely on them.

## 1. How Cambridge file names work

Pattern: `<syllabus>_<session><yy>_<type>[_<paper><variant>].pdf`

| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `syllabus` | 4-digit code, e.g. `9702` |
| session letter | `s` = May/June ("summer"), `w` = Oct/Nov ("winter"), `m` = Feb/March |
| `yy` | 2-digit year: `s25` = May/June 2025, `w25` = Oct/Nov 2025, `m26` = Feb/March 2026 |
| `type` | `qp` question paper, `ms` mark scheme, `er` examiner report (one per series, no paper number), `gt` grade thresholds (one per series), `in` insert (reading texts, source booklets), `ci` confidential instructions (practical set-up, given to the school before the exam), `sf` source files for ICT/CS practicals (usually a `.zip`), `sy` syllabus, `sp`/`sm` specimen paper/mark scheme, `pm` pre-release material, `ir` instructions/reading |
| paper + variant | first digit = component (paper 1, 2, 3...), second digit = time-zone variant (1, 2, 3...). `qp_42` = Paper 4, variant 2 |

[V] Real examples seen: `9702_s25_qp_11.pdf`, `9702_s25_ms_21.pdf`, `9702_s25_gt.pdf`, `9702_w25_ci_33.pdf`, `5054_w25_qp_11.pdf`, `0500_s25_in_11.pdf`, `0500_s25_er.pdf`, `0580_s26_qp_42.pdf`.

What the variants mean for Pakistan:
- Cambridge sets papers by **administrative zone**. Pakistan is in **zone 4** [K]. Each zone sits one variant (for example x2 for one zone and x1 or x3 for another). Every variant is the same standard, so all of them are useful practice.
- **Feb/March (`m`) is a March series for India.** It covers only IGCSE and AS/A Level, and usually only one variant (x2). O Level is not offered in March [V, Cambridge/press reports]. Students in Pakistan sit **May/June and Oct/Nov**. The `m` papers are still good extra practice.
- Examiner reports (`er`) are often missing on the mirror sites, or arrive late, for recent sciences series. For example, no `9702_s24_er` or `9702_s25_er` was listed on pastpapers.co or PapaCambridge [V]. They do exist for some subjects, such as `0500_s25_er` [V].

## 4. Direct PDF URL patterns (taken from real links on the pages)

### PapaCambridge [V]
```
https://pastpapers.papacambridge.com/directories/CAIE/CAIE-pastpapers/upload/<file>.pdf
e.g. https://pastpapers.papacambridge.com/directories/CAIE/CAIE-pastpapers/upload/9702_s25_qp_11.pdf
     https://pastpapers.papacambridge.com/directories/CAIE/CAIE-pastpapers/upload/5054_w25_ms_11.pdf
```
- Flat folder: every syllabus and session sits together in one `upload/` directory.
- Listing pages: `https://pastpapers.papacambridge.com/papers/caie/<level-subject-code>-<yyyy>-<session>`. Slugs seen: `as-and-a-level-physics-9702-2025-may-june`, `o-level-physics-5054-2025-oct-nov`, `igcse-mathematics-0580-2026-may-june`, plus `-oct-nov` and `-feb-march`.
- The download button goes through `download_file.php?files=<full URL above>`.

### PastPapers.co [V]
```
https://pastpapers.co/caie/<level>/<subject-slug>-<code>/<yyyy>-<session>/<file>.pdf
e.g. https://pastpapers.co/caie/a-level/physics-9702/2025-oct-nov/9702_w25_qp_11.pdf
     https://pastpapers.co/caie/o-level/physics-5054/2025-may-june/5054_s25_qp_21.pdf
     https://pastpapers.co/caie/igcse/mathematics-0580/2025-oct-nov/0580_w25_ms_42.pdf
```
- `level` is `a-level`, `igcse` or `o-level`. `session` is `may-june`, `oct-nov` or `march`.
- The subject slug has to match the site's own spelling (e.g. `english-first-language-0500`). Open the folder page first. One guessed slug (`ict-0417`) returned 404.

### BestExamHelp [V]
```
https://bestexamhelp.com/exam/<level-slug>/<subject>-<code>/<yyyy>/<file>.pdf      (PDF)
https://bestexamhelp.com/exam/<level-slug>/<subject>-<code>/<yyyy>/<code>-s25-qp-11.php  (viewer page)
e.g. https://bestexamhelp.com/exam/cambridge-international-a-level/physics-9702/2025/9702_s25_qp_11.pdf
```
- Level slugs seen: `cambridge-international-a-level`, `cambridge-igcse`. The year folder holds all three sessions.

### Others
- PMT: `https://pmt.physicsandmathstutor.com/download/<Subject>/<Level>/Past-Papers/CIE/Paper-<n>/[International/]QP|MS/<Month> <yyyy> (v<n>) QP.pdf` [V]. This is not the Cambridge naming.
- Cambridge official: `https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/<id>-june-2024-question-paper-11.pdf` [V]. The numeric id cannot be predicted.

## 5. Rough size per syllabus per year

Taken from the file sizes PastPapers.co lists [V]:

| Syllabus / series | QP | MS | Other | Series total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9702 Physics s25 (23 QP, 23 MS, 7 CI, GT) | ≈4.5 MB | ≈6.8 MB | CI ≈15 MB, GT 0.2 MB | **≈26 MB** (≈11 MB without CI) |
| 5054 Physics s25 (8 QP, 8 MS, 2 CI, GT) | ≈2.8 MB | ≈2.0 MB | CI 3.2 MB | **≈8 MB** |
| 0580 Maths w25 (12 QP, 12 MS, GT) | ≈3.4 MB | ≈5.3 MB | GT 0.15 MB | **≈9 MB** |
| 0500 English FL s25 (6 QP, 6 IN, 6 MS, ER, GT) | ≈0.6 MB | ≈2.1 MB | IN ≈8 MB, ER 1.7 MB | **≈12.5 MB** |

Rule of thumb: about 10–25 MB per syllabus per series, so about **20–50 MB per syllabus per year** (s + w, plus a small `m` set for IGCSE/A Level). Practical sciences sit at the top of that range because of the CI files. A file is typically 100–450 KB, and CI and insert files 1–3 MB. A typical Pakistani O Level set (8 subjects × 20 years) comes to roughly **3–5 GB**.

## 7. Pearson Edexcel (International GCSE and International A Level)

- **Official:** qualifications.pearson.com has a free past-paper search for International GCSE and IAL [V]. **Papers from the most recent 12 months are locked to teachers with Edexcel Online** accounts. Older papers are open to everyone without login [V].
- **Mirrors:** Physics & Maths Tutor (organises IGCSE/IAL papers by unit, very reliable). **Papafy** (papafy.com; pastpapers.papacambridge.com/papers/edexcel redirects there [V]) lists e.g. 4MA1, 4PH1, 4CH1, 4BI1, 4EC1. Dynamic Papers ("Edexcel recent papers" section [V]). Save My Exams (freemium). exam-mate (topical, paid).
- Edexcel codes look like `4MA1` (IGCSE Maths A), `4PH1`, `WMA11` (IAL Pure 1), `WPH11`. File names vary by site. There is no single convention like Cambridge's.

## 8. Caveats and copyright

- **Copyright:** all question papers, mark schemes and examiner reports are © Cambridge University Press & Assessment (or © Pearson). The mirror sites are unofficial and carry no licence from the boards. Using them for **personal study** is common and tolerated. **Re-hosting, bundling them into an app, or selling them is not allowed** without permission. For an app, link to the source or ask Cambridge for a licence. Do not redistribute the PDFs.
- Third-party content in the papers (extracts, images) is often removed from the public copies ("copyright acknowledgement" pages).
- Domains change and get hijacked (GCE Guide is the example from Oct 2026). Do not hard-code mirror domains in software without a health check.
- Mirrors sometimes mis-file papers (PapaCambridge listed files from other syllabuses on one session page). Check that the code and session in the filename match what you wanted.
- Examiner reports for recent series are often missing on the mirrors. The full set reaches schools through the School Support Hub.
- Syllabus changes matter for practice: 4024/0580/4037/0606 papers from **before 2025** have a different calculator split and some topics that have since been removed (loci, matrices in 4024). O Level sciences papers from **before 2023** use the old paper numbering (Paper 6 → Paper 4 in 5090).
- **Oct/Nov 2026** exams are under way at the time of writing. Expect `w26` papers on the mirrors around **Jan–Feb 2027** (s26 appeared ~19 Aug 2026).
